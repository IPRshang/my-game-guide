# About

**Game Strategy Hub** is a player-built, **original** game guide site covering GTA6, Black Myth Wukong, Elden Ring, Cyberpunk 2077, and Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.

## What We Do

- Provide **structured, actionable** text-and-image guides — from beginner starts and money-making tips to boss strategies, builds, hidden secrets, and collectibles;
- Bilingual (English & Chinese) content for players worldwide;
- Continuous updates following patches and DLCs.

## Content Sources

All guides are **originally written**, based on public game mechanics and community consensus, and aim to be accurate and verifiable. Found a mistake? Correct us in the comments below any page.

## Operation & Advertising

This Site displays **Google AdSense** ads to cover hosting and maintenance costs. Ad content is auto-matched by Google and does not represent our views. See [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/).
