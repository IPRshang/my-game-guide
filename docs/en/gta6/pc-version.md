## Rockstar's "Console-First" Pattern

Looking at Rockstar's recent history, its release rhythm is typically **console first, then PC**:

- Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on consoles in 2018 and reached PC in 2019.
- GTA 5 also shipped on consoles first, with the PC version arriving considerably later.

Based on this pattern, the community broadly expects a GTA 6 PC version to be announced and released **some time after** the console launch. But it must be stressed: this is inference from past behavior. **Rockstar has never promised a PC date, nor confirmed that a PC version will exist.** Until an official announcement, PC players should stay patient.

## What PC Players Can Do Now (All Speculation)

The suggestions below are preventive prep for a PC version that *may* arrive later. None of it comes from official specs — all is speculation:

- **16GB+ RAM:** Many recent AAA titles treat 16GB as a baseline, with more headroom being safer.
- **Strong GPU:** Open-world high-fidelity rendering is demanding; plan around a mid-to-high-end discrete graphics card, not an entry-level one.
