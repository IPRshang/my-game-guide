# GTA6 — Hidden Locations & Easter Eggs

> Explore the hidden corners of Vice City

## Collectibles

### 1. 100 Alien Spaceship Parts
- Scattered across the entire map. Collect all to unlock the **Alien Rifle**
- Recommended: Helicopter low-altitude search
- Each part emits a faint green beam at night (visible only in darkness)

### 2. 50 Character Cards
- Hidden inside buildings — enter and explore to find them
- 25 collected: Unlocks **Golden Pistol Skin**
- 50 collected: Unlocks **Detective Mode** (marks nearby enemies)

### 3. 30 Stunt Jumps
- Complete all to unlock **Nitrous Boost** upgrade
- Locations:
  - Airport runway (5)
  - Mountain cliffs (8)
  - Downtown overpasses (12)
  - Dock jump ramps (5)

## Easter Eggs & Secrets

### Swampland UFO
- **Location**: Abandoned church, northernmost swamp
- **Trigger**: Thunderstorm night, 3:00 AM
- A green beam shoots into the sky. UFO hovers ~30 seconds before vanishing
- Take a photo to unlock a special camera filter

### Underwater Shipwreck
- **Location**: Southeast ocean (Coordinates X:4521 Y:3847)
- Contains 3 gold bars ($25,000 each)
- Requires diving gear

### Ghost Train
- **Location**: Abandoned railway tunnel, eastern region
- **Trigger**: Full moon night, 11:00 PM
- A translucent steam train passes through. No collision damage.
- After the train passes, pick up a **Ghost Coin** from the tracks

### Mountaintop Yeti
- **Location**: Northeast face of Mt. Chiliad summit
- **Trigger**: After completing the main story, observe with sniper rifle 8x scope
- Discovery unlocks the **Yeti Outfit**

## Hidden Missions

### "Deep Fear"
- Dive into the southwestern ocean trench
- Discover a giant squid to trigger a special mission
- **Reward**: Submarine skin + $100,000

### "The Courier's Secret"
- Find an NPC in a yellow uniform at the docklands
- Trail him to a warehouse to trigger a shootout
- **Reward**: Rare shotgun + large cash haul
