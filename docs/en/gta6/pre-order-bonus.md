# What Is the GTA 6 Pre-order Bonus? How to Pre-order?

**GTA 6 pre-orders opened worldwide on June 25, 2026. Anyone who pre-orders or buys before November 20 receives the "Vintage Vice City" pack, and the digital edition adds 1 free month of GTA+.**

Where to pre-order: PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, major retail channels, and rockstargames.com/VI.

## Pre-order Bonus Details (Vintage Vice City)

- 1955 Vapid Stanier retro sedan
- Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach
- Exclusive Jason & Lucia outfits / hairstyles
- Weapon skins in the style of the original GTA: Vice City

## Editions & Prices

- **Standard Edition** $79.99 (base game)
- **Ultimate Edition** $99.99 (exclusive vehicles, weapons, outfits, and locations like Rideout Customs)

## Digital Edition Extra

The digital pre-order adds 1 free month of GTA+ (includes GTA$500,000, Shark Card bonuses, etc.; auto-renews after the trial — you don't need to remember to cancel).

> Note: pre-order bonuses and pricing follow each store's current page; regional pricing and gifts may differ.
## FAQ

**Q: Can I still pre-order GTA 6 now?**
A: Yes. Pre-orders stay open through November 20, 2026, after which it usually becomes a regular purchase but the Vintage Vice City pre-order bonus may no longer be included.

**Q: What is GTA+, and will it auto-charge me?**
A: GTA+ is Rockstar's membership subscription; the digital pre-order includes 1 free month. It auto-renews after the trial, so cancel manually if you don't want it — there are no hidden charges beyond that.
