# GTA 6 Map Guide: Vice City & Leonida

> 📌 **The short version**: GTA 6 returns to the sun-soaked **Vice City** and, for the first time, opens up the entire **state of Leonida (based on Florida)** — expected to be the largest open world Rockstar has ever built. The gameplay reveal is the first real chance to see this map up close.

---

## 1. Map Overview: The Biggest World in Series History

From the December 2023 reveal trailer to the gameplay footage shown through late 2025, one signal is clear: **the GTA 6 map is far larger than GTA 5's**.

| Feature | GTA 5 (Los Santos) | GTA 6 (Vice City + Leonida) |
|---|---|---|
| Real-world basis | California (LA) | Florida |
| Core city | Los Santos | Vice City (Miami) |
| Terrain variety | City / desert / mountains | Metro / beaches / swamps / islands / highway towns |
| Known scale | ~49 sq mi | Rumored 2–3× the GTA 5 map |

Rockstar guards map details closely before launch, but from leaked interior/exterior scenes, trailer shots, and official copy we can confirm this is no longer a single city — it is a **complete landform system strung together by state highways**, where you can drive from a neon metropolis to wetland islands within one continuous map.

> 💡 Exact map size will be confirmed in the gameplay reveal. This page updates as official info drops.

---

## 2. Vice City: Neon and Beaches Return

Vice City is the series' signature city, based on **Miami, Florida**. It debuted in *GTA: Vice City* (2002); GTA 6 is its full remake and expansion two decades later.

From public footage, the new Vice City keeps the series' iconic elements:

- **Beaches and coastline**: golden sand, high-rise hotels, surf and yacht culture — the opening-stage centerpiece.
- **Neon nights**: Art Deco towers, pink-and-cyan neon signs, carrying the retro-futuristic "Vice" mood.
- **Wealth next to poverty**: affluent coastal communities sit sharply against inland trailer parks and rundown blocks — GTA's usual class contrast.
- **Enterable interiors**: leaked footage shows a notable jump in interior density — more shops, homes, and venues you can walk into.

---

## 3. Leonida: More Than One City

"Leonida" is the personified state name Rockstar gave Florida (same logic as Liberty City = New York, San Andreas = California, Los Santos = LA). This means GTA 6's stage is the **whole state**, not just Vice City.

Based on leaked maps and credible reports, Leonida is known to include these region types (⚠️ marks leaked/speculative names, not final official labels):

| Region (leaked) | Real-world basis | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Vice City | Miami | Metropolis |
| The Keys | Florida Keys / Key West | Island highways |
| Grassrivers ⚠️ | Everglades | Wetlands / wild |
| Port Gellhorn ⚠️ | Tampa / Jacksonville area | Port / industrial city |
| Inland highway towns | Florida small towns | Road / trailer culture |
| Resort area ⚠️ | Orlando theme parks | Tourism |

This "state-level" structure gives GTA 6 its first true **ecological and climate range**: from humid metros to bug-filled swamps to endless overseas highways — a richer exploration rhythm than any predecessor.

## 4. Real-World Reference Table

The fastest way to understand the GTA 6 map is to map it onto real Florida:

| GTA 6 name | Real-world | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vice City | Miami | South Beach, Art Deco district closely reproduced |
| Leonida | Florida | Personified state name |
| The Keys | Florida Keys | Overseas Highway-style sea bridges |
| Grassrivers | Everglades | Alligators, airboats, mangroves |
| Coastal resorts | Orlando / Tampa | Theme parks, cruise ports |

This is also GTA 6's biggest tonal shift from GTA 5 (California): **more humid, more colorful, more "Southern"** — Latin culture throughout, plus America's most extreme wealth gaps.

## FAQ

### How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has not published an exact size, but leaks and trailer analysis suggest the GTA 6 map — covering Vice City and the state of Leonida — will be roughly two to three times larger than the GTA 5 map of Los Santos. It is widely expected to be the largest open world Rockstar has ever built.

### Where is Vice City in GTA 6?

Vice City is the neon beach metropolis at the heart of GTA 6, based on Miami, Florida. It returns as the main city after first appearing in GTA: Vice City (2002), now rebuilt on a much larger scale with denser interiors and coastal suburbs.

### What is Leonida in GTA 6 based on?

Leonida is the in-game U.S. state that surrounds Vice City, modeled on the real state of Florida. The name follows Rockstar's tradition of renaming real places (Liberty City = New York, San Andreas = California). Leonida includes swamps, keys (islands), highway towns, and resort areas beyond the city itself.

### When is the GTA 6 gameplay reveal?

The first extended gameplay demonstration airs on August 27, 2026 (3 PM ET), which is 3 AM Beijing time on August 28. Rockstar streams roughly 20–30 minutes of real gameplay on Netflix first, then on its official channels. This is the best early look at the map so far.

### Will GTA 6 have a PC version at launch?

No. GTA 6 is confirmed for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at launch; a PC version has not been announced and, based on Rockstar's history, will likely arrive later. See the [release and platform guide](/en/gta6/release-guide/) for details.
