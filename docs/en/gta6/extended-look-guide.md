# How to Watch GTA 6 "An Extended Look" (Aug 27)

Rockstar has confirmed that **Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look** premieres on **Thursday, August 27, 2026**. It is the studio's next major showcase of the most anticipated game of 2026, following the first two trailers. This page helps you watch it at the right time, on the right platform, and sets realistic expectations for what it does and does not promise.

## What Exactly Is It?

Rockstar officially titles this presentation **"An Extended Look"** — not the fan-named "Trailer 3." Importantly, the studio has **not confirmed** that the presentation contains gameplay, and it has not revealed any runtime or mission details.

In other words, "An Extended Look" reads as a deeper showcase than a traditional trailer, but Rockstar has not framed it as a gameplay demo. Go in treating "will there be gameplay footage?" as an open question rather than a default assumption.

## When to Watch: Two Windows

The presentation rolls out in two stages — first on Netflix, then on YouTube — both anchored to US Eastern Time (ET):

| Stage | Time (ET) | Platform | Cost |
|-------|-----------|----------|------|
| Premiere | Aug 27, 3:00 PM ET | Netflix | Netflix subscription required |
| Public stream | Aug 27, 9:00 PM ET | Rockstar's official YouTube channel / GTA VI website | Free, no subscription |

In other regions the premiere is roughly 12:00 PM PT and 8:00 PM CEST.

The public stream goes live simultaneously on YouTube and the GTA VI website, is **completely free, and requires no subscription** — anyone can watch directly.

## China Viewing Times

For players in mainland China, the two windows map to Beijing time as follows:

- **Netflix premiere**: 3:00 PM ET Aug 27 = **03:00 Beijing time, Aug 28**.
- **YouTube public stream**: 9:00 PM ET Aug 27 = **09:00 Beijing time, Aug 28**.

If you would rather not stay up for the 3:00 AM Netflix slot, the 9:00 AM free YouTube stream the next morning is the easier option.

## What "Extended Look" Promises — and Does Not

**Confirmed:**

- Date: August 27, 2026.
- Rollout: Netflix first, then a free public stream on Rockstar's YouTube and website the same night.
- Naming: officially "An Extended Look," not "Trailer 3."

**Not confirmed — do not assume:**

- Whether it contains actual gameplay footage — not confirmed.
- Runtime, number of shots, missions, or open-world systems — none revealed.
- Any new release date or platform news — no such promise was made.

## Reasonable Expectations

Launch is less than three months away (November 19, 2026). The community broadly expects a **substantial gameplay showcase**, and the reasoning is fair: both GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 had public gameplay previews at a comparable stage before release, while GTA 6 has not yet shown one. Many players therefore see deeper gameplay footage this close to launch as a reasonable expectation.

But be clear: **expectation is not announcement.** Rockstar has only committed to the name "An Extended Look" and the air date, with no guarantee of gameplay. Treat this watch as "learn more about the world and presentation," not "confirm the release date or lock in a platform."
