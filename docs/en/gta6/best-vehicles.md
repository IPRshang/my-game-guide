# GTA 6: The Vehicle Tier List That Separates *Race* Cars From *Mission* Cars

A tier list that only ranks top speed is half a guide — in GTA 6 the fastest car is often the *worst* choice for the job. The Pegassi Tormenta tops out at 215 mph, but you don't run a 346 km/h supercar into a five-star wanted escape; you want armor and seats. The real value read is *role fit*: track domination, mission fault-tolerance, and the free car most players overlook entirely.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** players sink their first millions into a supercar, then discover it's useless for crew missions and gets blown up with only the base price insured. The free Annis Elegy RH8 (Social Club registration) rivals supercars when fully upgraded — claim it before spending a dime.

## S-Tier (Must-Own)

### 1. Pegassi Tormenta (Supercar)
- **Top Speed**: 215 mph / 346 km/h
- **Price**: $3,800,000
- **Source**: Legendary Motorsport
- **Verdict**: Fastest production car in the game. Track-oriented handling. Acceleration dominates everything else.

### 2. Grotti Furia X (Supercar)
- **Top Speed**: 208 mph / 335 km/h
- **Price**: $2,950,000
- **Verdict**: Most balanced supercar handling. Excellent drift feel — perfect for street racing.

### 3. Nagasaki Buzzard 2.0 (Helicopter)
- **Top Speed**: 180 mph
- **Price**: $2,200,000
- **Verdict**: Built-in missiles + machine gun. Mission MVP. VTOL capable, 30% more agile than previous generation.

> The Buzzard 2.0 is the only S-tier pick that earns its keep outside racing — built-in missiles and a 30% agility bump make it the mission MVP. If you can only afford one S-tier item early, the helicopter often pays back faster than a track-only supercar.

## A-Tier (Best Value)

### 4. Annis Elegy RH8 (Free!)
- **Source**: Register on Rockstar Social Club
- **Verdict**: Best free car in the game. Fully upgraded, it rivals supercars.

### 5. BF Surfer Custom (Van)
- **Price**: $45,000 + mods
- **Verdict**: Seats 6 players. Essential for online crew missions.

**Our call:** the Elegy RH8 is the single best value in the entire list — free, and competitive once upgraded. The BF Surfer Custom at $45,000+mods is the unsung hero for crew content; six seats beat any supercar when the objective needs a team.

## B-Tier (Specialized)

### 6. Maibatsu Monster (Off-Road)
- **Price**: $850,000
- **Verdict**: Unstoppable in swamps and mountains. Terrible on pavement.

### 7. Shitzu Rocket (Motorcycle)
- **Price**: $1,200,000
- **Verdict**: Best choice for alley navigation and losing wanted levels.

> B-tier isn't "bad" — it's *role-locked*. The Maibatsu Monster is useless on pavement but dominant in the swamp/mountain terrain Vice City's map is built around. Buy it for the terrain, not as a daily driver.

| Upgrade | Recommended Tier | Cost |
|---------|:---:|------|
| Engine Tune | Lv.4 | $150,000 |
| Turbo | — | $85,000 |
| Bulletproof Glass | — | $120,000 |

**Our call:** a full build-out (Engine Lv.4 + Turbo + Bulletproof Glass + Armor Lv.3) runs roughly $555,000 in mods alone — before the car. Spend by role: armor and bulletproof glass for mission vehicles, engine/turbo for racers. And remember the warning above: insurance refunds only the base price, so a destroyed fully-modded ride is a $555k lesson.




## Common Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Skipping insurance — or assuming it covers mods.** Insurance only refunds the base price; mods are lost. Fix: insure every vehicle and re-buy mods after a loss.
2. **Buying a supercar for missions.** Terrible fault tolerance under fire. Fix: use an armored SUV/van (BF Surfer Custom) early; save supercars for racing.
3. **Overlooking the free Annis Elegy RH8.** Fix: register on Rockstar Social Club and claim it before spending on anything else.
4. **Maxing every upgrade up front.** Engine Lv.4 + Turbo + Armor Lv.3 is ~$435k+ before glass. Fix: prioritize upgrades by the car's role, not uniformly.
5. **Trusting trailer/off-road specs as final.** Pre-launch numbers can shift. Fix: re-verify top speeds and handling the week of November 19, 2026.
