**Our angle:** Most "GTA 6 characters" write-ups stop at *who* Lucia and Jason are. We go further — this is a guide about *how the two-protagonist loop is meant to be played*, and where the pre-release numbers hint Rockstar is steering each character. Read it as a strategy preview, not a stat sheet.

> **Common misconception we keep seeing:** "Jason is the fighter, Lucia is the hacker, so I should just pick one and ignore the other." That misses the point. The dual setup is designed so the *pair* clears content neither could solo — and the shared-bank / per-character-cash split means neglecting one protagonist can quietly stall your economy. The guide below explains why, with the [main story walkthrough](/en/gta6/story-guide/) framing when each takes the lead.

GTA6 builds upon the multi-protagonist system introduced in GTA V, with significant upgrades. The two protagonists — **Jason** and **Lucia** — each possess unique skill trees. Understanding their differences is key to efficient gameplay.

## Protagonist Overview

| Attribute | Jason | Lucia |
|-----------|:-----:|:-----:|
| Role | Combat Specialist / Driver | Hacker / Social Infiltrator |
| Special Skill | Bullet Time (precision aiming) | Eagle Eye (enemy marking) |
| Preferred Weapons | Rifles, Shotguns, Heavy Weapons | Pistols, SMGs, Melee |
| Driving Specialty | Land vehicles (cars/motorcycles) | Aircraft (helicopters) |

## Special Skills

### Jason — Bullet Time

Activates slow-motion for precise enemy targeting.

| Level | Duration | Cooldown | Bonus Effect | Unlock |
|:-----:|:--------:|:--------:|--------------|--------|
| Lv.1 | 5s | 60s | Basic slow motion | Default |
| Lv.2 | 8s | 50s | Mark +1 target | 5 precision kills |
| Lv.3 | 12s | 40s | Headshot damage +25% | 15 precision kills |
| Lv.4 | 15s | 30s | Chain mark (up to 4 targets) | 30 precision kills |

**Combat Tips:**
- Activate when surrounded to quickly eliminate high-threat targets
- Pair with sniper rifles for long-range multi-target marking
- Lv.4 chain marking is invaluable during heist missions

**Our call:** Bullet Time is the gentler curve for new players — its Lv.1 is useful from the first firefight, whereas Eagle Eye only blooms at Lv.3+. If you're unsure where to invest first, Jason's tree rewards you fastest.

### Lucia — Eagle Eye

Highlights all enemies, interactive objects, and hidden passages in range.

| Level | Range | Duration | Bonus Effect | Unlock |
|:-----:|:-----:|:--------:|--------------|--------|
| Lv.1 | 30m | 10s | Mark enemy positions | Default |
| Lv.2 | 45m | 15s | Show alert status | 5 stealth kills |
| Lv.3 | 60m | 20s | Reveal hidden items | 15 stealth kills |
| Lv.4 | 80m | 25s | See through walls | 30 stealth kills |

**Stealth Tips:**
- Always activate before infiltration missions to plan your route
- Lv.3+ reveals hidden safes and collectibles behind walls
- Synergy: Lucia scouts → Jason breaches

**Our take:** Eagle Eye is the higher-skill-ceiling tool. Pre-release expectations suggest it pays off most in the optional stealth and collectible layers rather than the critical path — worth a dedicated Lucia build, not a forced one. ## Character Switching

### Switch Mechanics

- **Free Roam**: Switch anytime with a 5-second cooldown
- **Missions**: Automatic or manual switching depending on mission design
- **Transition**: Seamless satellite-view zoom to the other character's position

### Independent vs Shared State

| State | Per Character | Recovery |
|-------|:------------:|----------|
| Wanted Level | Independent | Lose stars or hide |
| Pocket Cash | Independent | Earn individually |
| Bank Balance | **Shared** | — |
| Properties | **Shared** | — |
| Special Skill XP | Independent | Use the skill |

> **Important**: Bank deposits and owned properties/vehicles are shared. Pocket cash is not. Always deposit large sums.

**Our read:** The shared-bank / per-character-cash split is the single most important thing to internalize early. Pre-release data implies many players will lose progress by treating pocket cash as safe — it isn't. Bank it, then switch.

## Co-op Combat Strategies

### Heist Roles

**Plan A: Jason Breach + Lucia Support**
- Jason enters front, uses Bullet Time to clear rooms
- Lucia marks reinforcements from outside, provides sniper cover
- Best for: bank heists, armored truck robberies

**Plan B: Lucia Infiltrate + Jason Extract**
- Lucia sneaks in, Eagle Eye avoids patrols
- Jason drives to extraction point once she has the goods
- Best for: jewelry stores, art theft

**Plan C: Dual Heavy Assault**
- Both activate special skills simultaneously
- Jason's Bullet Time handles armored enemies
- Lucia's Eagle Eye ensures no targets missed
- Best for: final heist stages

### Efficient XP Farming

| Target Ability | Best Character | Method |
|---------------|:-------------:|--------|
| Shooting | Jason | Range challenges + street shootouts |
| Stealth | Lucia | Night heists + assassination contracts |
| Driving | Jason | Street races + taxi missions |
| Flying | Lucia | Flight school + air freight |

## Skill Point Priority

### Early Game (first 10 hours)

**Jason**: Shooting > Stamina > Driving
- Combat is the primary income source early on

**Lucia**: Stealth > Stamina > Shooting
- Early missions favor infiltration; high stealth = clean completions

### Mid Game (10–30 hours)

**Jason**: Strength > Driving > Flying
- Heavier enemies appear; melee and vehicle chases become critical

**Lucia**: Flying > Driving > Strength
- Helicopter missions unlock; flying skill determines mission efficiency

### Late Game (30+ hours)

Both characters approach their caps. Fine-tune based on personal preference.

## Common Mistakes (Pre-Release Edition)

1. **Treating pocket cash as shared.** It's per-character; bank deposits are the only shared pool. Hoarders who never deposit will lose cash on a character they stop using.
2. **Forcing Lucia into open combat.** Her caps (Shooting 90, Strength 80) favor stealth and recon — don't build her as a Jason clone.
3. **Ignoring the 5-second free-roam switch cooldown.** During chases or alarms, spamming switch wastes the window; plan the swap.
4. **Over-leveling off-stats.** Grinding Lucia's Strength to 80 while leaving Stealth at 80 leaves her signature edge unused.
5. **Assuming these tables are final.** This is pre-release data. Re-verify every cap and unlock against real gameplay after the Nov 19, 2026 launch.
