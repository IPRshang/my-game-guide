# GTA 6 Leaks Roundup: Map, Wanted System, Honor & Fuel

Since **August 18, 2026**, a source calling itself **"CyberLeek"** has released gameplay clips, map images, and footage online. Rockstar and Take-Two issued DMCA takedowns — which the community widely reads as a tacit signal that the footage is genuine — but **Rockstar has never officially confirmed** its authenticity.

Insiders (such as NateTheHate) note that some clips are reportedly over a year old, so they may **not reflect the upcoming Netflix event or the final game**. Treat every detail below as unconfirmed development-build material that is subject to change.

> **Important disclaimer: Everything below is unconfirmed leaked material from a development build. It does not represent the final game and may be changed or removed at any time. Do not treat it as official fact.**

## The Map of Leonida

An allegedly complete map of the state of Leonida has surfaced in the leaks. Regions described in community analysis include:

- **Vice City**: the urban core, reportedly much larger than GTA V's Los Santos.
- **Vice-Dale County**: suburban areas.
- **Kelly County**: Everglades-style wetlands with alligators.
- **Coastline and beaches**.
- Other named areas such as Mount Kalaga National Park, Port Gellhorn, and Ambrosia.

The exact layout and size are **unconfirmed**. Community estimates put the map at roughly **125 km²** (about double GTA V) — treat this as an estimate only. For a more structured tracking of map information, see the [GTA 6 map guide](/en/gta6/map-guide).

## Wanted System

The leaks suggest the **6-star Wanted Level** returns, upgraded from GTA V's 5-star scale:

- **Stars 1–2**: standard police patrol.
- **Stars 3–4**: SWAT teams plus police helicopters.
- **Stars 5–6**: National Guard with military vehicles.

Allegedly, **each level triggers real-time in-game news broadcasts**. As with everything here, these details come from unconfirmed material and the actual mechanics may differ.

## Honor / Karma Moral System

The leaks point to a new **Honor / Karma** moral system similar to Red Dead Redemption 2: how you treat NPCs — helping versus harming — affects how the world responds to you.

- **High honor**: shop discounts and tips about hidden locations.
- **Low honor**: more police attention and higher prices.

Whether this system survives to the final release remains unconfirmed.

## Vehicles & Fuel

The leaks indicate cars and motorcycles **need fuel**, with gas stations spread across Leonida; crashes can cause engine fires and flat tires that require a mechanic. Character stats also build over time — for example, playing basketball raises a "Focus" / stamina stat, while the gym raises strength.

Note that the specific rules for fuel, repairs, and stat growth all come from unconfirmed development-build material.

## Combat & World Systems

On combat, the leaks describe more detailed melee (knives, wrenches, tasers), "Greet" and "Taunt" NPC interactions, a separate item wheel, quicker looting, and a healing item called **Zombix** whose effectiveness decreases on repeated use. In at least one clip, the HUD shows **two separate cash figures**.

For mini-games and world content: a basketball mini-game tied to a focus meter; fishing and hunting spots for specific species like in RDR2; reportedly the radio lineup includes V-Rock, Symphony FM, Stockyard FM, Backcountry Radio, Emotion 98.3, Cocoteo FM, Worldwide FM, Kaleidoscope FM, The Ola, The Chamber, Circoloco Records, Radio On.U, plus a talk show called "The Wipe".

On protagonists, the leaks confirm dual leads **Jason Duval and Lucia** — Lucia being the first playable female lead in a mainline modern GTA — and the story opens with Lucia leaving Leonida State Penitentiary. All of this is unconfirmed leaked information.
