# GTA 6 Online: The Money Guide That Explains *Which* Loop to Run and When

Most "get rich" GTA write-ups are just numbered lists of activities. That framing fails in GTA 6 Online because the real skill isn't *knowing* the loops — it's *sequencing* them. Early on you have no warehouse, no nightclub, no seed capital, so the loops that look sexy (smuggling, heists) are actually traps until you've banked a baseline. This guide is ordered the way we'd actually play it: zero-to-hero, cheapest loop first.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** players blow their first few million on a smuggling warehouse or a supercar, then have no working capital to actually run the loop. The warehouse is a *multiplier*, not a starter — fund it only after the boring-but-reliable store, taxi, and stock flips have built a cushion.

## 1. Convenience Store Robbery Loop
- **Best Location**: Vice Beach area
- **Payout**: $800-1,200 per store
- **Tip**: Enter through the back door to avoid triggering alarms immediately

## 2. Taxi Missions
Unlocked after receiving Franklin's call. Each fare nets $200-500. Complete 50 cumulative rides for a $50,000 bonus.

## 3. Hidden Loot Crates

| Area | Location | Respawn |
|------|----------|---------|
| Docklands | Top of red shipping containers | 2 hrs (real time) |
| Swamplands | Bushes south of abandoned cabin | 4 hrs (real time) |

> Crates are the most underrated early loop because they're free and on a timer. The trick is *rotation*, not camping: the Docklands (2 hr) and Swamplands (4 hr) respawn timers mean you should be running other loops between pickups, not staring at a container.

## 4. Stock Market Manipulation

### LCN Exchange (Story Mode)
- **Strategy**: Invest before assassination missions, sell after
- **Best Stocks**: LifeInvader (after bombing mission), Redwood (after competitor hit)
- **ROI**: 80-200% per event

### BAWSAQ (Online)
- **Strategy**: Track community events and weekly bonuses
- **Tip**: Never invest more than 30% of your cash in a single stock

> The stock market is the highest-ROI loop in the game — but only if you time it around story events. Treat BAWSAQ as a community-sentiment play driven by other players' actions, not a guaranteed flip like the LCN assassination setup.

## 5. Heists

| Heist | Setup Cost | Potential Take | Difficulty |
|-------|-----------|----------------|------------|
| Fleeca Bank | $11,500 | $57,500-143,750 | ★☆☆☆☆ |
| Pacific Standard | $100,000 | $1,250,000 | ★★★★☆ |
| Leonida Diamond | $250,000 | $4,500,000+ | ★★★★★ |

**Our call:** for a solo player, Fleeca Bank is the only realistic early heist — the $11,500 setup is trivial and the rating is one star. Pacific Standard and Leonida Diamond need a coordinated crew, so treat them as mid/late-game goals. Don't burn a lobby trying to solo a five-star job.

## 6. Smuggling Operations
- **Buy**: Smuggler's warehouse (starting at $200,000)
- **Profit**: Source goods for $2,000-8,000, sell for $10,000-50,000 per crate
- **Tip**: Buy in full lobbies for bonus payout multiplier — but risk is higher

## 7. Nightclub Income
- **Passive income**: Up to $50,000 per in-game day with full popularity
- **Warehouse**: Accumulates goods from linked businesses automatically
- **Tip**: Keep popularity above 90% by doing promotion missions every 2 in-game days

## 8. Casino High Roller
- **High limit tables**: Minimum bet $50,000
- **Best game**: Three Card Poker (lowest house edge at 3.37%)
- **Strategy**: Count cards in Blackjack (works offline only)
- **Warning**: Gambling can drain your entire bankroll in minutes

**Our call:** the casino is a money *sink*, not a source — even Three Card Poker carries a 3.37% house edge, so over enough hands you lose. Use it as a one-time novelty, never as a grind, and never walk in with more than you can afford to lose.

> ⚠️ **Important**: Never carry all your cash. Deposit to the Maze Bank app regularly.


## Common Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Buying the smuggling warehouse too early.** At a $200,000 entry plus sourcing costs, it drains the seed capital you need for everything else. Fix: build a cushion via stores, taxi, and LCN flips first, then buy the warehouse as a multiplier.
2. **Treating the casino as a grind.** The 3.37% house edge means long-term losses. Fix: treat it as a novelty, cap your bankroll, walk away.
3. **Over-concentrating BAWSAQ.** Risk is real if one stock moves against you. Fix: never put more than 30% of your cash in a single stock, and track community events.
4. **Camping crate spawns.** The 2–4 hr real-time timers punish waiting. Fix: rotate Docklands/Swamplands pickups between other loops.
5. **Carrying unbanked cash.** Random events and NPC losses can wipe it. Fix: deposit to the Maze Bank app regularly.
