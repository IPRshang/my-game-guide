## 1. Convenience Store Robbery Loop
- **Best Location**: Vice Beach area
- **Payout**: $800-1,200 per store
- **Tip**: Enter through the back door to avoid triggering alarms immediately

## 2. Taxi Missions
Unlocked after receiving Franklin's call. Each fare nets $200-500. Complete 50 cumulative rides for a $50,000 bonus.

## 3. Hidden Loot Crates

| Area | Location | Respawn |
|------|----------|---------|
| Docklands | Top of red shipping containers | 2 hrs (real time) |
| Swamplands | Bushes south of abandoned cabin | 4 hrs (real time) |

## 4. Stock Market Manipulation

### LCN Exchange (Story Mode)
- **Strategy**: Invest before assassination missions, sell after
- **Best Stocks**: LifeInvader (after bombing mission), Redwood (after competitor hit)
- **ROI**: 80-200% per event

### BAWSAQ (Online)
- **Strategy**: Track community events and weekly bonuses
- **Tip**: Never invest more than 30% of your cash in a single stock

## 6. Smuggling Operations
- **Buy**: Smuggler's warehouse (starting at $200,000)
- **Profit**: Source goods for $2,000-8,000, sell for $10,000-50,000 per crate
- **Tip**: Buy in full lobbies for bonus payout multiplier — but risk is higher

## 7. Nightclub Income
- **Passive income**: Up to $50,000 per in-game day with full popularity
- **Warehouse**: Accumulates goods from linked businesses automatically
- **Tip**: Keep popularity above 90% by doing promotion missions every 2 in-game days

## 8. Casino High Roller
- **High limit tables**: Minimum bet $50,000
- **Best game**: Three Card Poker (lowest house edge at 3.37%)
- **Strategy**: Count cards in Blackjack (works offline only)
- **Warning**: Gambling can drain your entire bankroll in minutes

> ⚠️ **Important**: Never carry all your cash. Deposit to the Maze Bank app regularly.
