## 1. Understand the Dual Protagonists First

GTA 6 uses dual protagonists (Lucia & Jason) you can switch between during missions and free roam (source: official second trailer).

- **Treat them as a two-person squad**: one handles stealth / backup / hacking, the other drives / provides firepower.
- **Pre-stage two vehicles' trunk weapons** before missions to avoid scrambling mid-fight.

*Source: official showcase + GamesRadar / Polygon analysis.*

---

## 2. Wanted Level & Police (zero-spoiler hints)

Unlike before, the police are "smarter": they escalate the chase based on your **location, time of day, and whether witnesses remain**, and deploy roadblocks, encirclements, drones, etc. (source: official showcase footage and media — **exact mechanics follow the in-game experience**).

- Leave camera and witness sightlines quickly after a crime.
- Pre-position a "spare car" a few blocks away to swap and shake pursuit.
- Near water, pre-stage a boat as an escape route.

---

## 3. Early Money & Business

- Early on, prioritize **low-risk errand / delivery** missions to build startup cash.
- Watch for **legit front businesses** like car washes (laundering icon seen in leaked footage) — steadier long-term cash flow (source: community analysis of leaked footage, pending official confirmation).

---

## 4. Combat & Vehicles

- Gunplay feels tighter this time; melee adds **stamina and dodge** (source: official showcase).
- Vehicles handle differently across surfaces / weather, giving driving more "weight."

> **Key tip**: don't carry every weapon on your person — equipment management is more realistic here; rely on **trunk and vehicle caching**, and swap cars after a fight instead of tanking it.

| Myth | Truth |
