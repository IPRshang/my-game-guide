## 🔍 Collectible Achievements (12)

### 🎨 Street Art (50)
- **Achievement**: "Street Artist"
- **Locations**: Vice City downtown 30 + Swamps 10 + Countryside 10
- **Tip**: Use spray can, marked as purple spray icon on map
- **Reward**: Exclusive spray skins + Street Cred

### 🃏 Playing Cards (100)
- **Achievement**: "Poker Master"
- **Locations**: Bars, casinos, hotels across the map
- **Tip**: Every 10 cards unlock a new deck skin
- **Reward**: Exclusive card decks

### 🎯 Action Figures (30)
- **Achievement**: "Collector"
- **Locations**: Mostly high places / hidden spots
- **Tip**: Need to shoot or jump to reach
- **Reward**: Special emotes

### 🚗 Vehicle List (40)
- **Achievement**: "Car Collector"
- **Requirement**: Store 40 different vehicles in garages
- **Tips**:
  - Rare vehicles spawn at specific times/locations
  - Import/Export missions unlock special vehicles
  - Racing rewards unlock exclusive cars
