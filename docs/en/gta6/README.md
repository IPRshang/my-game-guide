## I. Game Background

Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar's first mainline entry in the series since GTA V (2013) — over a decade later — and the first to center on **modern Miami (Vice City) and the fictional state of "Leonida"**.

The two officially confirmed protagonists are **Lucia** and **Jason** — the series' first dual-protagonist setup. Trailer 2 (released May 6, 2025) shows a sun-soaked open world blending beaches, neon nightlife, a crowded metropolis, and wilderness, hinting that the pair get pulled into a major cross-state criminal conspiracy after a job goes wrong.

> Note: The above is all publicly revealed info from official trailers — **no story spoilers**. The actual plot and character arcs will be revealed at launch.

## II. Core Mechanics (based on trailers & official info)

GTA VI's full systems are not yet fully public. Confirmed directions so far:

- **Open world returns to Vice City**: the largest map in series history, spanning city, beaches, swamps, and small towns.
- **Dual-protagonist system**: Lucia and Jason can be switched between or alternated; exact switching mechanics TBD at launch.
- **Evolved RAGE engine**: significantly improved visuals and physics — Rockstar calls it "the biggest, most immersive evolution in the series."
- **Online mode (GTA Online)**: series tradition; a companion online service is expected after the single-player launch, but timing and form are unannounced.
- **Lifelike living systems**: trailers show greatly increased NPC density, environmental interaction, and richer vehicle / daily activities.

## IV. Advanced Tips & Strategy (for series veterans)

Efficiency advice for series players (based on GTA's consistent design, expected to apply here):

- **Push the main story to unlock systems**: GTA usually gates core systems (phone, properties, vehicle upgrades) behind early story beats — do a few chapters before free exploration for best efficiency.
- **Use the economy loop**: the series tradition of "buy property / run missions" is a snowball money-maker — don't blow your starting cash recklessly.

## V. FAQ

**Q1: When exactly does GTA VI release?**
A: Officially **November 19, 2026**. It was previously delayed from Fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then again to Nov 19. Any further change will follow Rockstar's announcements.

**Q2: Will it come to PC?**
A: **No PC version announced yet.** Rockstar has historically launched on consoles first, PC later (see GTA V, RDR2); industry expects a PC version possibly in 2027, but nothing is confirmed.

**Q3: Can PS4 / Xbox One play it?**
