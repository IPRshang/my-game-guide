# Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom Full-Collection Deep Guide

TOTK "full collection" is a marathon across **surface, sky, and depths** — 152 shrines, 900 Korok seeds, 27 Zonai devices, each tied to real power gains (stamina, vessels, weapon/shield/bow capacity). This guide reorganizes the evergreen content into a "follow-and-grind" route; all data follows community-accepted facts.

> Note: zero spoilers — only mechanics and collection ideas, no main-area names revealed.

## 1. 152 Shrines: Distribution & Puzzle Ideas

Total **152 shrines = 120 Surface + 32 Sky**. **4 are on the starting sky island** (mandatory tutorial; can't leave without them).

|------|------|------|

| In-isa | West | Fuse |

### 1.3 Shrine Types & Ideas

- **Puzzle**: use the four abilities (Ultrahand, Fuse, Ascend, Recall); when stuck, think "can I build a vehicle around it?"
- **Rauru's Blessing**: walk in for the reward, one chest inside; common after external challenges or crystal deliveries.
- **Proving Grounds ⭐**: all gear taken, given stick/bow/shield + 10 arrows; clear enemies to return.

**General idea**: Ascend through ceilings, Recall "ride" falling rocks up, Fuse premium materials onto base weapons for damage, Ultrahand-build vehicles to move crystals. **32 sky shrines usually need vehicles** — do them after stamina is sufficient.

> Each shrine gives 1 Light of Blessing + 1 chest; **4 Lights = 1 Heart or Stamina vessel (1/5 ring)**. Pulling the Master Sword needs **2 stamina rings (~20 shrines)** — clear Central Hyrule's 35 first (most, moderate difficulty).

## 2. 900 Korok Seeds: Distribution & Capacity

Korok Seeds expand **weapon / bow / shield pouch** capacity — the real fix for "weapons keep breaking."

### 2.1 By Region

| Region | Count | Share | Difficulty |
|------|:----:|:----:|:--------:|
| Surface | **640** | 71% | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Sky | **160** | 18% | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| Depths | **100** | 11% | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| **Total** | **900** | 100% | |

### 2.2 Capacity Needs (key data)

| Pouch | Start → Full | Seeds needed |
|------|-----------|:--------:|
| Weapon | 8 → **20** | **441** |
| Bow | 5 → 14 | 232 |
| Shield | 4 → 16 | 291 |
| **All full** | — | **964** (only 900 exist) |

> 900 seeds **can't** fill all three. Priority: **Weapon (441) > Shield (291) > Bow (232)** — weapons break most.

### 2.3 Puzzle Types (quick)

Stone piles (fill the missing one), flowers (step in order), acorns (shoot tree hollow), metal balls (Ultrahand into pit), stakes (move to position), candles (light all), windmills (hit all targets before stop), floating blocks (Ultrahand shape), leaves/apples (burn / orient), Zonai devices (new), cave entrances (new), sky/depths glow (new).

**Tip**: clear region by region; after activating a tower, collect nearby Koroks; surface is densest (Central Hyrule 80, forest 70, Faron 70) — do surface first, then sky/depths.

### 3.1 Core Mechanics

- **Construct Head**: makes connected weapon devices auto-target nearest enemy — the brain of auto-combat machines.
- **Fan**: low cost, medium thrust, universal power source.
- **Rocket**: one-time huge thrust (~3s); rocket-shield = launch straight up (rocket-shield jump).
- **Control Stick**: vehicle driving core; place at center of mass to avoid yaw.

### 3.3 Battery Upgrade Route

Battery from **1 cell (3 small)** to **max 16 cells (48 small)**, ~**4500 crystallized charges**, 100 per cell.

| Stage | Battery | Source |
|------|:----:|------|
| Early 1–4 | depths mining + Zonai forge (3 ore → 1 charge) |
| Mid 4–8 | defeat depths bosses (20 charges each) |
| Late 8–16 | sweep depths mining caves |

Max 16 cells lets the Hover Bike **fly 10+ min non-stop**. **Autobuild** unlocks in the depths "Central Great Abandoned Mine" after the starting island — save classic designs for one-click rebuild.

## 4. Three-Layer World Route

| Layer | Key collection | Survival |
|------|----------|----------|
| Surface | 120 shrines + 640 Koroks + towers | clear alongside main story |
| Sky | 32 shrines + 160 Koroks | needs vehicle building, late game |
| Depths | 100 Koroks + 120 Lightroots + Zonaite | gloom drains hearts; light flowers / Lightroots |

**Depths notes**: surface "Chasm" drops straight to depths; open glider to control landing away from gloom pools; stepping gloom **permanently lowers heart cap** — only **Sundelion** meals or standing in an activated **Lightroot halo** recover it. 120 Depths Lightroots **mirror** 120 surface shrines (names reversed) — light them en route for fast travel + heal. Depths are the main source of battery crystallized charges and strong gear (Bargainer statues trade for miner gear).

### Recommended Order

1. **Starting island**: 4 mandatory shrines + glider + learn 4 abilities.
2. **Central Hyrule**: 35 shrines first, build stamina for Master Sword; collect surface Koroks.
3. **Expand out**: Hebra → Lanayru → Necluda → Gerudo → Eldin → Akkala → Faron.
4. **Depths sweep**: mine battery, light Lightroots, collect 100 Koroks.
5. **Sky finish**: 32 shrines via Hover Bike / aircraft.

> Make the "Hover Bike" blueprint first — daily traversal efficiency doubles; most puzzles can be bypassed without brute force.

## FAQ

**Q1: Must I do all 152 shrines?**
A: Not forced. But 4 Lights = 1 vessel; full collection maxes stamina/hearts; Master Sword needs ~20 (2 stamina rings).

**Q2: Can 900 Koroks fill all three pouches?**
A: No. Full needs 964; only 900 exist. Priority Weapon (441) > Shield (291) > Bow (232).

**Q3: Where to get the 27 Zonai devices?**
A: Mainly Zonai device dispensers; densest on sky islands and depths; battery charges via depths mining.

**Q4: Depths too dark and draining hearts?**
A: use Brightbloom seeds (bind to arrow, shoot) for long light; gloom damage heals via Sundelion meals or Lightroot halo; normal food doesn't help.

**Q5: Can't find sky shrine entrances?**
A: light the regional tower first; map shows sky archipelagos; 32 need vehicles — do after stamina is enough.

**Q6: Does TOTK have DLC?**
A: No paid DLC (per public reports / third-party estimates; only free fix patches, latest Ver. 1.4.3, 2026-02).
