# How to Beat Malenia in Elden Ring (Waterfowl Dance · Bleed · Full Boss Table)

Elden Ring (Metacritic **96**, 2022 GOTY) is the most popular Souls-like open world ever, and **Malenia, Goddess of Rot** is widely regarded as its hardest boss — she has killed Tarnished over **300 million times** by official count. This guide revives the high-value strategies: master "how to beat Malenia / Waterfowl Dance," plus a full boss table, DLC notes, and a Switch 2 preview.

## 1. Prep Before the Fight

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|------|
| Level | **120–130** (vigor 50+ safer) |
| Build | **Bleed** (fast health drain, fewer of her moves) |
| Spirit | **Mimic Tear** — most stable, splits Waterfowl targeting |
| Talismans | Dragoncrest Greatshield, Great-Jar's Arsenal, Lord of Blood's Exultation |
| Key items | Purifying Crystal Tear (Scarlet Rot), Freezing Pot (interrupt Waterfowl) |

> **Lethal mechanic**: Malenia **heals** on any hit to you (including blocking) and even when your summon is hit. So **avoid Black Knife Tiche** — constant hits feed her healing.

## 2. How to Dodge Waterfowl Dance (3 Phases)

Her signature move, three phases; the safest responses (practice = no damage):

1. **Startup · leap**: the instant she jumps, **run outward** to create distance.
2. **Phase 1 · sword fall**: when she lands, **roll outward** to dodge the first wave.
3. **Phase 2 · lunge**: as she thrusts forward, **roll forward through her body** to her back.
4. **Phase 3 · finish**: her final forward dash — roll backward out of range.

> Tip: throw a Freezing Pot during her Waterfowl **windup** for frostbite stagger; or use Frost Stomp / ranged spells to pressure her early into phase 2 (fewer Waterfowl triggers).

## 3. Bleed Build (Diminishing Returns)

| Slot | Recommendation |
|------|------|
| Weapons | **Twinblade "Sepuku" + Bloodhound's Fang** style · White Mask + Lord of Blood's Exultation |
| Alternatives | Rivers of Blood, Eleonora's Poleblade |
| Stats | Vigor 50 / Arcane 60 / Dex 40 |
| Spirit | Mimic Tear (your build syncs to it — wear the full set) |
| Talismans | Lord of Blood's Exultation + Alexander's Shard + Rotten Winged Sword Insignia |

Bleed stacks fill, her bar evaporates, and she gets the fewest moves possible — the fastest clear.

## 4. Phase 2: Scarlet Rot

On phase transition she bursts a **Scarlet Rot AOE** — eat a Purifying Tear or back off. Phase 2 is denser but has longer windups; with Mimic Tear holding aggro, go aggressive. Her phantom-lance thrust: keep moving, roll at the end.

> One line: Malenia is a **memory boss** — learn Waterfowl timing + bleed pressure = stable win.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Waterfowl Dance really be dodged without damage?**
A: Yes. Mantra: "leap→run out → roll out → roll through → roll back." A few memory runs = stable no-damage.

**Q: Mimic Tear or Black Knife Tiche for Malenia?**
A: **Mimic Tear**. Tiche gets hit continuously, feeding her healing and slowing your damage race.

**Q: Must bleed use Twinblade?**
A: No. Rivers of Blood / Eleonora's Poleblade work; core is "high bleed accumulation + Lord of Blood's Exultation."

**Q: Can't enter the DLC?**
A: Beat Mohg and Radahn first; after entering, grab 10–12 Scadutree Fragments before pushing.

**Q: Does Switch 2 save/content cross with old versions?**
A: Tarnished Edition includes base+DLC+new classes/armor/horse skin; old players buy Tarnished Pack (~$4.99) for new content. Cross-save follows Nintendo / Bandai Namco announcements.

**Q: Main story first or Malenia directly?**
A: Malenia is hidden (Haligtree), recommended level 120–130; clear the main story and out-level her first.
