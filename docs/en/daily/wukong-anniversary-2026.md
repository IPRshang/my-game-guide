## 5. 81 Bosses: Mechanics First

Base game has **81 bosses: 36 main + 45 elite**, across 6 chapters. One rule: **don't get greedy, learn "see-through" (perfect dodge/parry).**

- **Universal opener**: Immobilize → heavy combo works on most bosses; keep distance when they wind up big moves.
- **Early practice**: Gu Guardian (first forced boss, unlocks Immobilize), Guangzhi (first optional yaoguai, drops "Red Tide" transformation).
- **Hard samples**: Yellow Wind Sage (dodge sandstorm); **Yellow Brow (hidden)** has a 3s cast instakill — interrupt or leave range; **Great Sage (final)** 3 phases, save transforms/Immobilize for P3 burst.
- **True ending**: all **5 secret areas** + Chapter 3 treasure-hunter quest, then Erlang.

> Can't win? Go farm gear and pills first — stats are also strength.
