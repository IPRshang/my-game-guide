## 2. How Does It Relate to Wukong?

The most-asked question. Based on the announcement and industry norms, three judgments:

1. **Same brand, almost certainly not a direct sequel**: "Black Myth" looks more like a **shared-mythology anthology** — each entry focuses on a different deity / legend, not a linear continuation. Zhong Kui and Wukong belong to different mythological lineages.
2. **Tech lineage almost certainly continues**: Wukong ran on **UE5 + in-house motion-capture / scanned-asset pipelines**; as a same-studio follow-up, Zhong Kui is all but guaranteed to reuse that industrial pipeline.
3. **Shared universe?**: Not stated. Conservative take: **standalone story, shared "Black Myth" art and production values**, not a direct continuation of Wukong's ending.
