# Elden Ring: Nightreign Beginner Survival Guide

**Elden Ring: Nightreign** is FromSoftware's spin on the Elden Ring combat feel, dropped into a **3-player co-op roguelike** frame. Faster pace, lower tolerance, "timed expedition + random build." This is a survival manual for total newcomers: first understand what it is **not**, then decide how to play.

> If you'd rather tackle the soulborne mainline, the same boss-logic thinking applies to [Elden Ring: Malenia (Switch 2 remaster)](/en/daily/elden-malenia-switch2).

---

## 1. What It Actually Is (Clear the Misconceptions)

| Misconception | Truth |
|---------------|-------|
| Is it Elden Ring 2? | No — a **standalone spin-off**, shared art and some enemies, but map / systems rebuilt |
| Can I solo-explore at my own pace? | Designed for **3-player co-op**; solo is possible but brutally hard, strongly group up |
| Open-world free roam? | There's a "big map" but each run is a **timed expedition** with a shrinking poison circle |
| Free character build? | No — each "Nightfarer" is a fixed class, powered by random **relics** |

## 3. Expedition Structure (Day 1 → Nightlord)

1. **Day 1**: land, loot weapons / levels, clear mini-bosses for resources, return inside the circle before nightfall;
2. **Day 2**: circle shrinks, prioritize forts / churches for permanent buffs, face the **Nightlord** by dusk;
3. **Shrinking mechanic**: out-of-circle drains HP fast — don't greed distant chests; surviving beats looting.

---

## 4. Seven Beginner Survival Tips

1. **Run with a full 3-stack**: solo difficulty spikes hard; matchmaking works but comms are poor;
2. **Don't hoard weapons**: builds shift per run, use what fits now, swap for stronger late;
3. **Prioritize level & HP**: early levels > flashy weapons; more HP = more tolerance;
4. **Learn one Nightlord's pattern**: each has fixed phase skills; die twice and memorize;
5. **Use relics to cover weaknesses**: melee short on FP? equip FP relics, don't force your weak side;
6. **Mark & stay together**: map pings to rally point; lone wolves die;
7. **Don't overstay the circle**: out-of-circle damage is brutal — pathing beats clearing.

---

## 5. Relics System Primer

- Relics are **run-external / in-run** stacking buffs granting passives (stats, effects, starting gear);
- Same-color slots map to different bonus axes; prioritize a **class-main-stat** set bonus;
- Newcomers: skip perfect combos, just equip "HP up / attack up" utility pieces.

## FAQ

**Q1: Does Nightreign require buying Elden Ring base game?**
A: No — it's a **standalone title**, purchased separately, no dependency on base-game saves.

**Q2: Must it be three players?**
A: Designed for 3-player co-op, but supports solo / duo / online matchmaking. Solo is playable yet notably harder; newcomers should group up.

**Q3: Which character is safest for beginners?**
A: **Wylder** has the highest tolerance and most intuitive kit — the newcomer default; pick Guardian to play support tank.

**Q4: How long is one run?**
A: A single expedition is typically **20–40 minutes** (Day 1 / Day 2 + Nightlord); failure restarts fast, much quicker than the base game.

**Q5: Compared to Elden Ring, is the skill floor high?**
A: Combat feel is shared, but the roguelike timer + shrinking circle add pressure; once you know soul-style dodge / parry you pick it up fast — the real hurdle is **team rhythm**, not solo mechanics.

**Q6: Do I lose relics on death?**
A: Relics earned in-run settle into your stash at expedition end and carry across runs; failure still keeps obtained relics — progress isn't fully wiped.
