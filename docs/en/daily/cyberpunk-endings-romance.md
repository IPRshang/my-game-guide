# Cyberpunk 2077 All Endings & Romance — Deep Guide

Unlike the crime epic of [GTA 6](/en/gta6/) or the urban open world of [Ananta](/en/ananta/), Cyberpunk 2077's most-searched, evergreen core is its **branching ending system** and **romanceable companions**. Based on the 2.0 overhaul and the Phantom Liberty DLC's real mechanics, this guide covers **the 5+1 ending triggers, the hidden solo-raid ending, and the 4 romance routes' gender gates and irreversible key choices** — so you can act straight from the table, whether starting fresh or going for platinum.

---

## 1. Ending Overview Table

The choice before the final mission "Nocturne OP.55 N.1" decides V and Johnny Silverhand's fate. There are **6 regular endings (5 base + 1 DLC)** plus **1 hidden ending**.

| # | Ending | Core trigger | Difficulty |
|---|--------|--------------|:--:|
| 1 | The Devil (Arasaka) | Trust Arasaka, let Hanako help | ★★ |
| 2 | The Sun (Rogue) | Take Rogue with you to storm the tower | ★★★ |
| 3 | The Star (Panam) ⭐ | Call Panam / Aldecaldos | ★★ |
| 4 | Temperance (Johnny) | Let Johnny take the body | ★★★ |
| 5 | Suicide (The Edge) | Give up on the roof | — |
| 6 | The Tower (Phantom Liberty) | Side with Reed, accept NUSA surgery | ★★★★ |

> The higher the difficulty, the stricter the prerequisite sidequests or combat gates. **The Star** is community-recommended as "the most worth experiencing" for its warm, complete ending.

## 2. The 5+1 Endings, Triggers One by One

### 1. The Devil (Arasaka)
- **Trigger**: final choice "Trust Arasaka, let Hanako help."
- **Flow**: enter Arasaka Tower with Hanako → beat Adam Smasher → accept the Soulkiller procedure.
- **Result**: V's consciousness is preserved, but the body must wait for Arasaka to find a host; Hanako takes over Arasaka. Most bittersweet — V becomes the company's "guinea pig."

### 2. The Sun (Rogue)
- **Trigger**: choose "Take Rogue with you" (complete Rogue's questline first).
- **Result**: V becomes a Night City legend, owns the Afterlife; but only ~**6 months** to live. Last job: a space heist on the Crystal Palace casino. Most "V" ending — the legend never ends.

### 3. The Star (Panam) ⭐ Recommended
- **Trigger**: choose "Call Panam for help" (complete Panam's questline and romance; see below).
- **Result**: V leaves Night City with Panam and the Aldecaldos, crossing the Badlands for a possible cure — open-ended and hopeful. Warmest ending; V is finally not alone.

### 4. Temperance (Johnny)
- **Trigger**: choose "Take Johnny with Rogue," then in cyberspace pick "Johnny stays."
- **Result**: Johnny takes V's body; V's consciousness stays in cyberspace forever. Johnny buys a guitar and leaves Night City. Most bittersweet-by-design.

### 5. Suicide (The Edge)
- **Trigger**: on the rooftop, choose "This is where it ends."
- **Result**: not recommended; Johnny's reaction is heartbreaking. Tied to the "Bad Ending" achievement.

### 6. The Tower (Phantom Liberty)
- **Trigger**: in the DLC main story, **side with Solomon Reed** and at the climax **accept NUSA's surgery** to remove the Relic.
- **Result**: the chip is removed, V survives — but **comatose for 2 years**; on waking, all friends are gone, V is "ordinary" again and **can never use cyberware**. Survived, but lost everything.

## 3. The Hidden Ending (Solo Tower Raid)

Beyond the 6 regular endings, there's the **hidden ending where V storms Arasaka Tower alone**, tied to the "Hidden Ending" achievement. All must hold:

1. **Johnny Silverhand affinity ≥ 70%** (high sync rate);
2. **Complete all Johnny sidequests**;
3. In "Nocturne OP.55 N.1," choose "**Think of another way**";
4. Then **wait ~5 minutes without acting** — Johnny offers the "one-on-one" plan with a unique option.

> This ending is brutally hard but narratively striking; a must for speedrunners and completionists. Manual-save before the mission to revisit other endings.

## 4. All Romance Routes (Gender Gate + Key Choices)

There are **4 romanceable companions**; the gender gate is strict and **irreversible** once set — choose carefully.

### Romance Quick Table

| Character | Gender requirement | Quests | Utility |
|-----------|--------------------|:------:|:------:|
| Panam Palmer | Male V (male body + any voice) | 6 | ★★★★★ |
| Judy Alvarez | Female V (female body + female voice) | 10 | ★★★ |
| Kerry Eurodyne | Male V (male body + male voice) | 7 | ★★★ |
| River Ward | Female V (female body + any voice) | 3 | ★★ |

> **Rule**: Panam & Kerry need **male V**; Judy & River need **female V** — determined by "body + voice" together.

### Panam Palmer (Male V only) ⭐ Highest utility
- **Questline**: Ghost Town → Lightning Breaks → Life During Wartime → Riders on the Storm → With a Little Help from My Friends → Queen of the Highway.
- **Key choices**: back her against Nash in the Ghost Town song; show care at the bar talk; **touch her thigh on the quad (key!)**; don't refuse her touch in the final mission.
- **Romance triggers**: after "Queen of the Highway," at the campfire.
- The **only partner who joins the final fight** — great for stealth or assault.

### Judy Alvarez (Female V only)
- **Questline**: Automatic Love → The Space in Between → Disasterpiece → Double Life → Both Sides, Now → Ex-Factor → Talkin' 'bout a Revolution → Pisces → Pyramid Song (10 total).
- **The trap (Pisces)**:

| Your choice | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| Refuse Maiko's plan, fight beside Judy | ✅ romance continues |
| Accept the plan but **refuse** her money | ✅ romance continues |
| Accept the plan **and** her money | ❌ romance **permanently ends** |

- **Pyramid Song key**: dive with Judy, spend the night in the cabin; **next morning at the dock choose "this is a good start"** to confirm. Romance triggers at the dock, not during Pisces — but Pisces decides whether you reach Pyramid Song.

### River Ward (Female V only)
- **Questline (shortest, 3)**: I Fought the Law → The Hunt → Following the River.
- **Key choices**: back River in The Hunt; show care at the family dinner; accept him on the rooftop where he confesses.

### Kerry Eurodyne (Male V only)
- **Questline**: Holdin' On → Second Conflict → A Like Supreme → Rebel! Rebel! → I Don't Wanna Hear It → Off the Leash → Boat Drinks (7 total).
- **Key choices**: help Kerry with the Rebel! Rebel! show; first kiss available at **Off the Leash** (Dark Matter rooftop); confirm the relationship with his kiss on the yacht "Boat Drinks."

## 5. Phantom Liberty DLC & the "Tower" Ending

- **Release**: 2023-09-26; new area **Dogtown (Pacifica)**, key figures netrunner **Songbird** and FIA agent **Solomon Reed (Idris Elba)**.
- **Unlock**: after beating base-game Act 2 "Transmission," Songbird contacts V to enter Dogtown; new players can "Skip to Phantom Liberty" from the main menu at **level 15**.
- **Core fork**: at the **Firestarter** mission, a major "help Songbird or help Reed" branch; the DLC has **4 endings** and **affects the base-game ending** (especially unlocking the new "Tower" ending). Manual-save before Firestarter.
- **New system**: the **Relic skill tree**, one of the strongest endgame growth lines, complements the base build.
- **Recommended level**: ~**35–45** entering; some skill checks failing triggers extra combat.

## FAQ

**Q1: Which is the "+1" in 5+1, and where does the hidden ending rank?**
A: 5 base endings (Devil/Sun/Star/Temperance/Suicide) + 1 DLC-exclusive "Tower" = 6 regular endings; plus 1 **hidden ending (solo raid)** that isn't counted in 5+1 but has its own achievement.

**Q2: Does the hidden ending require multiple playthroughs?**
A: No. With Johnny affinity ≥70%, Johnny sidequests done, and ~5 minutes of waiting after "Think of another way" in Nocturne, it triggers in the same playthrough; manual-save before the mission to revert.

**Q3: Does Judy's route lock after Pisces?**
A: No. Pisces decides **whether it continues** — taking Maiko's money **permanently ends** the romance; the actual relationship is confirmed later at the Pyramid Song dock ("this is a good start").

**Q4: Can I romance both Panam and River (both "female V")?**
A: V can only commit to **one** partner per playthrough, and romance is irreversible once triggered; Panam needs male V, River needs female V — mutually exclusive by gender gate, can't have both in one save.

**Q5: After the Tower ending, can I still use cyberware and Relic skills?**
A: No. In the Tower the chip is removed and V wakes after a 2-year coma as an ordinary person, **unable to use cyberware**; the Relic skill tree also stops working — the highest-cost "survival" ending.

**Q6: How much does Phantom Liberty affect the base ending?**
A: Significantly — the Firestarter choice unlocks and decides the base-game "Tower" ending's direction. If you care about V's final fate, plan the DLC and base endings together.
