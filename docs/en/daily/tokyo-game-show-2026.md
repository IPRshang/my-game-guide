# Tokyo Game Show 2026 Preview: Dates, Highlights & How to Watch

**Tokyo Game Show 2026 (TGS 2026)** runs **September 17-21, 2026** at **Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan**. This year marks the expo's **30th anniversary** and, for the first time, it spans **five full days** — making it the biggest TGS yet and one of the key gaming events of the second half of 2026. This preview lays out the schedule, the confirmed showcases, how to watch, and ties in the related game guides we already cover.

## 1. Key facts (confirmed)

- **Dates**: September 17 (Thu) – 21 (Mon/holiday), 2026 — 5 days.
- **Business days**: Sept 17-18, 10:00–17:00.
- **Public days**: Sept 19-20, 9:30–17:00; Sept 21, 9:30–16:00.
- **Venue**: Makuhari Messe (Halls 1–11, International Convention Hall) + TKP Tokyo Bay Makuhari Hall.
- **Scale**: ~300,000 visitors and ~3,500 booths expected (tentative).
- **Theme**: 30th anniversary — "the longest ever, a five-day show to enjoy to the fullest."

## 2. Confirmed program highlights

- **Opening Ceremony**: Sept 17, 09:30–10:00.
- **Keynote "30 Years of Tokyo Game Show"**: Sept 17, 11:00–11:50, with CESA chairman Haruhiro Tsujimoto.
- **Xbox Tokyo Game Show 2026 Broadcast**: Sept 17, 19:00–19:50 — watch for Xbox platform announcements.
- **Japan Game Awards 2026**: the METI Minister's Award / Game of the Year (Sept 15) and the Future Division ceremony (Sept 20).
- **30th-anniversary stages** and multiple publisher showcases (full lineup drops gradually).

## 3. How to watch

The official program streams across **eight platforms** — YouTube, X (Twitter), Nico Nico, Twitch, TikTok, DouYu, bilibili, and Douyin — so you can follow along live from anywhere.

## 4. Why it matters

TGS is the largest game expo in Asia, and several games we cover come from Asian studios — NetEase's *Ananta*, Hypergryph's *Arknights: Endfield*, and Game Science's *Black Myth* line. While each publisher's booth list is confirmed separately, a regional mega-show like this is a frequent source of new trailers, gameplay, and release-date news, so it's worth watching around September 17.
