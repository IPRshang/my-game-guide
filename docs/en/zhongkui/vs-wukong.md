# Black Myth: Zhong Kui vs Wukong: What's the Difference

**Black Myth: Zhong Kui and Black Myth: Wukong belong to the same "Black Myth" brand from Game Science, but their protagonist, combat system and source material are entirely different — Zhong Kui is a standalone title, neither a Wukong sequel nor a DLC.**

## Protagonist: Zhong Kui ≠ Sun Wukong

- **Zhong Kui**: a Taoist deity in Chinese folklore, traditionally the "demon-queller" who moves between the human world and the underworld. In-game he is a playable swordsman / exorcist with no story link to Wukong.
- **Wukong (the Destined One)**: a perspective drawn from Journey to the West, continuing the Monkey King's arc.

## Combat: Sword + Parry vs Staff + Transform

| Aspect | Black Myth: Zhong Kui | Black Myth: Wukong |
|---|---|---|
| Main weapon | Single-handed sword + martial arts | The staff (Ruyi Jingu Bang) |
| Core mechanics | Parry / deflect, fire & lightning talismans, summon & possess | Staff stances, 72 transformations |
| Source material | Scattered folklore, opera, centuries of literature | The single text Journey to the West |
| Movement | Backstep dodge, underwater exploration | Somersault-style traversal, transformation |

The standout design in the Zhong Kui demo turns the myth of "Zhong Kui commanding 80,000 demons" into a **capture — summon — possess** combat loop, rather than a simple reskin.

## Relationship: Standalone, Not DLC / Sequel

Game Science has been explicit:
- Zhong Kui is a **standalone single-player action RPG**, not a narrative continuation of Wukong;
- it is not DLC and does not depend on the base game.

## Why Game Science Shelved the Wukong DLC

- On August 20, 2025 (the 1st anniversary), producer Feng Ji explained in a long post that the team had pushed "DLC first" for over half a year, but Yang Qi asked whether he'd rather abandon the DLC and make something new — and they agreed to pivot.
- Yang Qi later summarized: "those old tricks and illusions don't work anymore; if we touch Journey to the West again, we need a new ground and to sharpen the fundamentals first" — meaning the DLC was shelved in favor of returning to the Westward Journey in a more complete form.
- Official stance: the **major story DLC for Wukong is shelved** (not officially cancelled, but resources clearly shifted to Zhong Kui); the Westward Journey story will "return in a more complete and solid form" in the future.

> Note: the above reflects public interviews and official long-form posts; concrete project plans follow Game Science's later announcements.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Zhong Kui a sequel to Wukong?**
A: No. Both carry the "Black Myth" brand, but their protagonist, combat and material differ, with no story continuity.

**Q: So is the Wukong DLC still happening?**
A: Per the official posts, the major story DLC is shelved and the team's resources moved to Zhong Kui. The studio says the Westward Journey will return "in a more complete, solid form," but not as DLC.

**Q: Is the combat very different from Wukong?**
A: Very. Zhong Kui leads with sword + parry/deflect + fire/lightning talismans + summon/possess, whereas Wukong uses staff stances + transformations. The feel is completely distinct.
