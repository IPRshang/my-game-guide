# Black Myth: Zhong Kui Platforms & Purchase Guide

> Bottom line: Zhong Kui launches on **PC (Steam + Epic Games Store)** and **current-gen consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S)**, as a **buy-to-play title with no microtransactions**, continuing Wukong's model. Pre-orders are not open; release date is unannounced (industry expects 2027 or later).

## 2. PC: Steam + Epic

- Confirmed for PC via **Steam and Epic Games Store**;
- Per ChinaJoy 2026 demo, the PC version gets visual options and **MOD support** absent on consoles;
- Demo machine ran RTX 5080 + 4K + path tracing + DLSS 4 frame gen — high PC ceiling.

## 3. Consoles: PS5 / Xbox Series X|S

- Current-gen only, **no PS4 / Xbox One**;
- Keep console OS up to date to run the game;
- Demo ran on a PC with a gamepad, so console feel should match PC.

## 4. Business Model: Buy-to-Play, No MTX

- Continues Black Myth: Wukong's standard single-player **buy-to-play** model;
- **No microtransactions / gacha**;
- Pricing unannounced; reference Wukong's launch price band (official TBD).

## 5. Pre-Order Status

- **No pre-order or trial build open yet**;
- Ignore third-party "pre-order / proxy" links; trust only Game Science's official store and platform pages.

## 6. Co-op & Cross-Play

- The Aug 20 demo ended teasing a 2-player co-op mode "**Judgment Companions**" (per show reports);
- Cross-play / online requirement unconfirmed; await launch notes.

## 7. Buying Advice

- Want day-one full content + MODs: pick **PC (Steam)**;
- Want couch convenience: **PS5 / Xbox Series X|S**;
- PS4/Xbox One owners need a hardware upgrade.
