# Black Myth: Zhong Kui Guide Hub

> **Black Myth: Zhong Kui** is Game Science's **standalone follow-up** to Black Myth: Wukong — revealed at Gamescom 2025 and shown in a 15-minute gameplay demo on August 20, 2026. It is **not a DLC or sequel** to Wukong; the protagonist Zhong Kui is a Taoist demon-queller.

## What Is Zhong Kui?

- Second game in Game Science's "Black Myth" brand; a single-player action RPG.
- Protagonist Zhong Kui: a Taoist deity, the "demon-queller," unrelated to Sun Wukong.
- Combat: one-handed sword + parry + fire/thunder talismans + summon & possession.
- Platforms: PC and "major consoles" (final list TBA).

## Confirmed Key Facts

- **Reveal:** August 19, 2025, Gamescom Opening Night Live — CG teaser.
- **Gameplay demo:** August 20, 2026 — 15 minutes of in-development footage, first combat and the multi-phase boss "Giant Toad True Lord."
- **Positioning:** standalone single-player action RPG, not a DLC, not a sequel.

## Release Date & Platforms

No release date yet. Based on Wukong's ~4-year public dev cycle, the industry broadly expects **2027 or later**. See [Zhong Kui Release Date & Platforms](./release-date).

## How It Differs From Wukong

Zhong Kui and Wukong share the "Black Myth" brand but differ in protagonist, combat, and source material; Game Science has also shelved Wukong's major story DLC and shifted resources to Zhong Kui. See [Black Myth: Zhong Kui vs Wukong](./vs-wukong).
