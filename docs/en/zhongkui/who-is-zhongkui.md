# Who Is Zhong Kui? The Taoist Ghost-Catching Deity & Game Setup

> In one line: Zhong Kui is the folk-religion "**Saint of Blessing and Home Protection**" and a **ghost-catching deity** — Game Science casts him as the protagonist of Black Myth: Zhong Kui, with zero relation to Sun Wukong.

## 1. Zhong Kui in History

- Said to be a **Tang-dynasty scholar** who, for his stark look and unbending integrity, was appointed "Demon-Subduing Grand Deity" after death;
- In folk belief he symbolizes **exorcism, ghost-slaying, and home blessing**;
- Traditional New Year prints and operas feature "Zhong Kui Catches Ghosts" and "Zhong Kui Marries Off His Sister."

## 2. Folk Image: Blessing, Protection, Exorcism

- His core tag is "**ghost-catching celestial master**," moving between the living and the underworld;
- Entirely different route from Sun Wukong's "Monkey King / Destined One";
- Naturally fits an "action RPG + ghost folklore" setting.

## 3. Zhong Kui in the Game

Game Science turns myth into gameplay:

- **Protagonist**: playable swordsman / ghost-catcher;
- **Core combat**: single-handed sword + block/parry + fire/lightning talismans;
- **Commanding 80,000 ghost soldiers**: becomes the "capture → summon → possess" loop (see [Combat System](/en/zhongkui/combat));
- **Source**: scattered folk legends, opera, and literature — not a single text.

## 4. Zhong Kui ≠ Sun Wukong

| Aspect | Zhong Kui | Sun Wukong (Destined One) |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Taoism / folklore | Journey to the West |
| Identity | Ghost-catching deity | Monkey King / Destined One |
| Combat | Sword + parry + talismans | Staff + transformation |
| Story link | Unrelated to Wukong | — |

See [Zhong Kui vs Wukong](/en/zhongkui/vs-wukong).

## 5. Why Zhong Kui as the New Lead?

- "Black Myth" is not a sequel to one storyline but a **multi-protagonist Chinese-mythology universe**;
- Zhong Kui brings a strong "ghost + underworld + talisman" action DNA, clearly differentiated;
- Complements Wukong as "one fights heaven, one catches ghosts."

## FAQ

**Q: Are Zhong Kui and Sun Wukong the same story?**
A: No. Same "Black Myth" brand, but different protagonist, theme, and plot.

**Q: Was Zhong Kui a real historical figure?**
A: A folk/deity figure; historicity is unsettled, with tales often attached to the Tang era.

**Q: Can Zhong Kui transform in-game?**
A: The demo shows "summon / possess ghost soldiers," different from Wukong's 72 transformations.
