# When Does Black Myth: Zhong Kui Launch? Release Date

**Black Myth: Zhong Kui is developed by Game Science. It was first revealed at Gamescom Opening Night Live 2025, then showed 15 minutes of in-development gameplay on August 20, 2026 (Wukong's 2nd anniversary). No launch date or window has been announced; industry estimates, based on Wukong's dev cycle, point to 2027 or later. It is a standalone title — not a Wukong DLC or sequel.**

## What's Confirmed

- **Reveal**: August 19, 2025, at Gamescom Opening Night Live — a CG teaser confirmed it as "the next Black Myth title," in early development.
- **Gameplay demo**: August 20, 2026 — with no showcase and no embargo, Game Science dropped 15 minutes of work-in-progress footage, the first real look at combat: single-handed sword, parry/deflect, fire & lightning talismans, summon-and-possess mechanics, an underwater segment, and a multi-phase boss "Giant Clam True Lord."
