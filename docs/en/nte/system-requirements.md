# Neverness to Everness System Requirements (2026)

Neverness to Everness (NTE) launched worldwide on **April 29, 2026** across **PC (Windows), Mac, iOS, Android, and PS5** (no Xbox), with full cross-platform progression. Below are the **minimum** and **recommended** specs per platform so you can judge whether your device will run it smoothly.

## Platform overview

| Platform | Status | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| PC (Windows) | Live | Official launcher / Steam / Epic |
| Mac | Live | Apple Silicon (M1+) & macOS 14.0+ |
| PlayStation 5 | Live | Cross-progression |
| Android | Live | Cross-play |
| iOS / iPadOS | Live | iPhone 12 Pro and up |

> NTE is an urban open-world ARPG by Hotta Studio (Tower of Fantasy devs), published by Perfect World Games.

## PC system requirements

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| OS | Windows 10 64-bit | Windows 10 / 11 64-bit |
| CPU | Intel Core i7-10700 (10th Gen) or equivalent | Intel Core i7-12700 (12th Gen) or equivalent |
| GPU | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 / AMD RX 5600 or equivalent | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 / AMD RX 6700 or equivalent |
| RAM | 16 GB | 32 GB or more |
| Storage | 60 GB+ free (extra 60 GB temporarily during install) | Install on an SSD |
| DirectX | 11 | 12 |

> **SSD advice**: the publisher recommends an SSD. Installation temporarily needs ~60 GB extra.

## Mobile & console specs

- **Android**: Android 10.0+; minimum Snapdragon 855 / Dimensity 8000, recommended Snapdragon 8 Gen2 / 8 Gen3 / Dimensity 9200; RAM 6 GB; 20 GB+ storage.
- **iOS / iPadOS**: iOS 15.0+; minimum iPhone 12 Pro Max, recommended iPhone 14 Pro; RAM 6 GB; 20 GB+ storage.
- **Mac**: Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or newer; Intel Macs are not supported.
- **PS5**: Console version.

## FAQ

**Q: How much disk space does NTE need?**
A: At least 60 GB on PC, with ~60 GB extra temporarily during installation. Install on an SSD and keep ~120 GB free to be safe.

**Q: Can a GTX 1660 run it?**
A: Yes — it is the official minimum GPU. Expect lower settings; for higher quality see the recommended spec (RTX 3060 and up).

**Q: Is there a Mac version?**
A: Yes, shipped at launch, but only on Apple Silicon (M1+) with macOS 14.0+. Intel Macs are not supported.

**Q: Do iOS and Android progress sync?**
A: Yes. The same PWG account syncs across platforms under one UID.
