# How to Use the NTE Gacha Simulator?

**The NTE gacha simulator lets you simulate banner odds, rehearse your pull rhythm, and predict when an S-rank character drops — all without spending real resources, making it a great tool to plan your progression economy.**

## Steps

1. Open the **[interactive gacha simulator](/en/nte/gacha)** in the /nte/ section of this site
2. Select your target banner (e.g. the current featured S-rank)
3. Set the number of simulated pulls and watch the S-rank / A-rank distribution and pity trigger point
4. Combine with the [beginner guide](/en/nte/beginner) to decide your real pull priority

## Why Simulate First

- **Rehearse pity**: see roughly how many pulls you need for your target S-rank, avoiding impulse-draining your currency.
- **Compare banners**: expected pulls differ per featured character; simulating helps you pick a banner more rationally.
- **Zero-cost trial**: consumes no real resources, purely for planning.

> Simulator odds are illustrative community estimates for planning and fun only; actual odds follow the in-game disclosure.
