# NTE Gacha Simulator

Plan your pulls before spending currency. Run single or 10-pulls and track your results.

<NteGacha />

## How to use it

- **Single Pull / 10-Pull** — simulate draws and watch the log fill in.
- **Stats** — track total SSR / SR / R pulled.
- **Reset** — clear and run a fresh simulation.

## Tips

- Use it to estimate how many pulls you need for a target character.
- Soft-pity is approximated above 70 pulls — real rates follow the in-game banner.
- Combine with the [Character Tier List](./tier-list.md) to decide who to chase.

> Probabilities shown are **illustrative** (community-estimated). Use for planning, not as
> official rates.
