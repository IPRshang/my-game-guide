Redeem codes are the quickest free boost in Neverness to Everness. This page covers **where
codes appear** and **how to redeem them**, then tracks active codes as a community resource.

Most code guides just dump a list. The problem with NTE is that a static list lies the moment a code expires — and codes here are **time-limited and case-sensitive**, so a copied list is only as good as the hour you read it. Our approach: explain *where* codes originate so you can catch them at the source, and keep a tracker that flags what's still live. Pair this with the [NTE beginner roadmap](/en/nte/beginner/) so your free materials actually reach the right characters.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** players screenshot a code from a random post, type it weeks later, and assume the game is broken when it says "invalid." In NTE, expired and mistyped codes both fail silently — redeem at the source, the day it drops.

## How to redeem a code

1. Open **Settings** from the main menu.
2. Find the **Redemption / Code** option.
3. Enter the code and confirm.
4. Collect rewards from your in-game **mail**.

**Our take:** step 4 is the step people forget. Rewards don't land in your inventory — they sit in mail, and unclaimed mail can cap out. Redeem, then *collect*, every time.

> Codes are usually **time-limited** and **case-sensitive**. Redeem promptly.

**Our take:** if you only watch one channel, watch Discord. It's the first and most consistent source; the other channels typically echo what lands there. Link it from the [NTE hub](/en/nte/README/) and turn on notifications.

## Common Mistakes — Our Analysis

1. **Waiting to redeem.** NTE codes are time-limited; by the time a code reaches a screenshot round, it may already be dead. Fix: redeem from the source the day it drops.
2. **Typing codes by hand.** They're case-sensitive — one wrong capital and it fails. Fix: copy-paste directly from the official channel.
3. **Forgetting to collect mail.** Redemption succeeds but rewards stay in mail. Fix: open mail after every redeem.
