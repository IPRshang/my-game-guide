# NTE Version 1.3 Overview: New Characters & Duskmoon Preview

Neverness to Everness (NTE) Version 1.3 went live in August 2026: **China on August 12, Global on August 19**. The update brings new characters, a new permanent mode, and a batch of limited-time events — but the most anticipated new region, Duskmoon, has not yet arrived as expected.

## Release timing

- China: August 12, 2026
- Global: August 19, 2026

There is roughly a one-week gap between the two servers, but the core content is the same.

## New characters

Version 1.3 introduces two new characters:

- **Shinku**: a Cosmos-attribute DPS whose Ultimate consumes the unique buffs she holds to deal increased damage. Her limited Arc "Blushing Mirage" has been added to the Arc Research Program.
- **Iroi**: a new character debuting in 1.3. Official details on her kit are relatively limited, with more to be revealed after launch.

For team-building ideas on these two, see the [NTE Shinku Build & Teams](/en/nte/character-shinku) and [NTE Iroi Build & Teams](/en/nte/character-iroi) guides.

## New permanent mode: 999 Nights

Version 1.3 adds the permanent mode **"999 Nights"** — a single-player, board-game-style adventure across the fantasy **"Auren Continent."** Unlike the main open world, this is a longer-form, more strategy-leaning solo experience.

## Limited-time events

Events running during 1.3 include:

- Volley Star
- Surf Breaker
- Hunter's Crucible
- Shipwreck Salvage
- Runaway Echoes

along with more events not listed individually here.

## Duskmoon preview (delayed)

Important note: the new region **"Duskmoon" was delayed in this 1.3 update**, and the community is still waiting for it. The studio has not yet given a firm release date; keep an eye on later version announcements.
