# NTE Character Tier List

A practical ranking of which NTE characters are worth your early pulls and resources.

## S-Tier — pull first

- **Exploration anchors** — characters that unlock shortcuts, traversal, or map control.
- **Burst DPS** — clear combat encounters fast, the core of most content.

## A-Tier — strong core

- **Sustain / healer** — keep the team alive in longer open-world fights.
- **Flex DPS** — good damage with useful utility.

## B-Tier — situational

- **Support / buffer** — valuable once your main team is set.
- **Niche controllers** — strong in specific stages.

## How to use this list

1. Spend early pulls on an **S-tier anchor or DPS**.
2. Add a **sustain** unit from A-tier to survive open-world bosses.
3. Fill gaps, then refine with banners you actually want.

> Rankings shift with new characters and balance patches. Re-check after each update.
