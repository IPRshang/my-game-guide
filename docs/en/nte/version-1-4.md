Annulith is NTE's gacha currency (universal across banners). During the 1.3 → 1.4 transition, it's recommended to plan as follows:

- **Whether to pull Linko in 1.3**: Linko (Anima general support, 9/9 - 9/30) is the second-half limited of 1.3. If you already have a formed Anima team and limited Annulith reserves, **consider skipping Linko to stockpile resources for 1.4**; if you're severely lacking a general support, you can do a small pull to fill the slot. - **Priority advice (leaked perspective)**: Akane Rin (versatile support) > Blackbird / Merula (functional / magic slot). But be sure to wait for official gameplay and stats before making a final decision.
