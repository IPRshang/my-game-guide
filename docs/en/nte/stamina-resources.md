# NTE Stamina & Resources Guide

In **Neverness to Everness (NTE)**, "resource management" is where most new players trip up. The game has **three separate stamina pools** and a pile of oddly named currencies — mix them up and you either stall your level or burn rare currency on the wrong thing. This guide breaks it all down.

> Note: figures are based on in-game behavior and community consensus; they may shift with patches. Always defer to the client.

## 1. The Three Stamina Systems

NTE's "stamina" is not one bar but three independent resources:

| Name | Used for | Recovery | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Character Pixels** | Anomaly zones — char/Arc/Console breakthrough mats, Hunter XP | **+1 every 6 min**; Diamonds (daily cap 6) | base **240** |
| **City Stamina** | City Tycoon (rides, heists, shops) → earns **Fons** | **refills every Monday 5:00**; Diamonds (weekly cap 10) | scales with Fame |
| **Combat Stamina** (white bar by char) | dodge, wall-run, swim | refills when not used | raised via Oracle Stone |

### Character Pixels (the workhorse)
- Anomaly zones are its only home. Char/Arc/Console breakthrough materials come only from here.
- +1 per 6 min → 240 in 24h. **Spend the natural refill daily**; don't let it overflow.
- Diamond recovery: 60 (uses 1–3) / 120 (uses 4–6), resets 5:00 daily. Early on, **spend Diamonds on Pixels first**, not City Stamina.
- Cap raised by the anomaly furniture "Yumeori Musubi" (unlocks at houses 2 & 4).

### City Stamina (your money printer)
- Spend stamina → earn **Fons** (the generic currency). Pink Claw Heist is the fastest Fons source pre-mid (unlocks Lv10).
- Refills Monday; use it before the weekend. Diamond recovery: weekly 10 uses (100 / 150 / 200 tiers).
- Higher Fame = higher City Stamina cap, so **push story/side quests** to lift Fame.

### Combat Stamina
- The white bar beside your character; drains on dodge/parkour/swim, refills when idle.
- Raise cap via Oracle Stone → VitalMax at the Witch's house; some anomaly furniture cut consumption.

## 2. Core Currencies

### Annulith — rarest, guard it
- Gacha currency: **160 Annulith = 1 die**.
- Two banners: **Limited (Solid Dice)** and **Standard (Synthetic Dice)**.
  - Standard is permanent → **never trade Annulith for Synthetic Dice**.
  - Limited rotates every 3 weeks → **save most Annulith for Solid Dice**.
  - After getting your limited, spill some Annulith into Arc banners for signature Arcs.
- ⚠️ **Never spend Annulith to refill Pixels**, especially as F2P/low-spender.

### Fons — the universal bottleneck
- City Tycoon, vehicles, shops all eat Fons. Dries up after quests → main "experience" bottleneck.
- Smart use: don't buy every vehicle on sight; save for better ones; invest in Tycoon level that unlocks content.

### Other mats
- **Arc / Console**: signature weapon & console; breakthrough needs Pixel-farmed mats.
- **Hunter Manual / Dye / Beetle Coin**: XP mats; some from quests, rest from Pixels.

## 3. Beginner Resource Rules

1. **Raise only 4–6 characters**; trim to 4 once your core is set — one strong team beats many weak ones.
2. **Early Pixels → main DPS breakthrough mats first**; XP mats second; **don't spend Pixels on Consoles before gold Console unlocks**.
3. **Story/side quests cost 0 stamina and give the most XP** — clear side quests when level-gated.
4. **City Tycoon is ~half the game** — spend City Stamina weekly for Fons & Tycoon level (can grant a awakened S-rank + signature Arc).
5. **Annulith only for Solid Dice**; stack Synthetic Dice from the standard banner.

## 4. FAQ

**Q: Pixels always short?**
A: Use the full 240 daily refill + raise cap via furniture + cheap Diamond refills (first 3/day). Don't hoard.

**Q: Never enough Fons?**
A: Pink Claw Heist (Lv10) is the fastest early source; single story quests can pay 80k+ Fons. Don't impulse-buy vehicles.

**Q: Can I buy stamina with Annulith?**
A: Technically yes, but **strongly not recommended** — Annulith caps your pulls; stamina refill is terrible value vs. limited banners.
