# NTE Shinku Build & Teams: Cosmic DPS Guide

Shinku is a new character in Neverness to Everness (NTE) Version 1.3, positioned as a **Cosmos-attribute DPS**. Her core kit revolves around "buff management": her Ultimate **consumes the unique buffs she holds** to deal increased damage. In short, Shinku's burst timing depends on how many effective buffs you can stack and preserve before pressing Ultimate.

> Note: the following is based on currently revealed information; exact numbers and combos should be confirmed against live-server practice after launch.

## Understanding the core mechanic

Shinku's gameplay fits one sentence: **build buffs first, then cash them in at once.**

- She accumulates unique self-buffs through skills / basic attacks.
- Those buffs aren't kept forever — they must be spent with her Ultimate within the right window.
- Each buff consumed raises her Ultimate's damage.

So the skill isn't "pop Ultimate as fast as possible," but "pop it when buffs are stacked or nearly full," avoiding wasted stacks.

## Team-building logic

Given her Cosmos DPS role, teams should aim at two things: amplifying her burst window, and providing buffs / sustain.

1. **Sub-DPS / buffer**: pick teammates whose attribute complements Shinku and who can set up the field or grant damage buffs before her Ultimate, making her burst more reliable.
2. **Sustain / control**: a Cosmos DPS is typically fragile; the team needs someone who can tank or control, giving Shinku a safe damage window.
3. **Breaker / sub-output**: improve stagger efficiency against elites and bosses, shortening fights.

Concrete pairings can be tuned with the [NTE Tier List](/en/nte/tier-list) and [NTE Team Build](/en/nte/team-build).

## Build direction

- **Core goal**: maximize the payoff of buffs consumed by Ultimate. Prioritize stats / gear tied to "Ultimate damage" and "buff-related gains."
- **Stat trade-offs**: balance crit, damage-up and survival — as a main DPS, crit / damage-up usually wins, but don't get one-shot.
- **Habit**: practice the "stack then spend" rhythm; don't dump buffs too early.

## Limited Arc: Blushing Mirage

Shinku's limited Arc **"Blushing Mirage"** has been added to the **Arc Research Program**. If you plan to invest in Shinku long-term, keep an eye on that Arc's acquisition and upgrade.

- Sustain: frontline or control-type character
- Flex: breaker or sub-output depending on the encounter
