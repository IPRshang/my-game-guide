# NTE Iroi Build & Teams Guide

Iroi is a new character introduced in Neverness to Everness (NTE) Version 1.3. Compared with her 1.3 counterpart Shinku, Hotta Studio has so far revealed relatively little about Iroi's specific kit, so this guide does not speculate on her skill numbers. Instead, it focuses on **general 1.3 pull and team-building principles** and explains what details need to be filled in after she launches.

> Note: Iroi's exact role, attribute and skill set should be taken from official info at launch. This page invents no numbers.

## Positioning as a 1.3 newcomer

Iroi is one of the new characters in Version 1.3. Until her kit is fully revealed, treat her as a "to-be-revealed new unit": observe her attribute class and team role (damage / buffer / sustain) first, then decide her training priority.

## Pull principles (1.3)

NTE's gacha is known for being **free-to-play friendly**: there is **no 50/50 mechanic** and the **pity system is clear**. That makes acquiring new characters (including Iroi) relatively predictable, easing the resource-planning pressure versus many peers.

- If your resources are tight, confirm Iroi's fit with your current roster before investing.
- For more on gacha mechanics and value, see the [NTE Gacha Guide](/en/nte/gacha).

## General team-building principles

Whatever Iroi's final role, teams can follow this logic:

1. **Clarify main-DPS vs support roles**: decide who the core damage dealer is, then place Iroi where she shores up a weakness.
2. **Attribute complement**: use the [NTE Tier List](/en/nte/tier-list) and [NTE Team Build](/en/nte/team-build) to cover more mechanics.
3. **Sustain safety net**: trial any new character inside a lineup with stable survival — don't blindly stack damage and wipe.

## What to confirm after launch

Once Iroi goes live, prioritize confirming:

- Her attribute and skill-set role;
- Her synergy with 1.3 characters like Shinku;
- Recommended gear / Arc direction (see the relevant Arc Research Program content).
