# NTE vs Wuthering Waves: Which Urban Anomaly RPG to Play

Neverness to Everness (NTE) is often called an "anime GTA" and frequently compared with Wuthering Waves. Both are open-world RPGs, but their tone and focus differ. Below is a **balanced, non-defamatory** comparison across five dimensions to help you decide which suits you.

## 1. Gacha fairness

- **NTE**: its banners are known for being **free-to-play friendly** — **no 50/50 mechanic** and a **clear pity system** — so pulling new characters carries less pressure. That said, vehicle and skin costs have drawn criticism.
- **Wuthering Waves**: also an anime open-world gacha with a mature pity system; community sentiment on its fairness is generally positive.

Verdict: if easy pulling matters most, NTE's current reputation leads — but watch NTE's extra skin / vehicle spending.

## 2. Open-world freedom

- **NTE**: an urban-anomaly setting where city exploration and vehicle driving are core selling points, leaning into "freely roam the city" gameplay.
- **Wuthering Waves**: excels at field exploration, combat combos and map puzzles, with a more action-adventure pace.

The freedom types differ: pick NTE for "urban driving + life feel," Wuthering Waves for "field action exploration."

## 3. Optimization & stability

- **NTE**: players widely report crashes and poor performance, even on high-spec systems — among its biggest current weaknesses. See the [NTE Crash Fix Guide](/en/nte/fix-crash).
- **Wuthering Waves**: generally steadier optimization, smoother on mainstream configs.

Verdict: if your machine is modest or you demand stability, Wuthering Waves is the safer bet today.

## 4. Story & presentation

- **NTE**: the writing is often called clichéd or lacking depth, and cutscenes can feel overly long, with divided player feedback.
- **Wuthering Waves**: story and cinematics score above-average among peers, with a relatively tighter narrative pace.

## 5. Driving gameplay

- This is NTE's differentiator: urban vehicle driving is one of its core systems, though the handling is still criticized as clunky. See the [NTE Vehicle Driving Tips](/en/nte/driving-guide).
- Wuthering Waves does not center on driving; vehicles play a much smaller role.

## How to choose

- Want "urban anomaly + vehicles + F2P-friendly gacha" → pick NTE.
- Want "stable optimization + action exploration + mature storytelling" → pick Wuthering Waves.
- With time to spare, try both and choose by feel and theme.

## FAQ

**Q: Which has friendlier gacha, NTE or Wuthering Waves?**
A: NTE leads with no 50/50, a clear pity and F2P-friendly banners — but note its extra skin/vehicle costs.

**Q: Which is better optimized?**
A: Wuthering Waves is generally steadier; NTE still has crash and performance issues.

**Q: What is NTE's unique strength?**
A: Urban vehicle driving and F2P-friendly gacha — at the cost of criticized optimization and story depth.
