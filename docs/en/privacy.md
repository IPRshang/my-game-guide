# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-09

Welcome to **Game Strategy Hub** (this "Site"). We provide original strategy guides for popular games including GTA6, Black Myth Wukong, Elden Ring, Cyberpunk 2077, and Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. We respect your privacy, and this policy explains how information related to you is handled.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Information you provide**: When you use the comments feature (powered by Giscus via GitHub authorization), your authorized display name and avatar may appear with your comment. We do not ask for sensitive personal information such as your real name, phone number, or ID.
- **Automatically collected data**: We use Baidu Analytics to collect anonymized usage data (page views, referrers, device type, approximate region) to understand content performance and improve the site. This data is not used to identify you personally.

## 2. Cookies and Advertising

This Site uses **Google AdSense** to display ads that help support our operation. AdSense and its partners may use **cookies**, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

- Serve ads relevant to your interests (personalized advertising);
- Measure ad performance and prevent invalid activity.

These cookies may be set by Google and third-party advertisers (e.g., `NID`, `IDE`, `__gads`). Their storage and management are governed by Google's and the respective advertisers' privacy policies.

**Your choices**: You can manage ad personalization and cookies via:
- Google Ads Settings: https://www.google.com/settings/ads
- Clearing or disabling cookies in your browser settings;
- EU users may opt out via YourOnlineChoices (http://www.youronlinechoices.com/).

## 3. Third-Party Services

This Site may link to third-party websites (game official sites, stores, video platforms). They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or data practices.

## 4. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for general players and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a guardian and believe your child provided us personal information, contact us.

## 5. Contact

For questions about this policy, reach us via the comments section or the project repository (GitHub: IPRshang/my-game-guide).
