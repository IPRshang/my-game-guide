# Ananta Characters: Captain, Taffy, Richie, Seymour

> **Ananta**'s cast sits inside a clear organizational frame: you play an Esper investigator codenamed **Infinite Trigger**, a member of the **A.C.D. (Anti-Chaos Department)**, handling supernatural anomalies in the city of **Nova City**. This page collects the four characters known so far.

> **Reading note:** descriptions below are drawn from official announcements and trailer material. **Gameplay specifics come from reveals and trailers, not a final build**, and may change before launch.

## The Setting and the Organization

Before the cast, the frame they operate in.

**Nova City** is Ananta's stage — a modern metropolis where supernatural phenomena coexist with everyday life. The **A.C.D. (Anti-Chaos Department)** is the body responsible for investigating and containing those anomalies, and it is the organizational home of this cast.

**The player character: Infinite Trigger**

You play an **Esper investigator** codenamed **Infinite Trigger**, working as part of the A.C.D. That premise sets the narrative vantage point: you are not an outsider wandering in, you are an investigator inside the city's own institutions.

This matters for reading character relationships. The four characters below relate to the player through shared organizational duty and role division, not through incidental companion collection.

## Captain: Leader of the A.C.D.

**Confirmed:** Captain is the **leader of the A.C.D.**

As head of the Anti-Chaos Department, Captain sits at the top of the organizational structure, which typically implies a narrative function around assigning cases, setting direction, and driving key story beats.

**On the design discussion:** one observation that comes up repeatedly is that **Captain's look reminds some players of Rover from Wuthering Waves**. That is a player-level visual comparison, and it is one reason Ananta and Wuthering Waves are frequently discussed side by side.

**To be clear:** the confirmed point about Captain is the "leader of the A.C.D." role. Combat positioning and skill specifics should be taken from future official material rather than inferred here.

## Taffy: Yeet Squad, and a Hammer That Becomes a Bicycle

**Confirmed:** Taffy belongs to the **Yeet squad**, and her **hammer transforms into a bicycle**.

Taffy is the most immediately memorable of the four, precisely because of that transformation. The design tells you two things about how Ananta builds characters.

**1. Weapons and traversal are unified**

In Ananta, a character's weapon is not only a combat tool — it can also be a means of getting around. A hammer that becomes a bicycle means **a direct switch between combat form and traversal form**. That fits the core of an urban open world neatly: the city needs to be crossed efficiently, and the character is part of how you cross it.

**2. A deliberately light, absurd tone**

The name "Yeet squad" and the hammer-bicycle mechanic both point to a design attitude that is not chasing solemnity. That matches Ananta's "anime GTA" positioning — a city sandbox naturally accommodates absurdity and play.

**On gameplay:** the transformation comes from reveal material. **The exact inputs, transformation conditions, and how it actually feels have not been fully detailed**, so treat the shipped version as authoritative.

## Richie: Veteran Professional

**Confirmed:** Richie is a **veteran professional**.

That positioning supplies the experience axis in the cast. Against the younger, looser energy implied by the Yeet squad, Richie represents the seasoned, professionalized side.

In an organization whose core mandate is investigating supernatural anomalies, a character like this usually carries the narrative function of passing on experience and providing technical grounding. **However, Richie's specific field, combat style, and skill makeup have not been elaborated in official material**, so no speculation is offered here.

## Seymour: Hacker

**Confirmed:** Seymour is a **hacker**.

A hacker role is highly functional in an urban setting. In a modern metropolis like Nova City, information, surveillance, and network systems are inherently part of the city's structure, and a hacker character maps onto the ability to intervene at that layer.

Viewed as a team composition, Seymour complements the other three:

| Character | Confirmed role |
|-----------|---------------|
| Captain | Leader of the A.C.D. |
| Taffy | Yeet squad member (hammer transforms into a bicycle) |
| Richie | Veteran professional |
| Seymour | Hacker |

Those four roles cover **command, mobility, experience, and technical access** — the skeleton of an investigation team with clean role division.

**Again, a caveat:** how Seymour's hacking manifests in actual gameplay — whether there are dedicated puzzle, intrusion, or information systems — **has not been fully explained officially**.

## How Characters Are Obtained: Mind the Information Boundary

This is one of the most asked questions and one of the most frequently misreported.

**Confirmed:** Ananta is **free-to-play**.

**Rumored (unconfirmed by NetEase):** Japanese press reports say Ananta will have **no gacha, give all characters for free, and monetize via cosmetics only**.

**Note carefully: "all characters free" remains a rumor and has never been officially confirmed by NetEase.** Being free-to-play does not by itself determine how characters are acquired. The full breakdown is in [Ananta Monetization: Confirmed vs Rumored](/en/ananta/monetization).
