# Ananta Beginner Guide: What to Do First

**Ananta (formerly Project Mugen) is NetEase Naked Rain's free urban open-world RPG, launching globally on January 15, 2027. The core loops blend "anime-style urban open world + Spider-Man-like movement + supernatural Chaos events + a social-media influence system." This page helps you see what it actually is and what to focus on at launch.**

## World & Cities

- **Nova City**: a neon-chaotic metropolis inspired by Tokyo, full of supernatural anomalies (Chaos events).
- **Chongxiao**: the newly added second city, modeled on Hangzhou / Chongqing, with mahjong parlors, arcades, cat cafés and hot springs — a calmer pace.

## Core Systems

- **Influencer / social system**: you play a "content creator" identity, completing city tasks and generating buzz to build social influence and reputation, unlocking new resources and perks. This is what sets Ananta apart from a standard open-world RPG.
- **Yeet Squad**: recruit NPCs with their own stories into a squad, build relationships, unlock exclusive items and combat abilities.
- **Vehicles & movement**: cars, motorcycles, helicopters, yachts, subways, hang gliders and more, with a Spider-Man-like traversal feel.
- **Martial-arts combat**: blends fighting moves with environmental interaction, turning everyday objects into improvised weapons and rewarding creative play.

## On "No Character Gacha"

- Per Japanese media reports, Ananta has **no character gacha**; every character is free, with revenue from cosmetics.
- **NetEase has not officially confirmed** this. The monetization model may change before launch — follow official announcements. See [Ananta monetization: confirmed vs rumored](/en/ananta/monetization).

> Note: the above qualitative gameplay comes from public demos and official intros; exact mechanics follow the shipped version.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Ananta a gacha game?**
A: Per Japanese media reports it has "no character gacha," all characters free, cosmetic-only revenue — but NetEase has not officially confirmed it; follow official announcements pre-launch.

**Q: How many cities does Ananta have?**
A: Two are public so far — Nova City and the newly added Chongxiao; whether more come later is up to the studio.

**Q: What should a beginner do first?**
A: Follow the main story and social-influence tasks to learn movement and combat, then build your Yeet Squad; the exact leveling route follows the shipped version.
