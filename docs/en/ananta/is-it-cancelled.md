# Is Ananta Cancelled? Status & Official Timeline

> **Short answer: Ananta is not cancelled.** The game is confirmed to be in development and is showing at **Gamescom Opening Night Live on August 25, 2026**. The "it got axed" narrative came from a long silent stretch after the rename, not from any official announcement.

## Current Status at a Glance

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| Cancelled? | **No** — confirmed in development |
| Official cancellation notice? | **None** |
| Next public appearance | Gamescom ONL, August 25, 2026 (confirmed lineup) |
| Official release date | **Not announced** |
| Developer | NetEase, Naked Rain studio (Hangzhou, ~800 people) |

Two things need to be kept separate: **"cancelled"** and **"has no release date."** The first is false. The second is true. Ananta still has no official release date, and the absence of a date is easy to misread as trouble — but a missing date is not a terminated project.

## The Official Timeline: Four Nodes That Refute the Rumor

The strongest rebuttal to cancellation talk is simply connecting the official dots. A cancelled project does not keep getting renamed, keep appearing at shows, and keep releasing gameplay.

### Node 1: August 24, 2023 — Revealed at Gamescom

The game was first announced under the development codename **"Project Mugen"** at Gamescom. That is the origin point for both its public profile and its ongoing association with the Gamescom stage.

### Node 2: December 5, 2024 — Officially Renamed Ananta

The project was formally renamed from "Project Mugen" to **"Ananta,"** taken from Sanskrit, meaning **"infinite / without end."**

This node carries real weight when judging project health: **a formal naming usually happens only after a project reaches stable footing.** Publishers do not spend on brand identity, trademark work, and global marketing assets for a title they intend to shelve. The late-2024 rename is itself a strong signal that development was proceeding.

### Node 3: TGS 2025 — Public Gameplay Showing

At Tokyo Game Show 2025, Ananta showed gameplay publicly. This was the last major public appearance before cancellation rumors picked up, and it remains the main reference point most players have for the game's current shape.

### Node 4: August 25, 2026 — Returning to Gamescom ONL

Ananta is confirmed to return to **Gamescom Opening Night Live**, making a **full-circle** return to the same show three years later. Geoff Keighley described the segment as "**a fresh look**" that will "**swing into the vibrant streets**."

**This node is the most direct evidence the project is alive**: an ONL slot requires locking in scheduling well in advance, preparing broadcast-ready material, and appearing on a publicly published lineup. Cancelled projects do not make that list.

## Where Did the "NetEase Chopping Block" Rumor Come From?

### The Silence and the Anxiety

After the December 2024 rename, Ananta entered a long stretch without official updates. For a game carrying this much attention, a prolonged gap with no new footage invites worst-case assumptions.

Chinese-language communities began circulating the phrase "**网易斩杀线**" — the idea that NetEase cuts losses aggressively on projects that underperform internally. The English-language equivalent was that the game was "**on NetEase's chopping block**." That framing reflects players' existing impressions of publisher behavior; it was **not confirmation from any official or internal source**.

### The Turning Point: The Bilibili Gameplay Leak

Roughly **two weeks before the ONL lineup announcement**, **leaked gameplay footage appeared on Bilibili**. The leak showed:

- notably detailed **city blocks**;
- **near-water exploration** content.

The leak mattered because it provided circumstantial proof of active development — it depicted runnable game content rather than a concept piece. The ONL announcement that followed shortly after closed the question officially.

## Why "No Release Date" Does Not Mean "Cancelled"

This is the most commonly conflated point, so it deserves its own breakdown.

**What is actually known about timing:**

- NetEase has **not announced** an official release date;
- Analysts including **JPMorgan have suggested a 1H 2027** window.

On that second point, the distinction matters: **this is an analyst estimate, not an official date.** Analyst notes are built from outside observation and financial modeling; they do not represent NetEase's internal schedule and constitute no commitment. Treating an analyst projection as an "official delay announcement" is a frequent misreading.

**More reliable signals of project health:**

- Continued presence at major shows (Ananta: yes, ONL 2026);
- Continued release of new material (Ananta: TGS 2025 gameplay, plus the 2026 ONL showing);
- Continued commercial groundwork (Ananta: pre-registration remains open and has **surpassed 17 million**);
- Completed brand naming (Ananta: done in December 2024).

The pre-registration figure is especially telling. A publisher does not keep accumulating **17 million-plus** sign-ups for a project it plans to cancel, because unwinding that carries genuine reputational cost.

## Platforms and Scope Remain Unchanged

The shape of the project has held steady since reveal, which is itself supporting evidence of steady progress:

- **Platforms:** PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, PC, iOS, Android;
- **Pricing:** **free-to-play**;
- **Genre:** urban open-world anime RPG, widely called "anime GTA";

## FAQ

**Q: Is Ananta cancelled?**
A: No. There has been no official cancellation notice. The game is confirmed in development and is showing at Gamescom Opening Night Live on August 25, 2026.

**Q: Why do people keep saying it got axed?**
A: Because of the long silence following the December 2024 rename, which prompted "NetEase chopping block" speculation in Chinese-language communities. That was community guesswork, not official or insider information.

**Q: Has Ananta been delayed to 2027?**
A: There is no official delay announcement. JPMorgan and other analysts have suggested a 1H 2027 launch window, but that is an estimate, not an official date. NetEase still has not announced a firm release day.

**Q: Was the Bilibili gameplay leak real?**
A: Leaked footage did appear on Bilibili roughly two weeks before the ONL announcement, showing detailed city blocks and near-water exploration. It is treated as circumstantial evidence of active development.

**Q: Is my pre-registration still valid?**
A: Yes. Pre-registration remains open and has passed 17 million sign-ups; the project is not cancelled, so earlier sign-ups are unaffected. Details in the [Ananta Pre-Registration Guide](/en/ananta/pre-registration).

**Q: How can I judge whether the project is genuinely healthy?**
A: Watch four signals — continued show appearances, continued new material, continued commercial groundwork like pre-registration, and completed brand naming. Ananta currently satisfies all four.
