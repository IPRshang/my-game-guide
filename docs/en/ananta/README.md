# Ananta Game Guide

> **Ananta (formerly Project Mugen)** is NetEase's urban open-world **anime RPG** — widely nicknamed **"anime GTA."** It fuses a GTA-style city sandbox, Spider-Man-style web-swinging traversal, Persona-style city life sim, and Genshin-style open-world exploration into one neon metropolis: **Nova City**.

## What Is Ananta?

Ananta is set in **Nova City**, a coastal metropolis roughly the size of Manhattan, in a world where "urban legends become reality and humans coexist with the supernatural." You play an **Esper** investigator codenamed **Infinite Trigger**, a member of the **A.C.D. (Anti-Chaos Department)**, investigating anomalies that threaten the city's balance.

The name "Ananta" comes from Sanskrit, meaning "infinite / without end" — fitting for the limitless urban adventure of Nova City.

## Core Gameplay: Why "Anime GTA"?

### 1. The 3C Traversal System

Ananta's headline feature is movement:

- **Web-swinging** between skyscrapers like Spider-Man, carried by momentum;
- **Parkour & wall-running** with no stamina bar;
- **Vehicles** — motorcycles, cars (full physics), and swimming;
- Character-specific traversal tricks (e.g., Taffy's hammer turns into a bicycle).

### 2. A Living Open World

Nova City isn't a backdrop. You can:

- Step inside buildings;
- Play arcade minigames (claw machines, darts);
- Take photos and pet cats;
- Explore distinct districts, each with its own activities (very Persona 5).

### 3. Combat: Martial Arts + Improvised Weapons

Combat centers on **dodges, counters, and martial-arts moves**, but the weapons are wild: golf clubs, tennis rackets, sledgehammers, axes — plus rocket launchers, flamethrowers, and grenades. You can:

- **Swap between a 4-person squad** on the fly, each with its own class, combat style, and personal story;
- Call in snipers or drones for ranged support;
- Trigger **Chaos / Esper abilities** to turn the tide.

### 4. Crime System & Co-op

A **GTA-like crime system** lets you cause chaos in the city. Ananta supports **up to 4-player co-op**, but remains fundamentally single-player at heart.

## Characters: No Gacha, All Free

Ananta's most distinctive choice: **no limited-time banner gacha.** Per Famitsu/Automaton and multiple outlets, every character is obtainable through gameplay; monetization is cosmetic — outfits, vehicle skins, and housing furniture — with a battle pass under consideration.

Confirmed playable characters include **Captain, Taffy, Richie, and Seymour**, with 8 known so far and more planned. Switching characters changes both combat feel and how you interact with the city.

## Platforms & PC System Requirements

Ananta is confirmed for **PC (NetEase's own launcher, not Steam), PlayStation 5, PlayStation Cloud, iOS, and Android**. Cross-play and cross-progression are widely reported but not yet formally itemized.

## Pre-Registration & Rewards

Global pre-registration opened in December 2024 and passed **17 million** sign-ups by May 2026. The form is at `anantagame.com`. As of this writing, **NetEase has not published specific milestone reward tiers** — watch official channels for launch perks. Step-by-step: [Ananta Pre-Registration Guide](./pre-registration).

## Pricing Model: Free-to-Play, No Paywalled Characters

Ananta is **free-to-play**. Revenue comes from cosmetics (outfits, vehicle skins, furniture) and a possible battle pass. There is no "pay-to-win" wall — a more extreme take on HoYoverse's PvE-friendly economy, since all characters are free.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I have to pay to get characters in Ananta?**
A: No. All characters are earned through gameplay; payment is cosmetic only.

**Q: Will Ananta be on Steam?**
A: PC is confirmed on NetEase's own launcher; Steam/Epic are not yet announced.

**Q: Is Ananta the same as Project Mugen?**
A: Yes. Project Mugen was the 2023 codename; it was renamed Ananta (Sanskrit for "infinite") in late 2024.

**Q: Does it support controllers / cross-play?**
A: CBT1 confirmed DualSense PS5 controller support on PC. Cross-play and cross-progression are widely reported but not formally itemized.
