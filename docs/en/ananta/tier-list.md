That dissolves the usual "can't play without pulling the strong unit" pressure, and a tier list shifts from "who to pull" to "who fits my playstyle." This is a pre-launch overview; real strength follows live data after launch.**

## Why Ananta's Tier List Is Different

- Mainstream gacha open worlds (Genshin, Wuthering Waves, etc.) gate character power behind pulls. If Ananta truly has no character gacha, everyone is free, so the list no longer decides "can you get them" — only "what style do you want."
- So treat this more as a **role-orientation cheat sheet** than a "must-pull" list.

## Known Role Orientations (Preview)

> Compiled from public demos and official intros; names and kits follow the shipped version.

- **Captain**: your avatar, a member of the anomaly-control bureau (A.C.D.), can switch among several playable characters and carries the story and exploration.
- **Martial-arts roles**: combo strikes + environmental interaction, high melee burst — for players who like in-your-face damage.
- **Elemental / supernatural roles**: wield Chaos abilities (fire, lightning, etc.) — for crowd control and AoE clear.
- **Vehicle / movement roles**: boost city traversal and vehicle combat — for exploration flow.
