# When Does Ananta Launch? Release Date

**Ananta (formerly codenamed Project Mugen) launches globally on January 15, 2027 across PC, PS5, iOS and Android; it is currently in pre-registration and has passed 10 million sign-ups.**

## Launch Info at a Glance

- **Launch**: 2027-01-15 (global, simultaneous)
- **Platforms**: PC (official client / Steam), PS5, iOS, Android — cross-progression
- **Pricing**: free-to-play (F2P), **no character gacha** — all characters are obtained free, monetization is cosmetic
- **Studio**: Naked Rain (NetEase), an urban open-world action RPG set in Nova City

## Why It's Worth Watching

Ananta combines "anime-style urban open world + Spider-Man-like movement + supernatural Chaos events," and explicitly avoids gacha monetization — every character is free. For players who dislike gacha or long-term grind, it's a low-barrier entry point.
