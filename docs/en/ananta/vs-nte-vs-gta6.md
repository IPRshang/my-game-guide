# Ananta vs NTE vs GTA6: Which Urban Open World to Pick

> The "urban open world" lane got crowded in 2026. **Ananta** (NetEase, Naked Rain), **Neverness to Everness (NTE)**, and **GTA6** all drop you into a city you can freely traverse — but they differ sharply on **character acquisition, payment structure, and availability**. This page helps you choose based on your own situation.

**Reading the table:**

- **Only NTE is playable right now** (live since April 2026);
- **GTA6 has a firm date** (November 19, 2026);
- **Ananta has no official release date** — the single biggest uncertainty among the three.

## Ananta: Anime GTA, With Acquisition Still Unknown

**Developer:** NetEase's **Naked Rain** studio (Hangzhou, roughly 800 people).

**Positioning:** an urban open-world anime RPG, widely called **"anime GTA"** by players and press. You play an Esper investigator codenamed **Infinite Trigger** in the **A.C.D. (Anti-Chaos Department)**, investigating supernatural anomalies in **Nova City**. Known characters include **Captain** (A.C.D. leader), **Taffy** (Yeet squad, hammer transforms into a bicycle), **Richie** (veteran professional), and **Seymour** (hacker) — see [Ananta Characters](/en/ananta/characters).

**Confirmed:** free-to-play; five platforms (PS5 / PS4 / PC / iOS / Android); pre-registration has **surpassed 17 million**.

**Unconfirmed (important):** Japanese press reports say Ananta will have no gacha, give all characters free, and monetize via cosmetics only — but **NetEase has never officially confirmed this**. When weighing this game, **do not treat "no gacha" as settled fact**. Full breakdown: [Ananta Monetization: Confirmed vs Rumored](/en/ananta/monetization).

**Timing uncertainty:** there is no official release date. Analysts including **JPMorgan have suggested 1H 2027**, but that is an estimate, not an official window.

**Next milestone:** an appearance at Gamescom Opening Night Live on August 25, 2026 — a full-circle return to the same show where it was first revealed in 2023. See [Ananta at Gamescom 2026 Opening Night Live](/en/ananta/gamescom-2026-recap).

## NTE: The Only One You Can Play Today

**Neverness to Everness** is an urban open-world **ARPG** built around a **life sim plus combat** combination, available on PC / PS5 / mobile, and **live since April 2026**.

**Its key advantage is availability.** Of the three, NTE is the only option you can enter and keep playing right now. If you want a city to wander today, it is the only answer.

**Its key tradeoff is gacha-based character acquisition.** That means probability and spend factor into getting characters, which contrasts with Ananta's rumored model — though again, Ananta's model is unconfirmed, so the contrast today is really "known vs unknown."

More in the [Neverness to Everness Hub](/en/nte/).

## GTA6: Realistic Take, Firm Date

**GTA6** takes the **realistic urban open-world action** route, launching on PC / PS5 / consoles on **November 19, 2026**.

**Its fundamental difference from the other two is structural:** GTA6 is **single-player with no character acquisition system**. You do not collect characters, pull gacha, or think about rosters — content is delivered through the campaign itself.

**Two direct consequences:**

- **A completely different cost structure**: a premium single-player purchase versus long-term free-to-play investment;
- **A completely different art direction**: a realistic city versus anime-styled cities.

More in the [GTA6 Hub](/en/gta6/).

## Choosing by Need: Three Decision Paths

### Case 1: You Want to Play Now

**Pick NTE.** It is the only launched option (April 2026). GTA6 waits until November 19, and Ananta does not even have a date.

### Case 2: You Want a Realistic City and a Certain Date

**Pick GTA6.** Firm date (2026-11-19), realistic art, single-player, no acquisition system. If gacha mechanics or live-service operation put you off, this is the cleanest choice.

### Case 3: You Want Anime Art, Mobile Support, and Zero Entry Cost

**Watch Ananta.** It is the only anime-styled title of the three covering **iOS / Android**, and free-to-play is confirmed. But accept two uncertainties: **no official release date**, and **character acquisition not yet confirmed**.

### Platform Notes

- **Playing on mobile:** Ananta (iOS / Android) and NTE (mobile) both work; GTA6 does not cover mobile;
- **Still on PS4:** among the three, **Ananta confirms PS4 support**;
- **On PC:** all three are covered. Ananta's hardware details are in [Ananta System Requirements](/en/ananta/system-requirements).

## Competing, or Coexisting?

Worth noting: Ananta is frequently compared to **NTE** and **Wuthering Waves** (some players find Captain's look similar to Wuthering Waves' Rover). Those comparisons indicate real audience overlap.

But by schedule, the three are actually staggered: **NTE is live**, **GTA6 arrives in November**, and **Ananta has no date**. For most players this is closer to "play them in sequence" than "pick one" — especially since Ananta and NTE are both free-to-play, making trial cost minimal.

The genuine tradeoff is **time**. Live-service titles demand sustained hours, and going deep on two at once is where the real pressure sits.
