Pre-registration milestone rewards (including an exclusive outfit) are delivered in-game by mail at launch; we'll list active codes here once the game ships. Follow official sources only.**

1. Open the in-game "Settings / Redeem Code" entry (exact name per shipped version).
2. Paste the official gift code and confirm.
3. Rewards arrive in your in-game mailbox — claim them there.
