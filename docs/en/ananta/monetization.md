# Ananta Monetization: No Gacha, All Characters Free? Confirmed vs Rumored

> **Key caveat up front: "no gacha, all characters free" is a rumor, not a confirmed fact.** The claim originates from Japanese press reports, and **NetEase has never officially confirmed it**. This page keeps confirmed information and rumor strictly separate so expectation is not mistaken for conclusion.

## Why This Distinction Matters

Ananta's monetization is one of the most repeated talking points in community discussion, and repetition tends to strip away the qualifiers. A report that "Japanese press says there may be no gacha" easily mutates, after a few hops, into "NetEase announced there is no gacha."

Those two statements carry very different weight. For a game with **no announced release date**, monetization models frequently change before launch. The two tables below draw the line clearly.

## Confirmed

The following is publicly confirmed:

| Item | Confirmed detail |
|------|-----------------|
| Pricing model | **Free-to-play** |
| Platforms | PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, PC, iOS, Android |
| Pre-registration | **Surpassed 17 million** |
| Developer / publisher | NetEase, Naked Rain studio (Hangzhou, ~800 people) |
| Genre | Urban open-world anime RPG (widely called "anime GTA") |

### What "Free-to-Play" Does and Does Not Tell You

**Free-to-play is confirmed, but free-to-play says nothing about the revenue structure.** It only means downloading and entering the game costs nothing. It is compatible with any of the following:

- gacha-driven character acquisition;
- cosmetics-only monetization;
- a season pass or battle pass;
- any mix of the above.

In other words, "Ananta is free" **cannot** be used to infer "Ananta has no gacha." That inference is the single most common logical leap in community discussion of this game.

## Rumored (Not Confirmed by NetEase)

Everything in this section is **unconfirmed rumor**, sourced to Japanese press reports:

| Rumored claim | Status |
|---------------|--------|
| **No gacha system** | Rumor, unconfirmed by NetEase |
| **All characters given for free** | Rumor, unconfirmed by NetEase |
| **Monetization via cosmetics only** | Rumor, unconfirmed by NetEase |

### What the Reports Actually Say

Japanese press reports state that Ananta will have **no gacha**, that **all characters will be given to players for free**, and that **monetization will run through cosmetic items only**.

### The Qualifiers That Must Stay Attached

- **NetEase has issued no official statement on any of this**;
- it is not an official announcement, nor an official confirmation relayed through official channels;
- monetization design remains subject to change before launch;
- even if the reporting accurately reflects design intent at some development stage, that does not bind the final shipped version.

**For that reason, do not make spending decisions or platform commitments on the assumption that there is no gacha.**

## If the Rumor Holds, What Would It Mean?

This section is **hypothetical analysis** conditioned on the rumor eventually being confirmed. Treat it as scenario modeling, not description of fact.

### Impact on Players

In character-driven free-to-play games, gacha is usually the primary source of spending pressure. If Ananta genuinely drops gacha and hands out every character:

- **Spending pressure falls sharply** — no sunk-cost anxiety over failing to pull a desired character;
- **Progression decouples from payment** — character access is gated by play, not probability;
- **Payment concentrates on expression** — outfits and skins do not affect power.

### Impact on the Competitive Lane

It would create clear differentiation against comparable titles. **NTE (Neverness to Everness)**, in the same urban open-world lane, uses gacha for character acquisition, while **GTA6** is a single-player premium release. Full comparison in [Ananta vs NTE vs GTA6](/en/ananta/vs-nte-vs-gta6).

### What Would Still Deserve Scrutiny

No gacha does not mean no monetization design. Even if the rumor is confirmed, watch for:

- cosmetic pricing tiers and release cadence;
- whether any recurring pass-style purchase exists;
- whether the **acquisition pace** for free characters is stretched out, substituting time cost for money cost.

There is currently **no public information** on any of these specifics.

## How to Vet Future Monetization Claims

When new leaks surface, filter them with three questions:

**1. Is the source official or a relay?**
Official channels are the site `anantagame.com`, official social accounts, and official broadcasts. Press coverage is secondhand and should carry "reportedly."

**2. Were qualifiers dropped?**
"Japanese press reports there may be no gacha" and "Ananta has no gacha" are claims of very different strength. When qualifiers vanish in transmission, discount the confidence accordingly.

**3. Does it distinguish test builds from launch?**
Beta design is not shipped design. Ananta ran a China-region PC closed beta in January 2026, but test-period design is not a launch commitment.
