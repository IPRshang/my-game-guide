## 📊 Trinket System Overview

Trinkets are one of the core equipment systems in Black Myth. You can equip **up to 3 trinkets** simultaneously, providing various passive bonuses.

| Category | Count | Acquisition |
|----------|:-----:|-------------|
| Attack Trinkets | 12 | Boss drops / Shop |
| Defense Trinkets | 10 | Chests / Hidden areas |
| Utility Trinkets | 14 | Side quests / Exploration / NPCs |
| Rare Trinkets | 12 | Hidden Bosses / True ending |
| **Total** | **48** | |

> 💡 **Trinket Principle**: Pick core trinket based on build → Add survival → Utility as needed

---

## ⚔️ S-Tier Trinkets (Must-Have)

### 1. 【Wind-Calming Pearl】
- **Effect**: Greatly reduces wind damage, immune to some knockback effects
- **Location**: Yellow Wind Sage Boss drop
- **Best for**: All builds
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Essential for Yellow Wind Ridge, useful late-game against knockback

### 2. 【Amber Light Armor】
- **Effect**: Below 30% HP, defense increases by 50%
- **Location**: Chapter 2 hidden chest
- **Best for**: Aggressive builds / Stance builds
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Low HP comeback god-tier, pairs great with healing items

### 3. 【Miraculous Gourd】
- **Effect**: Healing item effectiveness +30%
- **Location**: Earth God shop (Chapter 2 unlock)
- **Best for**: All builds
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: New player god-send, massively improves survivability

### 4. 【Golden Eyes Talisman】
- **Effect**: After perfect parry, attack +20% for 5 seconds
- **Location**: Erlang Shen side quest reward
- **Best for**: Skill players / Slash builds
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Parry build core, experts get insane damage

### 5. 【Nine-Revolution Golden Pill】
- **Effect**: Auto-revive every 60 seconds (once per battle)
- **Location**: True ending reward
- **Best for**: All builds
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Strongest trinket period, extra life per battle

## 🗺️ Collection Route Recommendation

### Chapter 1 (Black Wind Mountain)
1. Shop buys: Wolf Fang Pendant, Stamina Vessel, Treasure Compass
2. Bullguard drop: Bloodthirsty Jade
3. Hidden chest: Crit Stone
4. Black Wind King drop: Armor-Piercing Talisman

### Chapter 2 (Yellow Wind Ridge)
1. Earth God shop: Miraculous Gourd, Longevity Jade
2. Yellow Wind Sage drop: Wind-Calming Pearl
3. Hidden area: Regen Stone
4. Side quest: Dodge Ring

### Chapter 3 (Little Western Heaven)
1. Elemental bosses drop corresponding trinkets
2. Hidden boss: Mirror Flower Moon
3. Rare wine ingredients abundant

### Chapter 4 (Spider Ridge)
1. Late game bosses drop advanced trinkets
2. True ending path gives ultimate trinkets
