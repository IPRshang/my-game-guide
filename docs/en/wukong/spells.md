# Black Myth Wukong: Complete Spells & Transformations

Black Myth Wukong's combat revolves around **Spells** and **Transformations**. This guide covers every spell — how to get them, upgrade effects, combat applications, and optimal loadouts for each build.

## Spell Overview

The game features **7 core spells** + **4 transformations**:

### Core Spells

| Spell | Type | Chapter | Mana Cost | Core Function |
|-------|:----:|:-------:|:---------:|---------------|
| Immobilize | Control | Ch.1 | Medium | Freeze enemies, create damage windows |
| Cloud Step | Mobility | Ch.2 | Low | Transform into mist for rapid movement |
| Ring of Fire | Defense | Ch.2 | Medium | Draw a protective circle |
| Rock Solid | Defense | Ch.3 | High | Temporary super-armor |
| Pluck of Many | Summon | Ch.3 | Very High | Summon clones to fight |
| Spell Binder | Utility | Ch.4 | Low | Silence zone, disable spells |
| Life-Saving Strand | Survival | Ch.5 | High | Auto-trigger near-death heal |

### Transformations

| Transformation | Chapter | Continuous Cost | Core Function |
|---------------|:-------:|:---------------:|---------------|
| Red Tides (Blade Wolf) | Ch.1 | Might | High damage + burn DoT |
| Azure Dust (Stone Spirit) | Ch.2 | Might | Defense form, CC immune |
| Ebony Stream (Insect) | Ch.3 | Might | Flight & terrain bypass |
| Umbral Moon (Great Sage) | Ch.4 | Might | Ultimate form, massive stat boost |

## Spell Details

### 1. Immobilize

> Obtained: Ch.1 Black Wind Mountain — acquired after defeating Guangzhi

The most versatile control spell. Nearly every build should include it.

| Level | Duration | Mana | Bonus |
|:-----:|:--------:|:----:|-------|
| Lv.1 | 3s | 45 | — |
| Lv.2 | 4.5s | 40 | First hit on frozen target +10% damage |
| Lv.3 | 6s | 35 | Unlocks "Freeze Burst": AoE explosion on expiry |

**Usage:**
- Use when a boss winds up a big attack — interrupt and get 6s of free damage
- Combo with charged heavy: Immobilize → charge to 3 focus → devastating smash
- **Does NOT work on**: Erlang Shen (immune to control), some late-game bosses

**Best with:**
- Crit builds → Immobilize + 3-focus heavy = instant burst
- Caster builds → Immobilize + Pluck of Many + spell spam

---

### 2. Cloud Step

> Obtained: Ch.2 Yellow Wind Ridge — main story

Unmatched mobility. A favorite among skilled players.

| Level | Duration | Speed | Bonus |
|:-----:|:--------:|:-----:|-------|
| Lv.1 | 3s | 150% | — |
| Lv.2 | 4s | 175% | Minor damage when passing through enemies |
| Lv.3 | 5s | 200% | Full invincibility in mist form |

**Usage:**
- Dash behind a boss, exit mist, backstab immediately
- Best evasion for arena-wide AoE attacks (Lv.3 full immunity)
- Traverse poison swamps and fire hazards during exploration
- Kite pattern: Cloud Step → reposition → Immobilize → damage → repeat

---

### 3. Ring of Fire

> Obtained: Ch.2 Yellow Wind Ridge — hidden area after Yellow Wind Sage

Draw a protective circle — invincible inside.

| Level | Radius | Duration | Bonus |
|:-----:|:------:|:--------:|-------|
| Lv.1 | 3m | 5s | Damage immunity inside |
| Lv.2 | 4m | 7s | Minor HP regen per second |
| Lv.3 | 5m | 10s | +15% attack inside |

**Usage:**
- Pop during boss rage phases for safe damage
- Combine with Pluck of Many — clones tank outside while you attack in safety
- At Lv.3, safe drinking/healing and charging inside the ring
- **Warning**: Some boss grab attacks can pull you out

---

### 4. Rock Solid

> Obtained: Ch.3 New West — after defeating Yellowbrow

Activate diamond body — the tank's best friend.

| Level | Duration | DR | Bonus |
|:-----:|:--------:|:--:|-------|
| Lv.1 | 4s | 50% | — |
| Lv.2 | 6s | 65% | Reflect partial melee damage |
| Lv.3 | 8s | 80% | Super armor: immune to knockback/stagger |

**Usage:**
- Excellent against rapid-attack bosses (Guangzhi, Venom Daoist)
- Lv.3 super armor lets you trade hits and win
- Combo: Rock Solid → eat damage → 3-focus heavy counter
- **Weak against**: Magic-damage bosses (DR only applies to physical)

---

### 5. Pluck of Many

> Obtained: Ch.3 New West — main story (story-critical spell)

Summon clones. The flashiest spell in the game.

| Level | Clones | Duration | Bonus |
|:-----:|:------:|:--------:|-------|
| Lv.1 | 1 | 10s | Clones draw aggro |
| Lv.2 | 2 | 15s | Clones use basic staff attacks |
| Lv.3 | 3 | 20s | Clones inherit your gear stats |

**Usage:**
- Summon at battle start — clones draw boss attention
- Lv.3 clone gear inheritance doubles effectiveness with crit gear
- Combo: Clones attack → you charge → Immobilize → heavy smash finisher
- Extremely mana-hungry (120 per cast) — needs mana gear support

---

### 6. Spell Binder

> Obtained: Ch.4 Webbed Ridge — after Violet Spider, in Hundred-Eye Cave

A strategic spell with unique boss applications.

| Level | Radius | Duration | Bonus |
|:-----:|:------:|:--------:|-------|
| Lv.1 | 5m | 6s | Silence enemy spells |
| Lv.2 | 8m | 9s | All spells disabled in zone (yours too) |
| Lv.3 | 12m | 12s | Dispels enemy buffs |

**Usage:**
- Essential for Hundred-Eye Daoist — prevents his arena-wide poison mist
- Excellent against caster bosses (Yellowbrow, Violet Spider)
- **Warning Lv.2 double-edged**: Cast your own spells BEFORE using Spell Binder
- Nearly useless against pure physical bosses

---

### 7. Life-Saving Strand

> Obtained: Ch.5 Flaming Mountains — after defeating Bull Demon King

The ultimate safety net. Beginner-friendly.

| Level | Trigger HP | Heal | Cooldown | Bonus |
|:-----:|:----------:|:----:|:--------:|-------|
| Lv.1 | <15% | 30% | 180s | — |
| Lv.2 | <20% | 45% | 150s | +20% damage for 5s after trigger |
| Lv.3 | <25% | 60% | 120s | Auto-cast Immobilize on trigger |

**Usage:**
- New players' boss insurance policy
- Lv.3 auto-Immobilize gives time to heal + counterattack
- Skip this for no-hit challenge runs (wasted slot)

## Transformation Details

### Red Tides (Guangzhi)

> Obtained: Ch.1 — defeat Guangzhi, claim his spirit

- Attacks apply burn DoT
- Fast light attacks for combo builds
- Heavy attack: flame wave AoE

**Best used against:** Ice-weak bosses (Kang-Jin Loong), grouped trash mobs

---

### Azure Dust (Stone Vanguard)

> Obtained: Ch.2 — defeat Stone Vanguard

- Extreme defense, immune to knockback and CC
- Slow movement and attack speed
- Heavy attack: earthquake AoE + knockdown

**Best used against:** High-attack-speed bosses, crowd situations

---

### Ebony Stream (Insect)

> Obtained: Ch.3 — hidden area

- Full flight capability, ignores terrain
- Tiny hitbox, extremely hard to hit
- No attacks — pure utility form

**Best used for:** Narrow passages, hidden areas, dodging arena-wide AoE, fast traversal

---

### Umbral Moon (Great Sage True Form)

> Obtained: Ch.4 — main story

- All stats massively boosted (+50% ATK/DEF/SPD)
- Extremely high Might cost, short duration
- Unlocks exclusive staff techniques

**Best used for:** Boss finishing move (<30% HP), Erlang Shen emergency button

| Priority | Spell | Reason |
|:--------:|-------|--------|
| S | Immobilize | Most versatile: rush to Lv.3 ASAP |
| S | Pluck of Many | Highest DPS spell: Lv.3 game-changer |
