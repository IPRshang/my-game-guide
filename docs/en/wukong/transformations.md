# Black Myth: Wukong Transformations Guide

**Transformations in Black Myth: Wukong let you temporarily take the form of a defeated enemy or boss, gaining their moveset and a separate health pool. They cost mana (or a cooldown resource) and last a limited time, making them a tactical burst rather than a permanent state.**

## How the system works

- You unlock transformations by **defeating specific enemies or bosses** and acquiring their form.
- Activating a transformation **replaces your moveset** with that form's attacks.
- The transformation has its **own health pool** — damage depletes it, and when it runs out you revert.
- Duration and cost vary by form; stronger forms cost more and last differently.

## When transforming pays off

1. **Against groups** — many forms have wide sweeps that clear mobs faster than your staff.
2. **As a defensive reset** — the separate health pool effectively acts as a second life bar.
3. **To exploit elemental matchups** — some forms carry elemental damage that a boss is weak to.
4. **To break poise** — heavy forms stagger bosses that shrug off light attacks.

## When not to transform

- During tight boss patterns that you have already learned — your own moveset is often more efficient.
- When mana is needed for survival spells instead.
- Right as a boss is about to change phase — you may waste the duration.

## Tips

- **Transform before the fight escalates**, not at low health, to make the most of the extra pool.
- **Upgrade forms** where possible — damage scales with progression.
- **Experiment at shrines** — you can swap which transformation you have equipped.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I get more transformations?**
A: By defeating certain bosses and enemies, and in some cases by completing side content.

**Q: Do transformations cost health?**
A: They cost mana or a cooldown resource, and come with their own separate health pool.

**Q: Is one transformation best?**
A: No single form dominates — the best one depends on whether you need crowd control, burst or survivability.
