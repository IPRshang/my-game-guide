# Black Myth Wukong — Boss Strategy Guide

> Complete Boss Walkthrough | Hidden Bosses Included

## Chapter 1: Black Wind Mountain

### Wolf Scout (Tutorial Boss)
- **Difficulty**: ★☆☆☆☆
- **HP**: Low
- **Moves**: Triple claw swipe, lunging bite
- **Strategy**: Dodge → 3 light attacks → dodge loop. Watch for the lunge windup (head lowered, charging)
- **Reward**: Wolfskin Bracers

### Lingxuzi (Chapter Boss)
- **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆
- **HP**: Medium
- **Moves**: Claw combos, roar shockwave, wolf pack summon
- **Strategy**:
  1. Use Immobilize to interrupt the roar
  2. Clear wolves with staff spin
  3. Enrages at 50% HP — moves faster, keep distance
- **Reward**: Claw of Lingxuzi (weapon material)

## Chapter 2: Yellow Wind Ridge

### Yellow Wind Demon
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆
- **HP**: High
- **Moves**: Triple wind blades, sandstorm tornado, stealth thrust, falling boulders
- **Strategy**:
  1. **Critical**: Dodge the sandstorm tornado (windup: both arms raised 1.5s)
  2. Parry stealth thrust with Rock Solid
  3. Below 30% HP: consecutive sandstorms — save dodge stamina
- **Reward**: Yellow Wind Pearl (-30% wind damage)

## Chapter 3: Minor Western Paradise

### Bu Bai
- **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆
- **Strategy**: Fast-paced boss. Pressure with light attacks, use Immobilize whenever available.

### Yellow Brow (Hidden Boss)
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★
- **Trigger**: 100% zone exploration in Minor Western Paradise
- **Moves**: Human Sack (instant kill!), Golden Cymbal trap, Buddha's Radiance, Diamond Palm
- **Strategy**:
  1. Human Sack 3s windup — must interrupt or escape AoE
  2. Break Golden Cymbal with transformations
  3. P2 at 50% HP: adds Diamond Palm chains
- **Reward**: Hou Tian Bag (permanent +15% damage)

## Chapter 4: Spider Silk Cave

### Purple Spider
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆
- **HP**: Extremely high
- **Poison-based attacks throughout — bring antidote pills**

## Final Chapter

### Great Sage's Broken Shell
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★★
- **Three Phase Battle**:
  - P1 (100%-60%): Classic moveset — practice phase
  - P2 (60%-30%): Adds clones and AoE attacks
  - P3 (30%-0%): Full-screen ultimates + self-heal, extremely short damage windows
- **Key**: Save transformation and Immobilize for P3 burst

> Struggling? Go collect better gear and pills first. Stats are part of the game too.
