# Black Myth Wukong — Boss Strategy Guide

Most boss lists for this game just name the enemy and say "dodge a lot." That's useless. What actually wins fights here is *knowing which window to punish* — the red-glow tells you a guard-break is coming, and Immobilize only matters if you've saved the Mana for it. If you're still picking a combat direction, our [Wukong builds guide](/en/wukong/builds/) maps the four stances to specific boss matchups, and the [spell breakdown](/en/wukong/spells/) covers the Mana economy behind every Immobilize opener.

> **Common misconception we see constantly:** players think the hardest boss is the one with the most stars. It usually isn't — Yellow Brow (★★★★★) is gated behind 100% exploration, while the *Great Sage's Broken Shell* (★★★★★★) is the real skill check because its P3 damage windows are brutally short. Read phases, don't count stars.

## Chapter 1: Black Wind Mountain

### Bullguard (牯护院)
- **Difficulty**: ★☆☆☆☆
- **Type**: Yaoguai King
- **Notes**: The **first mandatory boss** in the game — a gatekeeper you cannot skip. Mid-fight you unlock Immobilize (定身术).
- **Strategy**: Learn the basic dodge + light attack + Immobilize combo. A training boss.
- **Reward**: Unlocks Immobilize spell

> **Our call:** don't rush past Bullguard. It's a tutorial disguised as a boss — the dodge→light→Immobilize loop you learn here is the exact rhythm every later fight demands. Master it here while the stakes are zero.

### Guangzhi (广智) ※ First Optional Yaoguai Chief
- **Difficulty**: ★★☆☆☆
- **Location**: Forest of Wolves / Outside the Forest
- **Notes**: The first optional Yaoguai Chief. Defeating him grants your first Transformation ability.
- **Strategy**: His dual-blade attacks have wide reach. Observe his patterns before closing in.
- **Reward**: Red Tides (赤潮) Transformation spell

> **Our call:** grab Guangzhi early. The Red Tides transformation is your first real "panic button" — when a later boss overwhelms you, transforming resets aggro and buys a full HP bar's worth of breathing room.

## Chapter 2: Yellow Wind Ridge

### Yellow Wind Demon
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆
- **HP**: High
- **Moves**: Triple wind blades, sandstorm tornado, stealth thrust, falling boulders
- **Strategy**:
  1. **Critical**: Dodge the sandstorm tornado (windup: both arms raised 1.5s)
  2. Parry stealth thrust with Rock Solid
  3. Below 30% HP: consecutive sandstorms — save dodge stamina
- **Reward**: Yellow Wind Pearl (-30% wind damage)

> **Our call:** the 1.5s arm-raise is the entire fight. If you can read that tell, the tornado is free i-frames; if you can't, you'll burn your whole stamina bar panicking. The [collectibles guide](/en/wukong/collectibles/) explains the Wind-Calming Pearl that makes this chapter trivial on replay.

## Chapter 3: Minor Western Paradise

### Bu Bai
- **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆
- **Strategy**: Fast-paced boss. Pressure with light attacks, use Immobilize whenever available.

### Yellow Brow (Hidden Boss)
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★
- **Trigger**: 100% zone exploration in Minor Western Paradise
- **Moves**: Human Sack (instant kill!), Golden Cymbal trap, Buddha's Radiance, Diamond Palm
- **Strategy**:
  1. Human Sack 3s windup — must interrupt or escape AoE
  2. Break Golden Cymbal with transformations
  3. P2 at 50% HP: adds Diamond Palm chains
- **Reward**: Hou Tian Bag (permanent +15% damage)

> **Our call:** two things trip people up — (1) they never trigger him because they skipped exploration, and (2) they eat the Human Sack because they didn't learn the 3s tell. Both are avoidable with preparation, not reflexes. The +15% damage bag is permanent, so the exploration tax is worth it.

## Chapter 4: Spider Silk Cave

### Purple Spider
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆
- **HP**: Extremely high
- **Poison-based attacks throughout — bring antidote pills**

> **Our call:** "bring antidote pills" is the whole tip and players still ignore it. Poison here isn't a nuisance — it's a stamina/HP tax that compounds over an extremely long HP bar. Pre-brew Healing and Antidote pills at a shrine before entering; the [collectibles & pills guide](/en/wukong/collectibles/) lists exactly what to craft.

## Final Chapter

### Great Sage's Broken Shell
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★★
- **Three Phase Battle**:
  - P1 (100%-60%): Classic moveset — practice phase
  - P2 (60%-30%): Adds clones and AoE attacks
  - P3 (30%-0%): Full-screen ultimates + self-heal, extremely short damage windows
- **Key**: Save transformation and Immobilize for P3 burst

> **Our call:** the mistake is hoarding everything for P3 and dying in P1. Use a transformation to survive P2's clone swarm if you're low — you can still clear P3 with Immobilize + one transformation if you've farmed gear. Stats are a real part of this fight; if P3 feels impossible, level and reforge first.

> Struggling? Go collect better gear and pills first. Stats are part of the game too.

## Common Boss Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Skipping Bullguard's lesson.** Players treat it as a pushover and never internalize dodge→Immobilize. Fix: deliberately practice the loop; it's the foundation of every later fight.
2. **Burning Immobilize on trash.** Mana is finite and recovers slowly without wine. Fix: save Immobilize for boss phase openings and guard-breaks, not mobs.
3. **Missing Yellow Brow entirely.** He only appears at 100% zone exploration. Fix: clear every side path in Minor Western Paradise before progressing.
4. **Hoarding transformations for a phase you never reach.** Fix: use a transformation to survive an earlier phase when low — a life now beats a perfect P3 you don't live to see.

## FAQ

**What's the universal opener against most bosses?** Immobilize → heavy-staff combo. It freezes the enemy, lets you land a charged heavy, and is the safest way to open any non-resistant fight.

**How do I handle the Yellow Wind Demon tornado?** Watch for both arms raised (1.5s windup) and dodge through it — that window is your free punish opportunity. Below 30% HP the tornadoes come back-to-back, so conserve dodge stamina.

**The Spider Cave poison is killing me — what do I do?** Brew Antidote and Healing pills at a shrine before entering; Purple Spider's poison is a sustained HP/stamina tax, not a one-off. See the [collectibles guide](/en/wukong/collectibles/) for pill recipes.

**The Great Sage's Broken Shell P3 feels impossible — am I underleveled?** Possibly. P3 has full-screen ultimates and self-heal with very short damage windows; if you can't burst it, farm gear, reforge at a shrine, and return. This is intended stat progression, not a skill wall alone.

**Where do I go after clearing the bosses?** Plan your full run around a build — our [builds guide](/en/wukong/builds/), [staff stances](/en/wukong/staff-stances/), and [hidden secrets](/en/wukong/secrets/) pages cover loadouts, combat style, and the true-ending path.
