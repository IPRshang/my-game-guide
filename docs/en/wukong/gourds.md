# Black Myth: Wukong Gourds Guide

**Your gourd is Black Myth: Wukong's primary healing item — it holds a set number of sips that refill when you rest at a shrine. What changes most is not the healing itself but the drinks and medicines you soak into it, which add effects like resistance, mana recovery or temporary buffs.**

## The basics

- Sip from the gourd to **recover health**; you get a limited number of sips.
- **Resting at a shrine (Keeper's Shrine) refills** your gourd at no cost.
- The gourd's **capacity can be increased** as you progress and collect upgrade materials.

## Drinks and medicines

- **Drinks** alter what the gourd does when you sip — stronger healing, gradual recovery, or added effects.
- **Medicines** (soaked ingredients) add resistances or stat benefits for a duration.
- You can swap your loadout at a shrine, so experiment against different bosses.

## Upgrade path

1. Find the **gourd upgrade NPC** (often encountered at shrines as you progress).
2. Spend the required materials to raise **capacity** or **potency**.
3. Prioritise capacity early — more sips is generally better than stronger sips.

## Practical tips

- **Sip early in a fight**, not at low health — the animation leaves you vulnerable.
- **Match the drink to the boss** — resistance drinks against elemental bosses make a real difference.
- **Do not hoard sips** — resting is free, so use them liberally during exploration.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I get more healing?**
A: Upgrade gourd capacity through the relevant NPC using materials found while exploring.

**Q: Do sips cost anything?**
A: No — resting at a shrine refills them for free, so there is no economy to manage.

**Q: Are drinks worth swapping between bosses?**
A: Yes. Elemental resistances and status cures can decide the hardest fights.
