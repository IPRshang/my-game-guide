## I. Game Background

Black Myth: Wukong is based on the classic Chinese novel *Journey to the West*, but tells a story set **after** the pilgrimage. You play the "Destined One" — a monkey who sets out to retrieve the remains of Sun Wukong (the Monkey King) and the "Five Skandhas" (relics of his power).

Game Science scanned and modeled real Chinese ancient architecture and artifacts, faithfully recreating a mythological Chinese atmosphere in art and music. As of 2026, global sales have surpassed **30 million copies**, making it the benchmark for domestic Chinese single-player games. A sequel, *Black Myth: Zhong Kui*, was announced in 2025 (early development).

> This guide defaults to **no spoilers** of main-story plots or boss real names — only mechanics and combat approaches.

## II. Core Mechanics

Black Myth blends "Soulslike + Journey to the West transformations" into an action game. Core systems:

- **Staff Stances (four)**: Smash Stance (fast attacks), Pillar Stance (anti-group / low guard), Thrust Stance (pierce & break guard), and a special derivative. Hold the attack button to charge, release for the corresponding stance.
- **Spell system**: e.g. *Immobilize* (freezes enemy — key to breaking guard), *A Pluck of Many* (clone harassment), *Cloud Step* (escape / reposition), *Rock Solid*, consuming Mana — paired with "sobering / wine" to recover.
- **Transformations**: defeating certain Yaoguai kings grants transformation forms with new movesets and HP — a key tool to interrupt boss ultimate attacks.
- **Wine & Pills**: drinks give sustained healing / buffs; pills are instant consumables — configure before boss fights.
- **Bestiary (Ying Shen Tu)**: defeating enemies drops "Spirit Essence" and fills the codex; completing it unlocks backstory and stat bonuses — both collection and progression.
- **Shrine (land temple)**: doubles as save point, stat allocation, equipment reforge, and respec (costs "Nine-Turn Golden Pill").

## III. Beginner Tips

1. **Pick a build direction first**: no need to overthink at start, but check [Builds](./builds) for a main direction (Smash / Pillar / Spell / Transform).
2. **Learn "See-Through" (perfect dodge/parry)**: the essence of Wukong isn't trading blows — it's watching the enemy's startup and precisely dodging or parrying; hitting the weak point breaks poise.
3. **Don't get greedy**: when enemies swing wide, back off — survive over dealing extra hits, especially boss phase two.
4. **Prioritize "Root · Vitality" (HP)**: early points into HP and defense give far more fault tolerance than pure offense.
5. **Use Immobilize**: against most bosses, Immobilize → heavy-staff combo is the universal opener.
6. **Return to shrines often**: leveling, respec, and gear are all at shrines — if stuck, adjust your Build there before retrying.
7. **Collect the Bestiary**: clearing trash mobs on the way both farms essence and unlocks strong passives.

## IV. Advanced Tips & Strategy

- **Full-charge four-stance**: Smash Stance full charge hits extremely hard; paired with "no whiff heavy" curios can one-shot mobs and break boss poise.
- **Spellcaster Build**: centered on "Immobilize + A Pluck of Many + Cloud Step," with high Mana cap and Mana-regen wine — good for less-skilled players but needs rhythm practice.
- **Weak-point attacks**: most bosses have breakable parts or specific guard-break windows; watch their red-glow / charge animations and punish.
- **Transformation as panic button**: when overwhelmed, transform to reset aggro and tank hits — a lifeline for both escape and counter.
- **NG+ (New Game+)**: carries gear and level; enemies are tougher but drops better — the path to full completion and true ending.
- **Hidden bosses / side quests**: some Yaoguai kings only appear via side quests or specific actions — see [Hidden Secrets](./secrets).

## FAQ

**Q1: The game is too hard — is there an easy mode?**
A: No traditional difficulty option, but stacking HP + Immobilize flow + summon curios (e.g. Bronze Head/Iron Arm) can greatly lower the bar; or wait for later balance patches.

**Q2: Is the DLC out?**
A: As of August 2026, the **major paid DLC is still in development**; the studio has repeatedly debunked rumored dates (e.g. June / September / a ¥98 price — all false), with a general expectation of **H1 2027**. The base game already received a free "Boss Rush" mode, chapter maps, and new armor.

**Q3: Can Xbox play it?**
A: Yes. The Xbox Series X|S version launched in 2025, content-identical to PS5 / PC.

**Q4: How many endings? How to unlock the true ending?**
A: The base game has multiple ending branches; the true ending requires specific collection / side-quest conditions — see [Hidden Secrets](./secrets) (mild spoiler hints included).

**Q5: Does it need internet?**
A: Fully playable offline single-player; no internet required. Cloud saves depend on platform.

**Q6: What Build do you recommend for beginners?**
A: A steady "Smash Stance + Immobilize + high HP" combo — see [Builds](./builds).
