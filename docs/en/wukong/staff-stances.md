# Black Myth: Wukong Staff Stances Guide

**Black Myth: Wukong gives you three staff stances — Smash (劈), Pillar (立) and Thrust (戳). Each changes how you build and spend focus, how your heavy attacks behave, and how safely you can deal damage. Switching stances mid-fight is the core of the combat system.**

## The three stances

| Stance | Character | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Smash** | Charged overhead heavy attacks that consume focus for big damage | Burst damage after a dodge window |
| **Pillar** | Stand on the staff to avoid ground attacks, then slam down | Evading sweeps and repositioning |
| **Thrust** | Long-range pokes that build focus safely | Keeping distance and chipping safely |

## How focus ties in

- Light attacks **build focus**; heavy attacks **spend** it.
- Different stances generate and spend focus at different rates.
- Spending focus at the right moment — usually after a successful dodge — is where most of your damage comes from.

## When to switch

1. **Open with Thrust** to build focus from a safe distance.
2. **Switch to Smash** for a heavy punish after dodging a boss combo.
3. **Use Pillar** when a boss spams low sweeps or you need to avoid ground hazards.
4. **Return to Thrust** while waiting for the next opening.

## Tips

- Do not stay in one stance all fight — stance switching is the intended damage loop.
- Pillar is more than a novelty; it hard-counters certain ground-based attacks.
- Practise the timing of heavy attacks, not just the stance choice — timing determines whether you land or get punished.

## FAQ

**Q: Which stance is best?**
A: None universally — Thrust is safest, Smash hits hardest, Pillar is the most defensive. Good play rotates between them.

**Q: Can I switch stances freely?**
A: Yes, stance switching is available at any time during combat.

**Q: Does stance affect focus gain?**
A: Yes, each stance builds and consumes focus differently, which is why switching matters.
