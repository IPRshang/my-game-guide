# Black Myth Wukong — Hidden Content & Secrets

> Miss these and you might skip 50% of the game

## True Ending Unlock

**Requirements (all 6):**
1. ✅ Defeat all hidden bosses (Yellow Brow, Centipede Demon, Roc Demon)
2. ✅ Collect all 10 Scriptures
3. ✅ Speak with Subhuti 3 times (end of Ch.2/4/6)
4. ✅ Complete Guanyin side quest
5. ✅ Don't destroy the Flower-Fruit Mountain stele
6. ✅ Choose "Let Go" before the final battle

**True Ending Content**: Wukong is freed from the golden headband, becomes the true Great Sage Equal to Heaven. Post-credits: 8-minute hidden cinematic.

## Complete Collection Guide

### 10 Scripture Locations
1. Black Wind Mountain — Guanyin Temple main hall
2. Yellow Wind Ridge — Deepest desert cave
3. Minor Western Paradise — After defeating Yellow Brow
4. Spider Silk Cave — Web-covered chamber (need torch)
5. Lion Camel Ridge — Dropped by Roc Demon
6. Flaming Mountain — Gift from Princess Iron Fan (side quest)
7. Celestial Court — Alchemy Lab secret room
8. Flower-Fruit Mountain — Behind Water Curtain Cave stele
9. Underworld — After defeating King Yan
10. Thunderclap Monastery — Automatic after clearing game

### 7 Ruyi Jingu Bang Fragments
- Collect all 7 to reforge the **True Ruyi Jingu Bang** at the Celestial Court blacksmith (doubles attack power)

## Side Quests

### "Guanyin's Request" (Required)
- Triggers in Chapter 2, spans the entire game
- **Reward**: Vase Nectar (one resurrection per battle)
- **Impact**: Required for the true ending

### "Zhu Bajie's Troubles"
- Triggers in Chapter 4
- Help Bajie find food (3 hidden ingredients)
- **Reward**: Nine-Toothed Rake skin + Bajie transformation
