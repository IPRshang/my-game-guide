## Ending 1: The Devil (Arasaka Route)

**Trigger**: Final choice: "Trust Arasaka, let Hanako help you"

**Path**:
1. Enter Arasaka Tower with Hanako's help
2. Defeat Adam Smasher
3. Undergo Soulkiller surgery

**Outcome**: V's consciousness is preserved, but awaits a compatible host from Arasaka. Hanako takes control of the corporation, but her father takes over as a digital engram.

**Verdict**: The most frustrating ending. V becomes a corporate lab rat.

## Ending 2: The Sun (Rogue Route)

**Trigger**: Choose "Let Rogue go with you"
→ Requires completing Rogue's side quest chain

**Outcome**: V becomes a Night City legend, owns the Afterlife bar. But only 6 months to live. Final mission: heist the Crystal Palace casino in space.

**Verdict**: The most "V" ending. Night City legends never die.

## Ending 3: The Star (Panam Route) ⭐ Recommended

**Trigger**: Choose "Call Panam for help"
→ Requires completing Panam's side quests and romance

**Outcome**: V leaves Night City with Panam and the Aldecaldos. Crossing the desert, searching for a cure. An open ending full of hope.

**Verdict**: The warmest ending. V is no longer alone.

## Ending 4: Temperance (Johnny Route)

**Trigger**: Choose "Let Johnny and Rogue go" + In cyberspace, choose "Johnny stays"

**Outcome**: Johnny controls V's body. V's consciousness remains forever in cyberspace. Johnny buys a guitar and leaves Night City for a fresh start.

**Verdict**: The most emotionally complex ending.

## Ending 5: Path of Least Resistance

**Trigger**: On the rooftop, choose "It all ends here"

> Not recommended. Johnny's reaction is heartbreaking.

## Ending 6: The Tower (Phantom Liberty Exclusive)

**Trigger**: Side with Reed in the DLC main quest → Accept NUSA surgery

**Outcome**: The chip is removed. V survives — but was in a coma for 2 years. Waking up, all friends have moved on. V is now "ordinary," permanently unable to use cyberware.

**Verdict**: You survived. But you lost everything.

## FAQ

**Q: How many endings does Cyberpunk 2077 have?** A: 5 base endings + 1 secret ending ("Don't Fear The Reaper") + 1 Phantom Liberty ending ("The Tower") = 7 total across all content.

**Q: How do you get the secret/hidden ending?** A: Reach 70%+ relationship with Johnny, then on the rooftop pick the new dialogue option to call Takemura. This unlocks "Don't Fear The Reaper", a solo suicide-run through Arasaka Tower.

**Q: Which Cyberpunk ending is the best or canon?** A: There is no canon ending. "The Star" (Panam) is the most hopeful; "The Tower" (Phantom Liberty) is the only one where V survives, but loses everything.

**Q: Does Phantom Liberty add a new ending?** A: Yes — "The Tower". Side with Reed and accept the NUSA surgery to have the biochip removed; V lives but loses 2 years and all connections.

**Q: Can you get all endings in one playthrough?** A: No. Endings branch on final and DLC choices, but you can reload saves or replay the choice via the "one more gig" epilogue.
