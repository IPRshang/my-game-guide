# Cyberpunk 2077 — All Endings Guide

> Phantom Liberty DLC endings included | **HEAVY SPOILER WARNING**

Most "all endings" pages treat the rooftop as a free menu of buttons. That framing loses the actual game: in Cyberpunk 2077 your ending is *decided across the whole playthrough* — by who you romanced, which gigs you closed, and a quiet Johnny-affinity meter almost nobody watches. If you only start thinking about the outcome at the final mission, the choices available to you have already collapsed to one or two.

> **Common misconception we keep seeing:** that the ending is a free pick at the end. It isn't. "The Star" needs Panam's full quest line *and* her romance; the secret "Don't Fear The Reaper" needs 70%+ Johnny affinity built up across the entire game. Choose your target ending before Act 2, not after — your [build](/en/cyberpunk/builds/) and [romance](/en/cyberpunk/romance/) plan should serve it.

## Ending 1: The Devil (Arasaka Route)

**Trigger**: Final choice: "Trust Arasaka, let Hanako help you"

**Path**:
1. Enter Arasaka Tower with Hanako's help
2. Defeat Adam Smasher
3. Undergo Soulkiller surgery

**Outcome**: V's consciousness is preserved, but awaits a compatible host from Arasaka. Hanako takes control of the corporation, but her father takes over as a digital engram.

**Verdict**: The most frustrating ending. V becomes a corporate lab rat.

## Ending 2: The Sun (Rogue Route)

**Trigger**: Choose "Let Rogue go with you"
→ Requires completing Rogue's side quest chain

**Outcome**: V becomes a Night City legend, owns the Afterlife bar. But only 6 months to live. Final mission: heist the Crystal Palace casino in space.

**Verdict**: The most "V" ending. Night City legends never die.

## Ending 3: The Star (Panam Route) ⭐ Recommended

**Trigger**: Choose "Call Panam for help"
→ Requires completing Panam's side quests and romance

**Outcome**: V leaves Night City with Panam and the Aldecaldos. Crossing the desert, searching for a cure. An open ending full of hope.

**Verdict**: The warmest ending. V is no longer alone.

> **Our call:** "The Star" is the most *preparation-gated* ending — it demands both the full Panam quest chain and a completed romance. If you want it, start the Aldecaldos quests early and don't botch the relationship checks; there is no endgame shortcut.

## Ending 4: Temperance (Johnny Route)

**Trigger**: Choose "Let Johnny and Rogue go" + In cyberspace, choose "Johnny stays"

**Outcome**: Johnny controls V's body. V's consciousness remains forever in cyberspace. Johnny buys a guitar and leaves Night City for a fresh start.

**Verdict**: The most emotionally complex ending.

## Ending 5: Path of Least Resistance

**Trigger**: On the rooftop, choose "It all ends here"

> Not recommended. Johnny's reaction is heartbreaking.

## Ending 6: The Tower (Phantom Liberty Exclusive)

**Trigger**: Side with Reed in the DLC main quest → Accept NUSA surgery

**Outcome**: The chip is removed. V survives — but was in a coma for 2 years. Waking up, all friends have moved on. V is now "ordinary," permanently unable to use cyberware.

**Verdict**: You survived. But you lost everything.

**Our call:** "The Tower" is the only ending where V truly survives — but the cost is everything else: two lost years and no cyberware ever again. It is a distinct branch off the DLC, so if you're also hunting the full [achievements](/en/cyberpunk/achievements/) set, plan a separate save for it.

## FAQ

**Q: How many endings does Cyberpunk 2077 have?** A: 5 base endings + 1 secret ending ("Don't Fear The Reaper") + 1 Phantom Liberty ending ("The Tower") = 7 total across all content.

**Q: How do you get the secret/hidden ending?** A: Reach 70%+ relationship with Johnny, then on the rooftop pick the new dialogue option to call Takemura. This unlocks "Don't Fear The Reaper", a solo suicide-run through Arasaka Tower.

**Q: Which Cyberpunk ending is the best or canon?** A: There is no canon ending. "The Star" (Panam) is the most hopeful; "The Tower" (Phantom Liberty) is the only one where V survives, but loses everything.

**Q: Does Phantom Liberty add a new ending?** A: Yes — "The Tower". Side with Reed and accept the NUSA surgery to have the biochip removed; V lives but loses 2 years and all connections.

**Q: Can you get all endings in one playthrough?** A: No. Endings branch on final and DLC choices, but you can reload saves or replay the choice via the "one more gig" epilogue.

## Common Ending Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Treating the rooftop as a free choice menu.** The real locks are set 40+ hours earlier by quests and affinity. Fix: decide your target ending before Act 2 and steer the relevant side content toward it.
2. **Missing the secret ending's 70% Johnny affinity.** It's built across the *whole* game, not at the finale. Fix: take Johnny's side gigs and pick the relationship-positive dialogue every chance you get.
3. **Assuming "The Tower" is reachable in the base game.** It requires Phantom Liberty and a specific DLC branch. Fix: own and play the DLC, then side with Reed.
4. **Not saving before the final mission.** You can't see all outcomes without reloads. Fix: manual-save at "Nocturne OP.55 N.1" and use the "one more gig" epilogue to sweep the rest.

## FAQ (Extra Questions We Get)

**Can I see multiple endings without replaying the entire game?** Yes. The "one more gig" epilogue reloads the final choice, and a manual save at Nocturne lets you branch repeatedly — you don't need five full playthroughs.

**Does my lifepath (Street Kid / Corpo / Nomad) change which ending I can get?** No. Lifepath shapes the prologue and a few flavour dialogues, but none of the six outcomes are locked or unlocked by it. See our [lifepaths](/en/cyberpunk/lifepaths/) breakdown for what it actually affects.

**Does romancing the wrong person lock me out of an ending?** Only "The Star" is romance-gated (it requires Panam). Every other ending is independent of who you romance, so a mismatched partner only costs you that one route.
