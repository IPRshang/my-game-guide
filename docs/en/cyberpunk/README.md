## I. Game Background

Cyberpunk 2077 comes from *The Witcher* studio CD Projekt RED, set in the dystopian megalopolis **Night City**. You play mercenary **V**, who — after a botched job — is implanted with the personality chip of legendary rockerboy-turned-terrorist **Johnny Silverhand**, leaving a ticking clock on their life and a race to remove it.

Based on the 1988 tabletop *Cyberpunk*, it's steeped in anti-utopian, cyberware, and hacker culture. After the **2.0 overhaul (2023)** and the **Phantom Liberty** expansion, its reputation reversed from launch-bottom to "one of the best open-world RPGs."

> This guide defaults to **no spoilers** of main story or ending direction — only mechanics and Build ideas.

---

## II. Core Mechanics

- **Attributes**: Body (melee / HP), Reflexes (blades / guns), Intelligence (hacking / quickhacks), Technical (crafting / engineering), Cool (stealth / cold-blood). Points gate which cyberware and skills you can use.
- **Cyberware**: replace body parts for abilities, but capped by **Cyberware Capacity** (introduced in 2.0) — must balance.
- **Quickhacks (hacking)**: Intelligence-core, remotely入侵 enemies / cameras; an "overheat" system replaces mana.
- **Cyberdeck / Sandbox / Operating System**: defines hacker style — Control / Combat / Overclock.
- **Street Cred**: rises with missions, unlocking vendors and cyberware.
- **Vehicle combat & AutoDrive**: Update 2.3 adds autonomous driving (AutoDrive) and self-driving taxis for smoother city travel.
- **Branching dialogue & endings**: choices shape the story and multiple endings (5+ endings + hidden) — see [All Endings](./endings).
- **Phantom Liberty**: a standalone spy-story expansion adding Dogtown and new skills / cyberware — strongly recommended.

## III. Beginner Tips

1. **Push the main story to unlock systems**: post-2.0, many systems open gradually with the story — don't blindly dump skills at the start.
2. **Pick one main style to start**: Netrunner (Intelligence) has the lowest floor and is safe at range; Solo (Body+Reflexes) frontlines; Shinobi (Reflexes+Cool) stealth-assassinates.
3. **Use cover and crouch**: first-person gunfights rely on cover — don't stand and trade.
4. **Netrunner is most friendly**: early "System Reset" / "Short Circuit" quickhacks clear rooms with no damage taken — good for less-skilled players.
5. **Start a new save post-2.0**: old saves use different systems; a fresh start gives the full new experience.
6. **Return to apartment / ripperdoc often**: upgrade cyberware, respec (small fee) at the doc.
7. **Buy "Ultimate Edition" (includes DLC)**: new players should grab **Cyberpunk 2077: Ultimate Edition** (with Phantom Liberty) for best value.

## IV. Advanced Tips & Strategy

- **Build crafting**: Netrunner stacks "cooldown + upload speed"; Solo stacks "crit + armor"; Shinobi stacks "melee damage + crit." See [Builds](./builds).
- **Cyberware capacity management**: don't over-install; leave headroom for key parts (e.g. "Adrenal Converter," "Kerenzikov").
- **Weakness & overload**: Netrunner can chain "upload virus"; Solo uses "double jump + slide" to kite.
- **Hidden ending trigger**: high Johnny sync rate + specific final-mission choice unlocks the "solo assault on Arasaka Tower" hidden ending.
- **Phantom Liberty synergy**: the expansion adds a "relic skill tree" that complements the base Build — the strongest endgame growth line.
- **AutoDrive tricks (2.3)**: turn on self-driving for long hauls and do other things en route; self-driving taxi enables fast travel.

## V. FAQ

**Q1: Still worth buying now? Same game as at launch?**
A: Post-2.0 it's a **transformative** version — systems rebuilt, bugs sharply reduced, reputation reversed. New players should just buy the Ultimate Edition.

**Q2: Need to buy Phantom Liberty separately?**
A: For the full experience **strongly recommended** (Ultimate Edition includes it). It's the final major expansion, top-tier story and systems.

**Q3: Can it run on Switch 2 / Mac?**
A: Yes. In 2026 the **Ultimate Edition** launched on **Nintendo Switch 2** and **macOS (Apple Silicon)**, with cross-platform cloud saves.

**Q4: PS5 Pro enhanced?**
A: Yes. A free April 2026 update added ray-traced Pro mode and PSSR upscaling, with three graphics modes.

**Q5: What's the latest version? More content?**
A: As of 2026, latest is **2.3 + 2.31**. 2.3 added AutoDrive, 4 new vehicles, photo mode enhancements; CDPR calls 2.3 the last major content update, with follow-up focused on tech / platform support. Sequel **Cyberpunk 2** (UE5) is in pre-production, expected after 2028.

**Q6: Motion sick from first-person?**
A: You can switch to third-person in cars / bikes; adjust FOV and motion blur in settings to ease it.
