# Cyberpunk 2077 — Build Guide

## 1. Netrunner (Mage)

**Core Idea**: Kill everyone through security cameras without ever entering the building.

**Stats**: Intelligence 20 → Tech 20 → Cool 15

**Core Gear**:
- Cyberdeck: NetWatch Netdriver Mk.5 (endgame) / Biotech Σ Mk.4 (early)
- Quickhacks: System Collapse, Cyberware Malfunction, Short Circuit, Contagion

**Playstyle**: Breach camera → Tag all enemies → Contagion + Overheat → Squad wipe. Never step inside.

**Rating**: ★★★★★ Safest, most comfortable build. God tier lategame.

## 2. Sandevistan Samurai (Meta King)

**Core Idea**: Before their bullet leaves the barrel, my blade has already finished its work.

**Stats**: Reflexes 20 → Tech 20 → Cool 18

**Core Gear**:
- Sandevistan: Militech "Apogee" (85% time slow, 3s cooldown)
- Katana: Byakko / Jinchu-Maru / Scalpel
- Kerenzikov (dodge-triggered time slow)

**Playstyle**: Activate Sandevistan → Slice everything → Time resumes → Pile of bodies

**Rating**: ★★★★★ Style + Power = Perfection.

## 3. Gunslinger (Traditional Shooter)

**Stats**: Reflexes 20 → Cool 20 → Tech 15

**Weapon Picks**:

| Weapon | Type | Trait |
|--------|------|-------|
| Yinglong | SMG | Smart weapon, auto-headshot |
| Archangel | Pistol | Explosive crits |
| Breakthrough | Sniper | Wall-penetrating rounds |
| Psalm 11:6 | Assault Rifle | Incendiary rounds |

**Rating**: ★★★☆ Classic but effective.

## 4. Berserker (Unga Bunga)

**Stats**: Body 20 → Reflexes 15 → Tech 20

**Core**: Berserk cyberware + Gorilla Arms + max armor

**Playstyle**: Rage → Charge → Smash. No technique. Pure emotion.

**Rating**: ★★★ Simple and brutal. Perfect for players who don't want to think.
