# Cyberpunk 2077 — Build Guide

Build guides for Cyberpunk 2077 usually stop at "put points in Reflexes." That advice is worse than useless post-2.0: since the overhaul, your *Operating System* cyberware — not your attribute spread — is what actually defines whether you're a hacker, a blade-dancer, or a bullet sponge. Attributes open the door; the OS and the [cyberware](/en/cyberpunk/cyberware/) loadout walk you through it.

> **Common misconception:** that any build can do anything with enough levels. In practice your Operating System (Cyberdeck vs Sandevistan vs Berserk) commits you to a combat loop on day one — and 20 Technical Ability is the gate for the legendary cyberware every top build relies on. Plan the OS before the perks.

## 1. Netrunner (Mage)

**Core Idea**: Kill everyone through security cameras without ever entering the building.

**Stats**: Intelligence 20 → Tech 20 → Cool 15

**Core Gear**:
- Cyberdeck: NetWatch Netdriver Mk.5 (endgame) / Biotech Σ Mk.4 (early)
- Quickhacks: System Collapse, Cyberware Malfunction, Short Circuit, Contagion

**Playstyle**: Breach camera → Tag all enemies → Contagion + Overheat → Squad wipe. Never step inside.

**Rating**: ★★★★★ Safest, most comfortable build. God tier lategame.

> **Our call:** this is the build to learn the game on. Because the loop is "hack, don't fight," your survivability doesn't depend on twitch aim — and the [quickhacks](/en/cyberpunk/quickhacks/) you stack scale with Intelligence, so the attribute curve above is doing real work, not just meeting requirements.

## 2. Sandevistan Samurai (Meta King)

**Core Idea**: Before their bullet leaves the barrel, my blade has already finished its work.

**Stats**: Reflexes 20 → Tech 20 → Cool 18

**Core Gear**:
- Sandevistan: Militech "Apogee" (85% time slow, 3s cooldown)
- Katana: Byakko / Jinchu-Maru / Scalpel
- Kerenzikov (dodge-triggered time slow)

**Playstyle**: Activate Sandevistan → Slice everything → Time resumes → Pile of bodies

**Rating**: ★★★★★ Style + Power = Perfection.

> **Our call:** the 85% slow is what makes this build feel unfair — but it lives or dies by the Sandevistan implant and the Kerenzikov follow-up. Skimp on [cyberware](/en/cyberpunk/cyberware/) and you're just a guy with a sword. The 3s cooldown note above is the reason it stays online in long fights.

## 3. Gunslinger (Traditional Shooter)

**Stats**: Reflexes 20 → Cool 20 → Tech 15

**Weapon Picks**:

| Weapon | Type | Trait |
|--------|------|-------|
| Yinglong | SMG | Smart weapon, auto-headshot |
| Archangel | Pistol | Explosive crits |
| Breakthrough | Sniper | Wall-penetrating rounds |
| Psalm 11:6 | Assault Rifle | Incendiary rounds |

**Rating**: ★★★☆ Classic but effective.

**Our call:** the most gear-dependent of the four. The stat line above favours crit and handling, so lean into the Archangel/Psalm explosive and crit synergy rather than spraying — this build rewards aim, not automation.

## 4. Berserker (Unga Bunga)

**Stats**: Body 20 → Reflexes 15 → Tech 20

**Core**: Berserk cyberware + Gorilla Arms + max armor

**Playstyle**: Rage → Charge → Smash. No technique. Pure emotion.

**Rating**: ★★★ Simple and brutal. Perfect for players who don't want to think.

> **Our call:** Berserk + Gorilla Arms is a commitment to face-tanking, which is exactly why the [cyberware](/en/cyberpunk/cyberware/) loadout (Second Heart, Subdermal Armor) matters more here than for any other build. No kiting, no stealth — your sustain *is* your offense.

## Common Build Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Picking attributes before the Operating System.** Your OS (Cyberdeck / Sandevistan / Berserk) commits you to a combat loop on day one. Fix: choose the OS fantasy first, then build attributes around it.
2. **Skipping 20 Technical Ability.** That's the gate for legendary cyberware every top build wants. Fix: route Tech to 20 if you're aiming for an endgame loadout.
3. **Mixing playstyles.** Netrunner stats under a Berserk OS fight each other — you get neither loop. Fix: commit to one OS and one attribute spine.
4. **Treating all ripperdocs as the same.** Fingers holds exclusive Sandevistan models; Viktor is the fair early stop. Fix: visit the specialist your build actually needs.

## FAQ

**Which build is best for a first playthrough?** The Netrunner is rated ★★★★★ and is the safest — you clear rooms through cameras without stepping inside, so weak aim isn't punishing. Sandevistan Samurai is the flashiest but demands timing and good cyberware.

**Do I need 20 Technical Ability for every build?** Only to equip *legendary* cyberware; most endgame builds want it precisely for that. If you're running epics you can skip it, but the build comes alive at legendary tier — see our [cyberware](/en/cyberpunk/cyberware/) guide.

**Can I respec if I change my mind?** Attributes and perks can both be reset for a fee at any ripperdoc, so experimentation is low-risk — but plan your [perks](/en/cyberpunk/perks/) around your Operating System so the refund actually helps.

**Which build pairs with the secret ending?** Any — but the solo "Don't Fear The Reaper" run is brutal, so a high-survivability [build](/en/cyberpunk/builds/) (Second Heart, good sustain) helps. Check our [endings](/en/cyberpunk/endings/) guide for the exact trigger.
