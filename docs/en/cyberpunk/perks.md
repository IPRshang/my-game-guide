# Cyberpunk 2077 Perks Guide

**In Cyberpunk 2077 (2.0+), perks are gated by five attributes — Body, Reflexes, Technical Ability, Intelligence and Cool. You earn a perk point per level plus a few from quests and skill progression, and you should plan around one or two attributes rather than spreading points thin.**

## How attributes gate your perks

| Attribute | Governs | Typical build |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Shotguns, blunt weapons, health, cyberware capacity | Solo / tanky brawler |
| Reflexes | Assault rifles, blades, mobility | Netrunner-gun hybrid / assassin |
| Technical Ability | Crafting, grenades, cyberware mods | Tech weapon specialist |
| Intelligence | Quickhacks, RAM, hacking | Netrunner |
| Cool | Stealth, pistols, ninjutsu | Stealth pistolero |

## Choosing where to invest

1. **Pick a damage source first** — a single weapon class or quickhack line.
2. **Push the governing attribute to 15–20** — most strong perks unlock in that range.
3. **Raise Technical Ability if you want cyberware** — it gates both crafting and cyberware capacity.
4. **Keep Body at least moderate** — more health and cyberware capacity benefit every build.
5. **Respec is cheap** — respeccing costs a modest fee, so experimenting is fine.

## Common mistakes

- Spreading points evenly across all five attributes (unlocks nothing powerful).
- Ignoring cyberware capacity — it is often a bigger power spike than a perk.
- Over-investing in crafting early, before you have the components to use it.

## FAQ

**Q: How many perk points do I get?**
A: Roughly one per level plus extras from skill progression and certain quests — not enough to fill more than two trees in a single playthrough.

**Q: Can I respec?**
A: Yes. Respeccing is available for a fee, so do not fear early experimentation.

**Q: Which attribute is best for a first run?**
A: Reflexes or Body — both give straightforward survivability and damage without complex systems.
