## 🗺️ Platinum / 1000G Optimal Route

### Phase 1: Story Progression (25-30 hrs)
1. Push main story, auto-unlock 12 story achievements
2. Do some side quests and gigs along the way
3. Collect tarot card graffiti as you go

### Phase 2: Side Content Cleanup (20-25 hrs)
1. Clear all district side quests
2. Complete all cyberpsycho sightings
3. Finish all gigs
4. Unlock 7 district achievements

### Phase 3: Collectible Sweep (15-20 hrs)
1. Collect all 20 tarot card graffiti
2. Collect all legendary equipment
3. Collect all crafting specs
4. Collect all vehicles

### Phase 4: Ending Cleanup (10-15 hrs)
1. Save before the final mission
2. Play through all 5 endings
3. Try to trigger the secret ending

### Phase 5: DLC Content (20-25 hrs)
1. Complete DLC main story
2. Clear all DLC side content
3. Collect DLC new items
4. Reach all DLC endings

### Phase 6: Cleanup (5-10 hrs)
1. Check for missing achievements
2. Fill in hidden achievements
3. Platinum unlocked!

## 💡 Quick Money / EXP Farming Tips

### Money Farming
1. **Craft & Sell**: Craft high-value items to sell
2. **Cyberpsychos**: Each has great rewards
3. **High-tier Gigs**: Difficult gigs pay well
4. **Sell Junk**: Collect and sell everything

### EXP Farming
1. **Cyberpsychos**: Most EXP per encounter
2. **NCPD Scanner Hustles**: Lots of small tasks
3. **Hacking**: Hack terminals for EXP
4. **Headshots**: Headshot kills give bonus EXP
