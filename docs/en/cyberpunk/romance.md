# Cyberpunk 2077 — Romance Guide

> In Night City, there's more than just combat. There's love too.

## Panam Palmer (Male V Only)

**Gender**: Male V (male body type, any voice)

**Quest Chain**: Ghost Town → Lightning Breaks → Life During Wartime → Riders on the Storm → With a Little Help from My Friends → Queen of the Highway

**Key Choices**:
1. In "With a Little Help from My Friends", support her against Nash
2. At the bar, express your care for her
3. Touch her leg on the Basilisk (⚠️ CRITICAL choice!)
4. Final mission: don't reject her physical advances

**Romance Trigger**: After completing "Queen of the Highway", by the campfire

> Panam is the only romance partner who joins the final battle. Highest utility.

---

## Judy Alvarez (Female V Only)

**Gender**: Female V (female body type + female voice)

**Quest Chain**: Automatic Love → The Space in Between → Disasterpiece → Double Life → Both Sides, Now → Ex-Factor → Talkin' 'bout a Revolution → Pisces → Pyramid Song

**Key Choices**:

### The Pisces Trap ⚠️
During the Pisces mission, you face Maiko's plan:

| Your Choice | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| Reject Maiko's plan (stand with Judy, fight) | ✅ Romance continues |
| Accept Maiko's plan, but **refuse** her money | ✅ Romance continues |
| Accept Maiko's plan **AND** take her money | ❌ Romance **permanently ends** |

> 🔴 **If you take Maiko's money, Judy leaves Night City and removes you from her contacts — irreversible!**

### The Pyramid Song Choice
1. Dive with Judy in "Pyramid Song"
2. Spend the night at the cabin
3. **The next morning at the dock** — choose "The beginning of something amazing" to lock in the romance

**Romance Trigger**: After completing "Pyramid Song", at the lakeside cabin / dock

> The romance is officially triggered at the dock conversation in Pyramid Song, NOT during Pisces. But Pisces determines whether you get to see Pyramid Song at all.

## River Ward (Female V Only)

**Gender**: Female V (female body type, any voice)

**Quest Chain**: I Fought the Law → The Hunt → Following the River

**Key Choices**:
1. Support River during "The Hunt"
2. Show affection at the family dinner
3. Accept him on the rooftop where he confesses

> River has the shortest quest chain of the four romance options (only 3 side missions).

---

## Kerry Eurodyne (Male V Only)

**Gender**: Male V (male body type + male voice)

**Quest Chain**: Holdin' On → Second Conflict → A Like Supreme → Rebel! Rebel! → I Don't Wanna Hear It → **Off the Leash** → Boat Drinks

**Key Choices**:
1. Help Kerry resolve the "Rebel! Rebel!" concert crisis
2. During **Off the Leash** (at the Dark Matter club terrace), you get your first opportunity to kiss Kerry
3. Accept his kiss on the yacht (romance confirmed in Boat Drinks)
