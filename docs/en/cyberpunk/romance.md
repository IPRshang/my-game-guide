# Cyberpunk 2077 — Romance Guide

> In Night City, there's more than just combat. There's love too.

## Panam Palmer (Female V Only)

**Trigger**: Complete "Riders on the Storm" main quest

**Key Choices**:
1. In "With a Little Help from My Friends", support her against Nash
2. At the bar, pick "You seem different now"
3. Touch her leg on the Basilisk (CRITICAL choice!)
4. Final mission: don't reject her physical advances

**Romance Trigger**: After completing all Panam side quests, by the campfire

> Panam is the only romance partner who joins the final battle. Highest utility.

## Judy Alvarez (Female V Only)

**Trigger**: After "Double Life" main quest, once Evelyn is dead

**Key Choices**:
1. Accept Judy's flirtation during "Both Sides, Now"
2. Dive with her in "Pyramid Song"
3. At the cabin, pick "It's a new beginning"
4. **Don't accept Maiko's money** (kills the romance permanently)

**Romance Trigger**: After completing "Pyramid Song", at the lakeside cabin

> Judy and Panam cannot be pursued simultaneously. Choose one and lock in.

## Kerry Eurodyne (Male V Only)

**Trigger**: Complete Johnny Silverhand's side quest chain

**Key Choices**:
1. Help Kerry resolve the "Rebel! Rebel!" concert crisis
2. Trash equipment with him on the yacht
3. Accept his kiss

## River Ward (Male V Only)

**Trigger**: Complete "I Fought the Law" quest

**Key Choices**:
1. Support River during "The Hunt"
2. Show affection at the family dinner
3. Accept him on the rooftop where he confesses
