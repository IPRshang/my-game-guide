# Cyberpunk 2077 Lifepaths Compared

**Cyberpunk 2077 offers three lifepaths — Nomad, Street Kid and Corpo. Each gives a different prologue, unique dialogue options throughout the game, and a few exclusive side jobs. None of them locks you out of any ending, and all three converge on the same main story after the opening hours.**

| Lifepath | Opening | Flavour | Unique dialogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nomad | Badlands, smuggling job | Outsider, family and borders | Nomad slang, Badlands context |
| Street Kid | Night City streets, local debts | Grew up hustling | Street slang, gang knowledge |
| Corpo | Arasaka tower, corporate intrigue | Insider, office politics | Corporate jargon, boardroom context |

## What actually changes

- **The prologue** — roughly the first hour plays out completely differently.
- **Dialogue options** — many conversations gain one or two extra choices tied to your background.
- **A handful of side jobs** — each lifepath has contacts and missions the others never see.
- **Some item and shortcut access** — small conveniences, not major power differences.

## What does not change

- The main story beats and all endings are available regardless of lifepath.
- Your attributes, skills and perk choices are entirely independent of this pick.
- Romance options are unaffected.

## How to choose

- Pick **Nomad** if you like the open Badlands and an outsider's perspective.
- Pick **Street Kid** if you want to feel like a native of Night City.
- Pick **Corpo** if the corporate intrigue angle appeals most.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I change lifepath later?**
A: No. It is locked at character creation, so pick based on roleplay rather than power.

**Q: Which lifepath is strongest?**
A: None is mechanically stronger — the differences are narrative and cosmetic.

**Q: Do I need three playthroughs to see everything?**
A: To see all three prologues and their exclusive jobs, yes; but the core story is identical.
