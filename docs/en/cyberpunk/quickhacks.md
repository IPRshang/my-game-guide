# Cyberpunk 2077 Quickhacks Guide

**Quickhacks are Cyberpunk 2077's spell-like hacking abilities: you target an enemy or device through your scanner, spend RAM, and after a short upload time the effect lands. A netrunner build works by chaining cheap hacks and letting them spread between enemies.**

## Core mechanics

- **RAM** is your hacking resource; it regenerates but is capped by your cyberdeck.
- **Upload time** is how long a hack takes to land once cast — shorter is better in open combat.
- **Spread** lets certain hacks jump to nearby enemies after a kill or proc, which is what makes hacking scale.
- **Queueing** lets you stack several hacks on one target before they resolve.

## Practical combos

1. **Control first** — apply a disabling hack (or one that forces a weapon malfunction) so enemies stop shooting.
2. **Then damage** — follow with damage-over-time hacks that tick while enemies are disabled.
3. **Lean on spread** — once a hack spreads, one cast can clear a whole room.
4. **Use cameras and devices** — hacking cameras lets you chain to enemies you cannot see.

## Levelling a netrunner

- Invest in **Intelligence** for RAM recovery and hack power.
- Upgrade your **cyberdeck** — it determines both RAM capacity and how many hacks you can queue.
- Take perks that **reduce upload time** and **recover RAM on kills** — these turn hacking from slow into dominant.
- Keep a backup weapon; some enemies are resistant or immune to hacks.

## FAQ

**Q: Is a netrunner build viable without killing anyone?**
A: A non-lethal run is possible using disable and memory-wipe style hacks, though some scripted encounters force combat.

**Q: Why do my hacks fail sometimes?**
A: Enemies may have resistance, or you may lack RAM. Check the target's resistances in the scanner.

**Q: What should I upgrade first?**
A: The cyberdeck — it gates RAM and queue size, which matter more than any single hack.
