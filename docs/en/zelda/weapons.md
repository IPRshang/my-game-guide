## Special Weapons

### Master Sword (One of a Kind)
- **Base ATK**: 30
- **Near Gloom / Dungeons**: 60 (glowing state)
- **Durability**: 188 (10-minute recharge when depleted)
- **Beam Attack**: Throw (R) at full health to fire sword beams

### One-Hit Obliterator
- Instantly kills any enemy
- But leaves you with only a quarter heart
- One of the DLC final rewards
