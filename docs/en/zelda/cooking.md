## Cooking Tips

1. **Cook during Blood Moon** — 23:30-00:15 guarantees critical success
2. **Stack same-effect ingredients** — 4 same + Dragon Horn = Lv.3 effect for 30 min
3. **Fairies can't be cooked** — they vanish, don't waste them
4. **Wood bundles ignite** — don't need flint to start a fire
