# Zelda: TotK — Complete Cooking Guide

> You can't save Hyrule on an empty stomach!

Most cooking pages just dump a recipe table and call it a day. This one is built differently: we kept every recipe, healing value, and farming spot from our master list below, but we're also telling you *which dishes earn a slot in your rotation* and *which ones are traps*. Cooking in Tears of the Kingdom is not about variety — it's about three buffs (Attack, Defense, and a temperature resist) held at Level 3 for the full 30 minutes while you do the dangerous stuff.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** players cook a "balanced" mix of effects and wonder why nothing lands. In TotK, effects from a single cook *don't* blend into one super-buff — a Meat Skewer with a Spicy Pepper gives you a weak heal *plus* a weak cold resist, instead of a strong version of either. Cook one effect per pot, max it with a Dragon Horn, and move on.

**Our call:** treat the Dragon Horn as a non-negotiable. Every top-tier buff below is written as "×4 + Dragon Horn" for a reason — without the horn you get roughly 1/6th the duration, and you'll be re-cooking mid-dungeon.

## Core Mechanics

- Ingredients + Ingredients = Food (effects stack)
- Monster Parts + Critters = Elixir
- **Golden Rule**: Add Dragon Horn shard to extend any effect to 30 minutes!

## Cooking Tips

1. **Cook during Blood Moon** — 23:30-00:15 guarantees critical success
2. **Stack same-effect ingredients** — 4 same + Dragon Horn = Lv.3 effect for 30 min
3. **Fairies can't be cooked** — they vanish, don't waste them
4. **Wood bundles ignite** — don't need flint to start a fire

## Common Cooking Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Cooking mixed effects.** You get a weak version of each instead of a strong one. Fix: one effect per cook, four of that ingredient, Dragon Horn on top.
2. **Hoarding Dragon Horns.** They're farmable by shooting a dragon's horn and waiting for respawn — use them, don't bank them. Fix: keep a running stock and cook at full duration.
3. **Healing with buff dishes.** Attack/Defense meals don't heal. Fix: carry a separate Hearty dish for emergencies; don't expect your Lv.3 meal to top you off.
4. **Ignoring critical-cook timing.** Cooking outside the Blood Moon window still works but wastes the guaranteed crit. Fix: batch-cook right after a Blood Moon for free bonus effects.

## FAQ

**Do effects from different dishes stack?** Yes — a Defense meal and an Attack meal run simultaneously, which is exactly why you cook them separately rather than combining ingredients. You can hold Attack + Defense + a temperature resist at once.

**Why only Lv.2 on cold/heat resist?** The Peppers and Hydromelons in the table cap at Level 2 with four ingredients. That covers every surface climate; deeper cold pockets in the Depths want a warmer outfit or a campfire instead.

**Is the Dragon Horn a one-time use?** No. Shooting any dragon's horn drops a shard and the dragon keeps flying; the horn regrows on its loop. Farm Farosh (or the other three) whenever your stock runs low.

**Where do I go after mastering cooking?** Pair buffs with a real [Fuse weapon plan](/en/zelda/weapons/) and the [shrine stamina path](/en/zelda/shrines/) that lets you pull the Master Sword — that trio is the core of any serious TotK run.
