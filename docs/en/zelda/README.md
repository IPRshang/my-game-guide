> 🗡️ **A New Chapter in Hyrule** · Nintendo · May 12, 2023 · Nintendo Switch / **Switch 2 Edition**

Ours is opinionated: we think TotK's learning curve is backwards for new players — the game hands you four reality-bending abilities on the Great Sky Island, then expects you to *invent* your own solutions, and most people under-use three of the four. This page is the spine; the deep dives on [cooking](/en/zelda/cooking/), [weapons](/en/zelda/weapons/), [shrines](/en/zelda/shrines/), [Zonai devices](/en/zelda/zonai-devices/), and [Korok seeds](/en/zelda/korok-seeds/) hang off it.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** new players treat weapons like permanent gear and hoard them. TotK weapons are *consumables*. The real power is Fuse — glue a material to a stick and the stick becomes a weapon. If you're saving your "good" sword for later, you're playing the wrong game.

**Our call:** spend your first five hours *building*, not fighting. A Hover Bike and a fused spear beat any legendary drop you haven't found yet.

## I. Game Background

Tears of the Kingdom is the direct sequel to *Breath of the Wild*. The story picks up right after the prior game: Hyrule Castle lifts into the sky, a mysterious "cataclysm" descends, and while Link and Zelda explore the depths, something goes wrong and Zelda vanishes. You control Link, wielding the new "power of the left hand" to adventure across **sky, surface, and depths** — three layers of the world — uncovering Hyrule's ancient secrets.

Its biggest feature is a **physics-sandbox creator**: the prior "Sheikah Slate" abilities are replaced by new "Zonai Devices," letting you build almost any vehicle, machine, or contraption with extreme freedom.

> This guide defaults to **no spoilers** of main story or key region names — only mechanics and exploration ideas.

## II. Core Mechanics

- **Four abilities (from the left hand)**:
  - **Ultrahand**: grab / fuse objects — core for building vehicles, bridges, machines.
  - **Fuse**: fuse weapons / materials onto gear, customizing attack and properties (e.g. stick + rock = hammer).
  - **Ascend**: rise straight up through the surface above you — a puzzle / climb god-skill.
  - **Recall**: rewind an object's motion — "ride" falling rocks / traps back upward.
- **Zonai Devices**: battery-powered carts, fans, rockets, stabilizers, steering sticks, etc., combined with Ultrahand for vehicles / aircraft.
- **Autobuild**: records structures you've built for one-click reconstruction — key to late-game efficiency.
- **Three-layer map**: sky islands (start), surface (BotW Hyrule), depths (dark caves needing light resources).
- **Shrines & Koroks**: shrines teach abilities / grant hearts; Koroks raise weapon / shield / bow capacity.
- **Cooking & elixirs**: ingredient combos yield healing / attack / defense / temperature effects — see [Complete Recipes](/en/zelda/cooking/).

**Our call:** of the four abilities, Ascend and Recall are the ones veterans under-rate. They don't deal damage, so they feel passive — but they delete more "stuck" moments than any weapon. Practice them before you ever touch a lynel.

## III. Beginner Tips

1. **Master the four abilities first**: practice the sky-island tutorial thoroughly, especially Ultrahand and Ascend — they're mandatory all game.
2. **Fuse to build weapons**: any stick + material becomes a weapon; don't scoff at low early attack — Fuse is the key.
3. **Explore the depths early**: the depths hold "Brightbloom Seeds" and abundant Zonai ore — materials for batteries and devices.
4. **Collect Koroks for capacity**: weapons / shields / bows break easily; more Koroks = bigger carry cap, the most practical upgrade.
5. **Learn basic vehicles**: fan + platform = wind cart, rocket + platform = aircraft — double your traversal efficiency.
6. **Keep cooking stocked**: make a few "defense + attack" dishes before heading out — huge boss-fight fault tolerance.
7. **Use Ascend & Recall**: when stuck on terrain, these two often work wonders, easier than climbing.

## IV. Advanced Tips & Strategy

- **Blueprint reuse**: after building common vehicles, save to blueprint and summon instantly — skip re-assembling each time.
- **Zonai battery management**: batteries upgrade via "Zonai Cores" in the depths; prioritize enough for flight / driving.
- **Fuse ideas**: ranged with "bow + fruit" auto-aim; break guard with "hammer + rock"; elemental with "weapon + ice/fire/lightning fruit."
- **Depth farming**: depths enemies drop high-tier materials — the main source for endgame gear.
- **amiibo (supported from 2025)**: Sidon / Tulin / Yunobo / Riju amiibo grant exclusive gear; usable in Switch 2 Edition.
- **Speedrun tricks**: a mastered "rocket shield jump" via Ultrahand ignores terrain to reach targets directly — a world-record-level technique.

**Our call:** the single highest-leverage mid-game goal is a full battery stack for the Hover Bike. Everything else — depth farming, shrine sweeping, [Korok capacity](/en/zelda/korok-seeds/) — gets faster once you can fly anywhere.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I play TotK without playing Breath of the Wild?**
A: Yes — the story is self-contained and newcomer-friendly; playing the prequel just adds resonance with the map and characters.

**Q: What's different in the Switch 2 version?**
A: **Switch 2 Edition** (from June 2025) improves frame rate / resolution and supports **ZELDA NOTES** (official app: map markers, daily bonuses). Old Switch saves carry over.

**Q: Is there DLC?**
A: TotK has **no paid DLC** (unlike BotW's DLC pass). Only free fix patches since (latest Ver. 1.4.3, Feb 2026).

**Q: Weapons keep breaking — what do I do?**
A: It's a series trademark. More Koroks for capacity + use cheap materials as consumables via Fuse; save precious weapons for bosses.

**Q: Depths too dark?**
A: "Brightbloom Seeds" scattered in the depths light up areas; carry "glowing / firefly" type materials for temporary light.

**Q: Need internet?**
A: Fully offline single-player. ZELDA NOTES needs internet and a Nintendo account, but is optional.

## Common Mistakes New Players Make (and the Fix)

1. **Saving weapons instead of using them.** They break by design; the material on the end is what matters. Fix: fuse cheap horns to sticks and spend them freely.
2. **Skipping the depths early.** You miss Brightbloom Seeds and Zonai ore that make everything else easier. Fix: drop down as soon as you have a light source.
3. **Building vehicles from scratch every time.** Fix: learn Autobuild and save the Hover Bike as a favorite on day one.
4. **Grinding shrines for hearts over stamina.** Fix: prioritize two full stamina wheels early — pulling the Master Sword needs them, and [shrine routing](/en/zelda/shrines/) rewards it.
