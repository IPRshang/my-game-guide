## Recommended Collection Order

### Phase 1: Stamina First
- Collect 24 shrines → Full 2 wheels of stamina → Pull Master Sword

### Phase 2: Hearts Priority
- Continue collecting → 13 hearts → Pull Master Sword (alternative)

### Phase 3: Completionist
- All 152 complete → Hero of the Wild Set
