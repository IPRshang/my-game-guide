# Tears of the Kingdom Depths Guide

**The Depths are a vast dark underworld mirroring the surface map of Tears of the Kingdom. They are dangerous mainly because of darkness and Gloom rather than enemies — so the core skills are lighting the way, managing Gloom damage, and knowing what is actually worth collecting down there.**

## Before you go down

1. **Bring light sources** — items or Zonai devices that illuminate are essential.
2. **Stock healing that restores Gloom-damaged hearts** — normal food will not heal hearts lost to Gloom.
3. **Bring armour with Gloom resistance** if you have any — it reduces the chip damage.
4. **Unlock Lightroots** — each one permanently lights a portion of the Depths.

## How to explore

- **Lightroots are the priority** — they reveal the map and make everything else easier.
- The Depths' layout **mirrors the surface** — landmarks above correspond to features below, which helps navigation.
- **Chasms** are the main entrances; mark the ones you find on the surface map.
- **Avoid wandering blindly** — without light, you will miss ledges and resources.

## What is worth collecting

- **Zonai charges and devices** — the Depths are the best source for building materials.
- **Unique ore and crafting materials** not found on the surface.
- **Lightroots** themselves, which are both progress and convenience.
- **Boss rematches** — some tough enemies reappear below, useful for farming strong parts.

## Tips

- **Do not fight everything** — mobility beats combat in the dark.
- **Use the terrain** — elevated structures let you glide past hazards.
- **Return to the surface regularly** to restock and reset.

## FAQ

**Q: Why do my hearts disappear?**
A: Gloom damage temporarily removes maximum hearts; you need specific healing or safe areas to restore them.

**Q: Is the Depths required for the story?**
A: It is optional for the main quest, but it holds major resources and progression items.

**Q: How do I see down there?**
A: Light sources, Lightroots, and certain armour or items — bring several options.
