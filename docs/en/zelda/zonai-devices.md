# Tears of the Kingdom: Zonai Devices & Engineering Guide

Zonai Devices are the defining innovation of Tears of the Kingdom. Using **Ultrahand** to combine devices into machines — from simple vehicles to fully autonomous combat drones — is where the game's true freedom begins.

## Device Overview

There are **27 Zonai devices** across four categories:

| Category | Count | Key Devices |
|----------|:-----:|-------------|
| Propulsion | 6 | Fan, Rocket, Wheel, Steering Stick |
| Weapons | 7 | Beam Emitter, Cannon, Flame Emitter, Frost Emitter |
| Utility | 9 | Spring, Balloon, Hover Stone, Stake |
| Special | 5 | Wing, Hot Air Balloon, Battery, Stabilizer |

---

## Propulsion Devices

### Fan

> Most-used device. Provides continuous thrust.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|:-----:|
| Thrust | Medium |
| Energy cost | Low |
| Source | Most Device Dispensers |

**Classic builds:**
- **Hover Bike**: 2 Fans + 1 Steering Stick = best aerial vehicle
- **Fan Boat**: 1 Fan + 1 wood plank = simple water transport
- **Helicopter**: 4 Fans downward + 1 Steering Stick

---

### Rocket

> One-time extreme thrust. Instantly launches objects skyward.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|:-----:|
| Thrust | Extreme |
| Duration | ~3 seconds (single use) |
| Source | Most dispensers |

**Classic uses:**
- Shield Rocket Jump: attach to shield → hold guard → launch
- Takeoff booster: attach under an aircraft for catapult launch
- Shrine shortcuts: rocket directly to high exits

---

### Steering Stick

> The pilot seat for all vehicles.

**Mechanics:**
- Stand on it to enter driving mode
- All propulsion devices on the same structure respond to stick input
- Controls: left stick for direction, right stick for camera

**Key tip:** Place the stick at the vehicle's center of gravity. For the Hover Bike, position it slightly forward of center.

---

### Wheels

| Type | Speed | Traction | Best Terrain |
|------|:-----:|:--------:|--------------|
| Small Wheel | Medium | Medium | Flat ground |
| Big Wheel | Slow | High | Rough, sand, snow |

---

### Propeller

> Motor-driven thrust device

Requires electricity (Shock Emitter + metal conduction). Doesn't directly consume battery, good for long-duration flight.

## Weapon Devices

### Beam Emitter

> Long-range laser. Highest damage weapon device.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|:-----:|
| Damage | High (~8-12 per hit) |
| Range | Long |
| Energy cost | High |
| Source | Sky Islands (common) |

**Combat builds:**
- **Auto Turret**: Construct Head + 3× Beam Emitter → auto-targets and fires
- **Beam Tank**: Big Wheels + 4× Beam Emitters → mobile fire platform

---

### Cannon

> Area explosive damage. Best for breaking obstacles.

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|:-----:|
| Damage | Massive (AoE) |
| Fire rate | Slow (~2s per shot) |
| Energy cost | Very high |

**Best for:** Rock walls, ore veins, mob clearing, large bosses (Stone Talus, Hinox)

---

### Flame Emitter

> Continuous fire stream with burn DoT

One-shots ice-type enemies. Great for clearing thorns.

---

### Frost Emitter

> Freezes enemies — control + damage amp

**Combo:** Frost → physical attack (×3 damage on frozen enemies) → done.

---

### Shock Emitter

> Electric damage + paralysis + AoE in water

- Strongest weapon near water (chain lightning)
- Can power motors to drive propellers

---

### Homing Cart

> Self-driving bomb that seeks enemies

Drop 3 into a monster camp and watch the fireworks.

---

### Construct Head

> **Critical!** Makes attached weapon devices auto-target the nearest enemy.

- Continuously tracks the closest enemy
- All weapons attached to it share the aiming direction
- The core component of any autonomous combat machine

---

## Utility Devices

### Spring

Launch pad. Height depends on object weight. Spring + Shield = Shield Bounce.

### Balloon

Slow upward lift. Multiple balloons stack for heavier loads. Heat with a torch or Flame Emitter to rise faster.

### Hover Stone

Activates and stays suspended in place.

**Extreme uses:** Mid-air sniper platform, shrine puzzle filler.

### Stake

Drives into ground or walls as a fixed anchor.

**Classic:** 2 Stakes + long plank = instant bridge. Stake + Construct Head + weapons = fixed turret.

### Portable Pot

One-time mobile cooking pot. Invaluable in the wilderness far from stables or towns.

### Light

Illuminates dark caves. Attach to a shield for a mobile light source.

### Mirror

Reflects light. Can blind enemies. Used in specific shrine puzzles.

## Best Blueprints

### 🥇 Hover Bike

> The most practical vehicle in the entire game

**Materials:** Fan ×2, Steering Stick ×1

**Build:**
1. Place Steering Stick in the center
2. Attach Fans to front-left and front-right, tilted 45° backward-downward
3. Save to Autobuild favorites

**Why it's the best:** Extremely low energy cost, reaches anywhere on the map, costs only 9 Zonaite.

---

### 🥈 Combat Drone

> Fully automatic monster clearing

**Materials:** Construct Head ×1, Beam Emitter ×2-3, Homing Cart ×1 (optional), Cannon ×1 (optional)

**Build:**
1. Construct Head as base
2. Attach 2-3 Beam Emitters on top
3. (Optional) Homing Cart underneath for mobile version
4. (Optional) Add a Cannon for crowd control

---

### 🥉 Bomber

> Aerial bombardment platform

**Materials:** Wing ×1, Fan ×2, Steering Stick ×1, Cannon ×2 (under wing)

Fly over enemies — cannons fire downward automatically.

---

### Helicopter

Vertical takeoff for complex terrain exploration.

**Materials:** Fan ×4 (downward), Steering Stick ×1, Hover Stone ×1 (optional, to hover in place)

---

### Wing Fighter

Classic combination using base-game devices.

**Materials:** Wing ×1, Fan ×3 (2 rear + 1 lift), Steering Stick ×1, Rocket ×1 (takeoff boost, optional)

---

### All-Terrain Vehicle (ATV)

Best land vehicle.

**Materials:** Big Wheel ×4, Wood/Stone Slab ×1 (body), Steering Stick ×1, Beam Emitter ×1 (optional weapon)

Perfect for Depths exploration — light up an area, then cruise in your ATV.

## Battery Upgrade Path

Batteries are the "fuel" for Zonai devices. Bigger capacity = longer operation.

### Energy Cell Capacity

| Stage | Cells | Crystallized Charges | Cumulative |
|:-----:|:-----:|:--------------------:|:----------:|
| Start | 1 cell (3 bars) | 0 | 0 |
| 1st upgrade | 2 cells | 100 | 100 |
| 2nd upgrade | 3 cells | 100 | 200 |
| ... | ... | 100/cell | ... |
| Max | 16 cells (48 bars) | — | 4,500 |

### Crystallized Charge Sources

| Method | Efficiency | Notes |
|--------|:----------:|-------|
| Depths mining | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Zonaite deposits everywhere underground |
| Depths bosses | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Each drops 20 Crystallized Charges |
| Forge Constructs | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Trade Zonaite → Charges (3:1) |
| Yiga camps | ⭐⭐⭐ | 20 Charges per camp chest |
| Surface caves | ⭐⭐⭐ | Small deposits |

### Recommended Upgrade Path

```
Early (1-4 cells) → Depths mining + Forge Constructs
  │
Mid (4-8 cells) → Depths boss hunting (20 each)
  │
Late (8-16 cells) → Full Depths mining sweep
```

> **Tip:** At max 16 cells, the Hover Bike can fly nonstop for over 10 minutes.

## Advanced Techniques

### Thermal Updraft Takeoff

Place 4-5 campfires under a Wing (throw Hylian Pine Cones for updraft). Zero-energy liftoff.

### Electric Motor Drive

**Shock Emitter** + metal weapon/shield = current loop → drives **Propeller**. Almost no battery drain, great for long flights.

### Rocket Shield

Rocket + Shield = Shield Rocket Jump. Fastest vertical mobility, but consumes the rocket.

### Spring Shield

Spring + Shield = Shield Bounce. Reusable, costs nothing.

### Freeze Combo

Frost Emitter freeze → two-handed charged attack → ×3 damage on frozen enemies. One-shots almost all non-boss enemies.

## Summary

Zonai devices are the soul of Tears of the Kingdom. Master these core blueprints and you'll reach every corner of Hyrule with machines of your own design. Remember:

1. **Hover Bike** = peak cost-efficiency. Once you learn it, you never go back
2. **Construct Head + Beam Emitter** = automated mob clearing
3. **Shield Rocket Jump** = best vertical mobility
4. **Upgrade batteries first** = foundation for everything else
