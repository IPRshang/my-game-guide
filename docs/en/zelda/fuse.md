# Tears of the Kingdom Fuse Guide

**Fuse lets you attach almost any object to a weapon, shield or arrow in Tears of the Kingdom. The fused material changes the weapon's damage, durability or behaviour — and because most weapons in the game have low durability, Fuse is the main way to keep your damage competitive.**

## What Fuse does

- **Weapons**: attach a material to raise attack power or add an effect (elemental, explosive, etc.).
- **Shields**: attach objects for utility — such as a makeshift sled or a defensive spike.
- **Arrows**: attach materials to change the arrow's effect — elemental bursts, explosions, or homing behaviour.

## Practical combinations

1. **Strong horn or claw on a weapon** — the most reliable flat damage boost.
2. **Elemental materials** — apply fire, ice or shock to exploit enemy weaknesses.
3. **Explosive barrels** on arrows — efficient crowd clearing when used carefully.
4. **Rocket or fan** on shields — movement and utility solutions in open areas.

## Why durability matters

- Most surface weapons break quickly, so **Fuse effectively multiplies what you get out of each one**.
- Fusing a valuable material to a nearly broken weapon wastes it — check durability first.
- Carrying a few strong materials is better than hoarding piles of weak ones.

## Tips

- **Fuse before tough fights**, not mid-fight — the menu pause leaves you exposed.
- **Peel off bad fusions** where the game allows it, so you do not waste good materials.
- **Elemental fusions double as puzzle tools**, not just combat upgrades.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I fuse anything?**
A: Most objects and monster parts can be fused; a few unique items cannot.

**Q: Does fusing use up the material?**
A: Yes — the attached item is consumed as part of the weapon until it breaks.

**Q: What gives the biggest damage boost?**
A: Generally the strongest monster parts you have access to; horns and claws scale well.
