# Tears of the Kingdom Armor Guide

**Armor in Tears of the Kingdom provides defence, and wearing a complete set usually grants an extra set bonus. Upgrading pieces at a Great Fairy raises their defence and eventually unlocks those bonuses at lower investment, so choosing what to upgrade matters more than hoarding pieces.**

## How armor works

- Each piece adds **defence points**; the total determines how much damage you take.
- **Sets** grant a bonus when all pieces are worn together — often elemental resistance, attack up, or movement perks.
- **Upgrades** require materials and rupees, and are done at Great Fairy Fountains once unlocked.
- Some armor is **situational** — cold resistance, heat resistance, or stealth — rather than strictly combat gear.

## What to upgrade first

1. **A general-purpose set** you will wear most of the time.
2. **Elemental resistance sets** for the regions that require them.
3. **Climbing or movement sets** — quality of life that saves constant consumables.
4. **Fully upgrade one set before spreading out** — defence scaling matters more than variety early on.

## Set bonuses

- Typically unlock when every piece of a set is worn **and upgraded at least once**.
- Effects range from **attack up** and **elemental immunity** to **reduced stamina use**.
- Check what a set does before investing — some bonuses only matter in specific situations.

## Tips

- **Elemental resistance can replace meals** — wearing the right armour frees inventory slots.
- **Do not sell armour** — pieces you outgrow are often needed for completion or specific puzzles.
- **Dye and armour passives stack with food**, but not with identical food effects.

## FAQ

**Q: Where do I upgrade armor?**
A: At Great Fairy Fountains, which you unlock progressively as you help each Great Fairy.

**Q: Do I need every armor set?**
A: No. A few well-upgraded sets cover nearly all situations.

**Q: Does armor affect stealth?**
A: Yes — some sets and pieces reduce the noise you make and how quickly enemies notice you.
