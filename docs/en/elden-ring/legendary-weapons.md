## 🌟 S-Tier Legendary Weapons (Must-Have)

### 1. 【Dark Moon Greatsword】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / INT S
- **Base Stats**: Physical 114 + Magic 78
- **Ash of War**: "Moonlight Greatsword" — Enchants weapon, adds magic damage, heavy attack fires moonlight wave
- **Location**:
  1. Complete Ranni's questline (long side quest)
  2. Obtained at the Moonlight Altar
- **Best For**: Pure mages / Spellblades
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Mage endgame weapon. Moonlight waves have long range and high damage. Strong in both PvE and PvP.

### 2. 【Hand of Malenia】
- **Type**: Katana
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX S
- **Base Stats**: Physical 138
- **Ash of War**: "Waterfowl Dance" — Forward dash with multi-hit flurry, final hit launches
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Malenia, Blade of Miquella"
  2. Trade her Remembrance
- **Best For**: Pure DEX / Bleed builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: DEX build endgame weapon. Waterfowl Dance deals insane damage, fast startup and large range.

### 3. 【Blasphemous Blade】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / FAI D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 121 + Fire 78
- **Ash of War**: "Taker's Flames" — Releases fire shockwave, heals HP on kills
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Praetor Rykard"
  2. Trade his Remembrance
- **Best For**: Faith builds / Fire builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Faith warrior god-tier. Lifesteal massively improves survivability, amazing for clearing groups.

## ⭐ A-Tier Legendary Weapons (Highly Recommended)

### 4. 【Starscourge Greatsword】
- **Type**: Colossal Sword (dual-wield)
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / FAI C
- **Base Stats**: Physical 131 + Magic 69
- **Ash of War**: "Starscourge Greatsword" — Dual swords slam ground, summon gravity shockwave
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Starscourge Radahn"
  2. Trade his Remembrance
- **Best For**: STR/FAI / Gravity builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Dual colossal swords look epic. Starscourge skill has large AoE and high damage, but slow startup.

### 5. 【Sword of Night and Flame】
- **Type**: Straight Sword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / INT D / FAI D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 87 + Magic 57 + Fire 57
- **Ash of War**: "Stance of Night and Flame" — Two modes:
  - Light attack: Comet (magic damage)
  - Heavy attack: Flame spray (fire damage)
- **Location**:
  1. Caria Manor — room accessed by jumping from rooftops
  2. Need to drop down from upper manor
- **Best For**: INT/FAI hybrid / Mages
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Former meta weapon. Still strong after nerfs, top pick for hybrid builds.

### 6. 【Moonlight Greatsword】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / INT S
- **Base Stats**: Physical 110 + Magic 82
- **Ash of War**: "Moonlight" — Enchants weapon, heavy attack fires moonlight projectiles
- **Location**:
  1. Complete Ranni's questline
  2. Different from Dark Moon Greatsword
- **Best For**: Mages / Spellblades
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Classic weapon from the Souls series. Nostalgia + solid performance.

## 🛡️ B-Tier Legendary Weapons (Niche Use)

### 7. 【Golden Order Greatsword】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / FAI D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 120 + Holy 76
- **Ash of War**: "Golden Order" — Enchants weapon with holy damage, restores HP
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Golden Order Guardian"
  2. Mountaintops of the Giants
- **Best For**: Holy faith builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Holy damage weapon, great against undead enemies, but limited general use.

### 8. 【Greatsword】
- **Type**: Colossal Sword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 164
- **Ash of War**: "Stomp" — Stomp to create shockwave, followed by downward slash
- **Location**:
  1. Giant-Conquering Hero's Grave
  2. Found in a chest
- **Best For**: Pure STR / Chad builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Pure STR players' favorite. Insane AR, just slow.

### 9. 【Ruins Greatsword】
- **Type**: Colossal Sword
- **Scaling**: STR C / DEX D / INT D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 124 + Magic 68
- **Ash of War**: "Ruins" — Releases gravity wave
- **Location**:
  1. Redmane Castle after defeating Radahn
  2. Or specific boss drop
- **Best For**: STR/INT hybrid
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Cool looking, but damage is mediocre.

## 📈 Tier Rankings

### PvE Tier List
1. 🥇 **Dark Moon Greatsword** — Mage BIS, moonlight wave clears everything
2. 🥈 **Blasphemous Blade** — Lifesteal + AoE, survival and damage
3. 🥉 **Hand of Malenia** — DEX build ceiling
4. **Starscourge Greatsword** — Dual colossal, absolutely epic
5. **Sword of Night and Flame** — Hybrid god-tier, ranged and melee
6. **Moonlight Greatsword** — Nostalgia + power
7. **Greatsword** — Pure STR chad choice
8. **Golden Order Greatsword** — Holy specialized
9. **Ruins Greatsword** — Middling performance

### PvP Tier List
1. 🥇 **Hand of Malenia** — Waterfowl Dance is nearly unreactable
2. 🥈 **Dark Moon Greatsword** — Moonlight wave pressure
3. 🥉 **Sword of Night and Flame** — Ranged combos are strong
4. **Starscourge Greatsword** — Gravity pressure
5. **Blasphemous Blade** — Sustained healing is annoying

## 🗺️ Collection Route

### Early Game
1. **Sword of Night and Flame** — Caria Manor (Liurnia of the Lakes)
2. **Greatsword** — Giant-Conquering Hero's Grave (needs key)

### Mid Game
3. **Starscourge Greatsword** — Defeat Radahn (Redmane Castle festival)
4. **Ruins Greatsword** — Redmane Castle (after Radahn)

### Late Game
5. **Blasphemous Blade** — Defeat Rykard (Volcano Manor)
6. **Golden Order Greatsword** — Mountaintops of the Giants
7. **Dark Moon / Moonlight Greatsword** — Ranni's questline (very long)
8. **Hand of Malenia** — Defeat Malenia (Haligtree)
