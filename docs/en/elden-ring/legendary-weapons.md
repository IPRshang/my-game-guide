# ⚔️ Elden Ring — Legendary Weapons Complete Guide

> All 9 base game legendary armaments + DLC | Locations, Scaling, Ash of War & Tier List

---

Most "legendary weapon" lists rank by raw AR and stop there. We rank by *opportunity cost* instead: how early you can realistically get the weapon, what build it actually enables, and how many of your limited Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones it deserves. A weapon that needs Ranni's 11-step quest to unlock is not "S-tier" for a first-timer the same way Blasphemous Blade is.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** players blow their single Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone on a weapon they think is cool, then realize their actual build wants a different one and have no stone left. You get roughly one per playthrough for the +10 versions — choose the weapon your build is built around, not the prettiest moveset.

## 🌟 S-Tier Legendary Weapons (Must-Have)

### 1. 【Dark Moon Greatsword】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / INT S
- **Base Stats**: Physical 114 + Magic 78
- **Ash of War**: "Moonlight Greatsword" — Enchants weapon, adds magic damage, heavy attack fires moonlight wave
- **Location**:
  1. Complete Ranni's questline (long side quest)
  2. Obtained at the Moonlight Altar
- **Best For**: Pure mages / Spellblades
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Mage endgame weapon. Moonlight waves have long range and high damage. Strong in both PvE and PvP.

### 2. 【Hand of Malenia】
- **Type**: Katana
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX S
- **Base Stats**: Physical 138
- **Ash of War**: "Waterfowl Dance" — Forward dash with multi-hit flurry, final hit launches
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Malenia, Blade of Miquella"
  2. Trade her Remembrance
- **Best For**: Pure DEX / Bleed builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: DEX build endgame weapon. Waterfowl Dance deals insane damage, fast startup and large range.

### 3. 【Blasphemous Blade】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / FAI D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 121 + Fire 78
- **Ash of War**: "Taker's Flames" — Releases fire shockwave, heals HP on kills
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Praetor Rykard"
  2. Trade his Remembrance
- **Best For**: Faith builds / Fire builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Faith warrior god-tier. Lifesteal massively improves survivability, amazing for clearing groups.

**Our call:** Blasphemous Blade is the safest first +10 in the game — Rykard is a manageable early-ish boss and the lifesteal singles it out for any Faith or hybrid run. Pair it with a defensive [Talismans](/en/elden-ring/talismans/) setup and it carries you through the entire mid-game.

## ⭐ A-Tier Legendary Weapons (Highly Recommended)

### 4. 【Starscourge Greatsword】
- **Type**: Colossal Sword (dual-wield)
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / FAI C
- **Base Stats**: Physical 131 + Magic 69
- **Ash of War**: "Starscourge Greatsword" — Dual swords slam ground, summon gravity shockwave
- **Location**:
  1. Defeat "Starscourge Radahn"
  2. Trade his Remembrance
- **Best For**: STR/FAI / Gravity builds
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Dual colossal swords look epic. Starscourge skill has large AoE and high damage, but slow startup.

### 5. 【Sword of Night and Flame】
- **Type**: Straight Sword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / INT D / FAI D
- **Base Stats**: Physical 87 + Magic 57 + Fire 57
- **Ash of War**: "Stance of Night and Flame" — Two modes:
  - Light attack: Comet (magic damage)
  - Heavy attack: Flame spray (fire damage)
- **Location**:
  1. Caria Manor — room accessed by jumping from rooftops
  2. Need to drop down from upper manor
- **Best For**: INT/FAI hybrid / Mages
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Former meta weapon. Still strong after nerfs, top pick for hybrid builds.

### 6. 【Moonlight Greatsword】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / INT S
- **Base Stats**: Physical 110 + Magic 82
- **Ash of War**: "Moonlight" — Enchants weapon, heavy attack fires moonlight projectiles
- **Location**:
  1. Complete Ranni's questline
  2. Different from Dark Moon Greatsword
- **Best For**: Mages / Spellblades
- **Tier**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Verdict**: Classic weapon from the Souls series. Nostalgia + solid performance.

**Our call:** Sword of Night and Flame is the best "early access" legendary — Caria Manor is reachable in Liurnia with no remembrance boss required, so hybrid builds get a top-tier weapon 30 levels before Rykard. It's the one we tell most players to grab first.

  1. Redmane Castle after defeating Radahn

## 🏰 Notable Shadow of the Erdtree Weapons (DLC)

The DLC doesn't add achievement-listed "Legendary Armaments", but these greatswords are among the strongest in the game and are must-haves for endgame builds:

### 【Fire Knight's Greatsword】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / DEX D / FAI C
- **Ash of War**: "Flames of the Redmanes" — fire thrust that staggers
- **Location**: Drops from Fire Knights in the Shadow Keep (Specimen Storehouse)

### 【Anvil Hammer】
- **Type**: Great Hammer
- **Scaling**: STR C / FAI D
- **Ash of War**: "Anvil Hammer" — leap and slam creating a gravity shockwave
- **Location**: Drops from Commander Gaius (Scadu Altus)

### 【Putrescence Cleaver】
- **Type**: Greatsword
- **Scaling**: STR D / ARC D
- **Ash of War**: "Putrescent Cleaver" — heavy rot-inflicting slash
- **Location**: Drops from the Putrescent Knight (Stone Coffin Fissure)

### 【Devonia's Hammer】
- **Type**: Great Hammer
- **Scaling**: STR C / DEX D
- **Ash of War**: "Devonia's Hammer" — spinning holy slam
- **Location**: Drops from the ancient dragon forge in the Rauh Ancient Ruins

**Our call:** if you own the DLC, Anvil Hammer and Fire Knight's Greatsword out-class most base-game legendaries for Faith builds — they just don't count toward the achievement, so treat them as bonus slots, not substitutes for the 9 you still need to collect.

## Common Legendary-Weapon Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Wasting the Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone.** You get roughly one per playthrough for a +10. Fix: commit it to the weapon your build is built around, not the flashiest moveset.
2. **Assuming DLC weapons count for the achievement.** Fire Knight's Greatsword and Anvil Hammer are not "Legendary Armaments" — you still need all 9 base-game ones. Fix: collect the 9 first, treat DLC pieces as bonus.
3. **Skipping Sword of Night and Flame because it was "nerfed."** It's still a top hybrid pick and reachable in Liurnia with no remembrance boss. Fix: grab it early; it punches far above its access cost.
4. **Forgetting the ash-of-war lock.** Legendary weapons can't swap skills, so a weapon whose unique skill doesn't fit your playstyle is a dead slot. Fix: read the Ash of War line before committing the stone.
5. **Rushing Ranni's quest for the Moonlight swords too early.** It's an 11-step chain; if you're not an INT build, the stone is better spent on Blasphemous Blade. Fix: only pursue Dark Moon/Moonlight if you're actually a mage.

## FAQ

**Q: How many legendary weapons are in Elden Ring?** A: The base game has 9 Legendary Armaments. Shadow of the Erdtree adds several top-tier greatswords (Fire Knight's Greatsword, Anvil Hammer), but they aren't part of the "Legendary Armaments" achievement.

**Q: What is the best legendary weapon in Elden Ring?** A: It depends on your build — Dark Moon Greatsword for pure mages, Blasphemous Blade for Faith builds, Hand of Malenia for Dex/Bleed. All three are S-tier.

**Q: Can you upgrade legendary weapons?** A: Yes, to +10 using Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones (very limited — one per weapon). You cannot change their built-in Ash of War.

**Q: Do you need all 9 for the achievement?** A: Yes. Collecting all 9 Legendary Armaments unlocks the "Legendary Armaments" trophy/achievement.

**Q: Are there legendary weapons in Shadow of the Erdtree?** A: The DLC doesn't add achievement-listed legendaries, but weapons like Fire Knight's Greatsword and Anvil Hammer are among the strongest in the entire game.
