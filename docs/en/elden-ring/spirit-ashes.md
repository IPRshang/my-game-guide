# Elden Ring Spirit Ashes Guide

**Spirit ashes let you summon AI allies in Elden Ring. You unlock the ability early by receiving the Spirit Calling Bell, then summon by spending FP near a rebirth monument (the small glowing marker that appears on screen). Summons are limited to certain areas — mainly boss fights and open-field encounters.**

## Getting started

1. Obtain the **Spirit Calling Bell** early — without it you cannot summon at all.
2. Equip an ash in your item slots.
3. Enter a boss arena or open encounter and watch for the **summon marker** on screen.
4. Spend FP to call the ash; it stays until it is killed or the fight ends.

## What ashes actually do for you

- **Draw aggro** — the biggest benefit. A summon that takes hits gives you free windows to heal or attack.
- **Add damage** — some ashes output meaningful damage over a long fight.
- **Apply status** — certain ashes build up bleed, frost or poison reliably.
- **Cost FP** — summoning is not free, so low-mind builds may struggle to use powerful ashes.

## Choosing an ash

- **Tanky ashes** suit fragile builds: they hold the boss while you attack from safety.
- **Ranged or status ashes** suit melee builds that need chip damage.
- **Upgrading matters more than rarity** — an upgraded early ash usually beats an unupgraded late one.

## Tips

- Summon **as soon as the marker appears**, before the boss closes distance.
- Do not rely on a summon alone — it rarely survives a full boss fight unaided.
- Ashes cannot be used in every area; if no marker appears, summoning is not available there.

## FAQ

**Q: Why can't I summon?**
A: Either you lack the Spirit Calling Bell, have no FP, or the area does not allow summons.

**Q: Do spirit ashes make the game too easy?**
A: They make it fairer, not trivial — bosses still punish mistimed dodges.

**Q: Can I upgrade ashes?**
A: Yes. Upgrading significantly improves their health and damage.
