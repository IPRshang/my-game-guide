## Early Stat Priority

**First 30 levels — recommended allocation:**

1. **Vigor** → 20 (staying alive > everything)
2. **Endurance** → 15 (wear armor + one extra roll)
3. **Main damage stat** → 20 (pick ONE: STR/DEX/INT and commit)
4. **Vigor** → 30
5. **Mind** → 15 (enough for a few Ash of War uses)

> ⚠️ **Cardinal sin**: Spreading stats evenly. 20 STR + 20 DEX is worse than 40 STR.

## Must-Grab Early Items

### Lone Wolf Ashes (Free)
- **Location**: Church of Elleh — meet Renna at night
- **Verdict**: Best early-game spirit ash. Three wolves tank aggro.

### Meteorite Staff + Rock Sling (Sorcerers)
- **Location**: Street of Sages Ruins, Caelid
- **Verdict**: Best early staff + spell combo in the game

### Bloodhound's Fang (Melee)
- **Location**: Forlorn Hound Evergaol, Limgrave
- **Verdict**: STR/DEX powerhouse. Weapon art has generous i-frames.

### Gold Scarab (Essential)
- **Location**: Abandoned Cave, Caelid
- **Effect**: +20% runes from all enemies

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Kill everyone on sight → many NPCs are gone forever
- ❌ Equip load over 70% → fat-rolling hell
- ❌ Sell every weapon → some weapons are unique
- ❌ Ignore Ashes of War → skill damage dwarfs basic attacks
