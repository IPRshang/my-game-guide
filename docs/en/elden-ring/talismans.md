# Elden Ring Talismans Guide

**Talismans in Elden Ring are passive buffs you equip in dedicated slots. You start with a small number of slots and unlock more by defeating key bosses, and the right talisman can matter more than a weapon upgrade for survivability.**

## Unlocking talisman slots

- You begin with a **single talisman slot**.
- Defeating a major early boss grants an additional pouch.
- Further pouches come from **story-critical bosses** later in the game.
- By the late game you can equip up to **four talismans** at once.

## Common effect categories

| Category | What it does | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Damage negation | Reduces physical or elemental damage taken | Any build — always useful |
| Stat boost | Raises vigor, endurance, mind or damage stats | Covering a weak stat |
| Stamina / equip load | More stamina or higher carry capacity | Heavy armour or greatshield builds |
| Situational | Better jumps, more runes, stronger ripostes | Farming or specific bosses |

## How to choose a loadout

1. **Slot one: defence.** A damage-negation talisman is almost never wasted.
2. **Slot two: your build's core stat or damage type.** Match it to your main damage source.
3. **Slot three: quality of life.** Stamina recovery, equip load, or FP management.
4. **Slot four: fight-specific.** Swap this before hard bosses — e.g. more negation against their element.

## Tips

- **Swap before bosses**, not during — you cannot change talismans mid-fight.
- **Elemental negation matters** more than it looks; stacking it trivialises some bosses.
- **Do not hoard "nice" talismans** — a modest boost you actually equip beats a perfect one in storage.

## FAQ

**Q: How many talismans can I equip?**
A: Four, once you have unlocked every pouch through story progression.

**Q: Can I respec talismans?**
A: Talismans are not tied to respec — just swap them freely from the menu.

**Q: Which talisman is best?**
A: There is no universal best; a damage-negation talisman plus two build-matched ones is a safe default.
