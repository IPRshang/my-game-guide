# Elden Ring — Strategy Hub

Elden Ring rewards *wandering*, but it punishes *flailing*. The single biggest reason new players quit isn't difficulty — it's the silence: no quest log, no arrow, no hand-holding. Our editorial stance for this hub is simple: treat exploration as the tutorial, treat level-gating as the crutch, and treat every boss as a *pattern to learn, not a wall to break*. If you only read one page here, make it the [Beginner Guide](/en/elden-ring/beginner-guide/) and the [Build Compendium](/en/elden-ring/builds/) — they turn "where do I go" into "I know exactly what to grind."

> **Beginner misconception we see constantly:** players think they're "underleveled" and grind one spot forever. You're almost never underleveled — you're under-*explored*. The Lands Between is layered so you can always loop to an easier region, power up, and return. Go around, don't grind stuck.

## I. Game Background

Elden Ring was built by FromSoftware together with *Game of Thrones* author George R.R. Martin, who co-created the world. The story unfolds in **The Lands Between** — once blessed by the Erdtree, but after the **Elden Ring** shattered, the demigods waged war over its shards (Great Runes), and the world fell into decay.

You play a **Tarnished**, summoned back to the Lands Between to gather Great Runes, mend the Ring, and become **Elden Lord**. The game is famed for **high-freedom open world + hardcore Soulslike combat** with almost no forced guidance — exploration is the point.

> This guide defaults to **no spoilers** of boss names or story direction — only mechanics and progression ideas.

---

## II. Core Mechanics

- **Open-world Soulslike**: no quest arrow; explore freely between Sites of Grace (checkpoints); if stuck, go around.
- **Roll & Parry**: rolling has i-frames (core dodge); small shields / fists can parry some enemies, triggering a critical riposte.
- **Ash of War**: attach weapon skills and affinity changes to weapons, defining your build direction.
- **Spirit Ashes**: spend FP to summon aids (wolves, Mimic Tear, etc.) — the strongest fault-tolerance tool for newcomers.
- **Torrent (ghost horse)**: double-jump, fast repositioning, summonable in boss fights — a positioning lifesaver.
- **Sites of Grace & leveling**: graces double as save points, leveling, and respec (needs Larval Tear); free stat allocation defines your Build.
- **Co-op / PvP**: ground summon signs let you call other players (co-op) or be invaded by red phantoms (PvP); use "invader" items to invade actively.
- **Shadow of the Erdtree (DLC)**: a separate map "Land of Shadow," harder, with new weapons / sorceries / remembrance bosses.

## III. Beginner Tips

1. **Start as "Vagabond" or "Samurai"**: high HP / good weapons, strong survival, less early death (see comparison below).
2. **Prioritize Vigor**: HP is always the #1 stat in Soulslikes — aim 30–40 before level 40.
3. **Go around when stuck**: can't beat Limgrave? Go level in Liurnia, Caelid, or the Siofra underground — level-gating is the most reliable crutch.
4. **Get Spirit Ashes early**: "Lone Wolf Ashes" from Stormhill Tombs; summoning wolves in boss fights absorbs huge damage.
5. **Use Torrent often**: double-jump clears terrain and attacks — core for traversal and dodging.
6. **Summoning help is fine**: golden summon signs at graces call other players; use them boldly when stuck on a boss.
7. **Don't respec recklessly**: Larval Tears are rare — commit to a build before dumping points.

### Our Take on the Early Game

We'd push back gently on the "grind Vigor and nothing else" advice floating around forums. Vigor is king, yes — but Endurance (for equip load and stamina) and the *mind* to use Spirit Ashes/Torrent matter just as much for a first-timer's sanity. Our recommended first 20 levels: get Vigor to ~25, grab a couple Endurance points so you can medium-roll in starting armor, then follow your chosen [build's stat spread](/en/elden-ring/builds/). The goal isn't a perfect character at level 20 — it's one that stops dying to the first knight you meet. When you start hunting for the right weapon to commit to, cross-reference the [Legendary Weapons](/en/elden-ring/legendary-weapons/) list so you don't sink upgrades into a dead end.

## IV. Advanced Tips & Strategy

- **Build directions**: Bleed (Rivers of Blood + Poison Mist), pure Sorcery (Meteorite Staff + Rock Sling), Faith warrior (Flame / Erdtree), Strength bonk (Great Club) — see [Builds](./builds).
- **Parry timing**: humanoid / small enemies have wide parry windows; large bosses favor roll + Spirit Ashes; practice parry to speed runs drastically.
- **Skip routes**: use level gaps (underground / snowfield farming), Mimic Tear can solo most bosses.
- **Affinity choice**: "Cold" / "Blood" infusions are most versatile, paired with corresponding stats.
- **DLC prep**: before entering Land of Shadow, aim level 120+, upgrade +20 (normal) / +9 (special) or it's brutally hard.
- **Switch 2 new content**: Tarnished Edition (Aug 28, 2026) adds 2 new classes (Knight of Ides, Heavy Knight), new armor, and Torrent customization; veterans can buy the "Tarnished Pack" standalone (~$4.99).

## FAQ

**Q: The game is too hard — am I just not suited for it?**
A: The Soulslike threshold is "learning enemy moves," not raw mechanics. Vagabond + Spirit Ashes + summons let ordinary players finish. Stuck? Level up and go around.

**Q: How to enter the DLC? Need to buy it?**
A: **Shadow of the Erdtree** is paid DLC (June 21, 2024) — must purchase; enter from a specific grace after defeating a certain main-story boss.

**Q: Can Switch 2 play it? Any difference?**
A: Yes. **Tarnished Edition** (Aug 28, 2026) bundles base game + DLC + new classes / armor / Torrent cosmetics. Other-platform veterans can buy the Tarnished Pack standalone for the new content.

**Q: Does online cost money?**
A: PS / Xbox need membership (PS Plus / Game Pass Core); PC co-op is free.

**Q: How long for 100% achievements?**
A: ~150–200h, including multiple endings and hidden bosses.

**Q: Main story first or explore first?**
A: Explore Limgrave's outskirts to level up, get the horse and Spirit Ashes, then tackle the first major boss (Margit).
