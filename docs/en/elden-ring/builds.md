# Elden Ring — Ultimate Build Guide

A "build" in Elden Ring is less a fixed template and more a *conversation between your stats, your weapon art, and the boss in front of you*. Most tier lists rank builds in a vacuum; this guide ranks them by **what they actually demand of you** — Vigor to survive, the right stat breakpoint to hit, and a win condition that works against remembrance bosses, not just mobs. If you came from our [Elden Ring Strategy Hub](/en/elden-ring/README/), treat the spreads below as the "what to aim for" that the hub's class picker only hints at.

> **Common myth we keep seeing:** "Bleed is the only viable endgame build." It isn't. Bleed is *forgiving* and snowballs hard, but every build below has cleared the full game including the DLC. Pick the one whose fantasy you'll still enjoy at hour 120 — not the one a spreadsheet says is 3% stronger.

## 1. Bleed Build (Meta King)

**Our take:** This is the build we point new players to *because it punishes mistakes less*. Bleed procs scale with Arcane, so you're rewarded for the same stat that powers status resistances and item discovery — efficient, not just strong.

**Stats**: Vigor 50 / Arcane 60 / Dexterity 40

**Core Weapons**:
- Rivers of Blood (Corpse Piler weapon art — absurd bleed buildup)
- Dual Scavenger's Curved Swords (jump attack 4 hits, boss melting machine)
- Eleonora's Poleblade

**Talismans**: Lord of Blood's Exultation + Shard of Alexander + Prosthesis-Wearer + Rotten Winged Sword

**Verdict**: Dominant from patch 1.0 to the final update. The one true meta.

## 2. Sorcery Build (Ranged Safety)

**Our take:** The safest *feeling* build, but the least forgiving of bad positioning — if a boss closes distance you have no melee crutch. The infinite-FP Physick combo is a boss delete button, but it's a one-trick that wastes itself on trash. Save Comet Azur for the fight that matters.

**Stats**: Intelligence 80 / Mind 40 / Vigor 40

**Core Gear**:
- Lusat's / Carian Regal Scepter
- Adula's Moonblade + Comet Azur + Night Comet

**Talismans**: Magic Scorpion + Godfrey Icon + Radagon Icon + Graven-Mass +2

**Physick**: Magic-Shrouding Cracked Tear + Cerulean Hidden Tear

**Combo**: Infinite FP Physick → Comet Azur → one-shot the boss

## 3. Colossal Sword (Pure Strength)

**Our take:** The highest skill floor and the most satisfying ceiling. Two-handing turns 66 Strength into an effective 99, but you'll feel squishy until Vigor and Endurance catch up. This is the build that rewards learning poise-breaking and guard counters more than any other. Pair it with the [boss strategies](/en/elden-ring/bosses/) if you're struggling with pacing.

**Stats**: Strength 66 (two-handed = effective 99) / Endurance 40

**Core Weapons**:
- Greatsword (Lion's Claw Ash of War)
- Giant-Crusher (Prayerful Strike Ash of War)

**Talismans**: Great-Jar's Arsenal + Dragoncrest Greatshield + Axe Talisman + Green Turtle

**Playstyle**: Lion's Claw → Critical → Lion's Claw → Critical ...

## 4. Faith Warrior (All-Rounder)

**Our take:** The "no bad matchup" pick. Blasphemous Blade's heal-on-kill turns long dungeon runs into a non-event, and buffs like Golden Vow are team-wide if you ever co-op. Slower to come online than Bleed, but it ages better into the DLC where boss HP balloons.

**Stats**: Faith 60 / Strength 40 / Vigor 40

**Core Weapons**:
- Blasphemous Blade (heal on kill — infinite dungeon sustain)
- Godslayer's Greatsword (black flame damage)

**Incantations**: Golden Vow + Flame Grant Me Strength + Black Flame's Protection + Ancient Dragons' Lightning Strike

**Verdict**: Perfect hybrid — damage, healing, buffs, everything.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Neglecting Vigor for the damage stat.** Every build above lists Vigor 40–50 for a reason — a dead DPS dealer deals zero. Hit 40 Vigor before you pour points into Arcane/Int/Str.
2. **Over-investing past breakpoints.** Once Arcane 60 (Bleed) or Int 80 (Sorcery) is hit, returns flatten hard. Shift the rest into Vigor, Endurance, and Mind.
3. **Treating the Build Comparison stars as law.** Those ratings assume a competent pilot. A Colossal main who learns guard counters will beat a Bleed main who panic-rolls.
4. **Skipping talismans.** The four slots are ~30% of your power. Lord of Blood's Exultation alone doubles your damage during bleed procs — leaving it out is leaving the build half-built.
5. **One-shotting trash with Comet Azur.** You'll burn your Physick on mobs and have nothing for the boss. Save the combo.

## FAQ

**Which build is best for a first playthrough?** Bleed. The Arcane scaling rewards the same stat that boosts status resist and item discovery, so you're efficient on every front, and the proc-based damage forgives imperfect dodging. See our [beginner guide](/en/elden-ring/beginner-guide/) for the leveling route that gets you there.

**Do these builds work in Shadow of the Erdtree?** Yes, with a catch: boss HP and poise are much higher in the Land of Shadow, so status-reliant builds (Bleed) need more Arcane and better talismans, while Faith Warrior's sustain shines. Aim level 120+ and +20/+9 upgrades before entering.

**Why does the guide list no armor?** Because armor is build-agnostic here — you wear whatever keeps you at med/light roll while hitting your talisman goals. The four builds differ in *weapon, stats, and talismans*, not in a fixed wardrobe.

**Can I respec into a different build later?** Yes, at any Site of Grace using a Larval Tear (from certain NPCs/bosses). Your starting class only matters for the first few hours, so don't fear committing early.
