## Chapter 1: Early Game (Limgrave → Stormveil → Raya Lucaria)

---

### ① Margit, the Fell Omen

::: tip Quick Info
- **Location:** Stormveil Castle gate
- **Mandatory/Optional:** ✅ Mandatory
- **Recommended Level:** 20 ~ 25
- **Weapon Upgrade:** +2 ~ +3
- **Effective Damage:** Slash / Bleed
- **NPC Summon:** Rogier (gold sign right of fog gate)
- **Spirit Ash:** Lone Wolf Ashes
- **Reward:** Talisman Pouch (+1 slot)
- **Runes:** 12,000
:::

#### Attack Pattern Breakdown

| Attack | Trigger | Counter | Danger |
|--------|---------|---------|:--:|
| **Light Blade 2-Hit** | Mid-range | Roll backward ×2 | ★★ |
| **Light Blade 3-Hit** | Close range | Roll→Roll→**Roll backward** | ★★★ |
| **Holy Daggers** | Long range | Sidestep | ★ |
| **Leap Hammer Slam** | P2 below 50% | **2 sec recovery → DPS window** | ★★★ |
| **Hammer 3-Hit Combo** | P2 mid-range | Left roll→Right roll→Roll forward | ★★★★ |
| **Tail Sweep** | Behind him | Immediate roll | ★★ |
| **Charge Thrust** | Extreme range | Side roll, not back | ★★ |

#### Phase-by-Phase

**Phase 1 (100% ~ 55%) — Foundation**
1. Summon Rogier + Lone Wolves immediately
2. Let Rogier tank, attack from the side
3. Use a bleed weapon (Bloodhound's Fang / Reduvia / Uchigatana+Bloody Slash)
4. The final hit of light blade combos always requires a **backward roll**

**Phase 2 (55% ~ 0%) — Hammer Phase**
- Signature: leaps up, golden hammer slam
- After leap slam, **wait before attacking** — check for follow-up
- Maintain mid-range, bait the 3-hammer combo, punish after the 3rd slam
- Priority: re-summon if NPC/spirits die

> 💡 **Newbie trap:** Don't get greedy! This boss teaches you "turn-based combat" — he attacks, you attack. No more than 2-3 hits per window.

### ② Godrick the Grafted

::: tip Quick Info
- **Location:** Stormveil Castle summit
- **Mandatory/Optional:** ✅ Mandatory
- **Recommended Level:** 30 ~ 35
- **Weapon Upgrade:** +4 ~ +5
- **Effective Damage:** Slash
- **NPC Summon:** Nepheli Loux (gold sign before fog)
- **Spirit Ash:** Lone Wolves / Rotten Stray
- **Reward:** Godrick's Great Rune + Remembrance of the Grafted
- **Runes:** 20,000
:::

#### Attack Pattern Breakdown

| Attack | Counter | Danger |
|--------|---------|:--:|
| **Axe Sweep** | Roll forward through → get behind | ★★ |
| **Storm Combo** | Roll left ×3 continuously | ★★★ |
| **Earthquake** | Jump to avoid | ★★★ |
| **Dragon Fire Sweep** | P2, **duck — flame goes over your head** | ★★★★ |
| **Dragon Grab** | P2, immediate retreat (oneshot potential) | ★★★★★ |

#### Phase-by-Phase

**Phase 1 (100% ~ 50%) — Storm Axe**
1. Summon Nepheli immediately
2. Core idea: **always circle behind, hit the hands**
3. Every axe sweep leaves a 1.5 sec opening for 2 light attacks

**Phase 2 (50% ~ 0%) — Dragon Head Graft**
- **Free damage during the cutscene transition**
- Dragon fire breath → **walk right up to his face and crouch** — flames go overhead
- Dragon grab is the biggest threat → glow from dragon mouth = roll backward ×2
- Circle-behind strategy still works, watch for tail wind

> 💡 **Key insight:** This boss teaches you — getting close is safer than staying far. His wide attacks hit the outer radius, not point-blank.

---

### ③ Red Wolf of Radagon

- **Recommended Level:** 38 ~ 42
- **Weakness:** Very low poise — heavy weapons stagger in 3-4 hits
- **Strategy:** Jump heavy attacks relentlessly. Magic swords → run, don't roll. Sword-in-mouth charge → **side roll** (back roll = caught). Chain stagger with charged heavies after ripostes.

---

### ④ Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

- **Recommended Level:** 40 ~ 45
- **P1:** Hit the 3 glowing golden girls. Ignore unlit ones. After all 3 → Rennala falls → burst DPS for 5-6 sec. Repeat twice.
- **P2:** Rush her immediately (lock-on teleport closes gap). All spells dodgeable by sidestep/forward roll. Summoned spirits (dogs/giant/dragon) → kill dogs first. **Physical damage only** — she has extremely high magic resistance.

> ⚠️ **Mage warning:** Pure sorcery builds suffer here. Bring a physical sidearm (Moonveil) or use Rock Sling (physical damage spell).

## Chapter 2: Mid-Game Watershed

---

### ⑤ Starscourge Radahn ※ Optional but recommended

- **Recommended Level:** 55 ~ 60
- **Key:** Scarlet Rot (Rotten Breath / Antspur Rapier) trivializes him
- **Phase 1 (ranged):** Summon NPCs from gold signs across the battlefield. Gravity arrows → sidestep. Arrow rain → gallop sideways.
- **Phase 2 (melee):** Dual sword sweeps → forward roll. Gravity pull → move away 5m.
- **Phase 3 (50% to 0, meteor):** **MOUNT TORRENT AND GALLOP AT FULL SPEED** when he vanishes. After meteor lands → 4-5 sec punish window. Apply Scarlet Rot via breath.

## Chapter 3-5: Late Game — Leyndell → Farum Azula → Ashen Capital

### ⑥ Draconic Tree Sentinel (Lv 60-65)
Horseback circling is safest. Counter-clockwise, hit once after each slam/fire breath. Lightning strike → gallop sideways. Fireballs from horse → sprint diagonally.

### ⑩ Godskin Duo ★ Major Roadblock

::: tip Quick Info
- **Mandatory/Optional:** ✅ Mandatory
- **Recommended Level:** 95 ~ 100
- **Weapon Upgrade:** +22 ~ +25
- **‼️ ESSENTIAL:** Sleep Pots / St. Trina's Sword
:::

Two bosses share **one health bar**. Strategy:
1. **Sleep pot the fat one** → 20-30 sec nap
2. Fight the skinny one 1v1
3. Reapply sleep if it wakes. Use pillars as cover from fireballs.
4. Summon Mimic Tear / Black Knife Tiche for aggro split.

---

### ⑭ Radagon + Elden Beast (Final Boss)

**Radagon:**
- **Weak to Fire** (Blasphemous Blade / Flame Grant Me Strength)
- Every hammer slam has a delayed holy AoE → roll twice
- Grab → right hand glows → roll backward immediately

**Elden Beast:**
- Weak to Physical (Fallingstar Beast Jaw / Black Blade)
- Meteor rain → run sideways
- Gold rings → run out or roll through
- Cosmic backdrop mega-attack → keep running, dodge light pillars
- **Wear highest holy defense gear** (Haligtree Knight set / Bull-Goat set + Haligdrake Talisman+2)

## Hidden Boss: Malenia, Blade of Miquella ★ ★ ★ ★ ★ ★

> Hardest boss in the game. Over **300 million** Tarnished killed (official stat).

::: danger Fatal Mechanic
**Lifesteal:** Every hit Malenia lands on you — even blocked hits — heals her. Your spirit summons getting hit **also heals her**. Tank spirits are counterproductive here.
:::

| Attack | Counter | Danger |
|--------|---------|:--:|
| **Normal Slashes** | Roll backward ×3 or block | ★★ |
| **Thrust** | Side roll — extremely sudden | ★★★★ |
| **Aerial Downslash** | Side roll ×2, then punish window | ★★★ |
| **Waterfowl Dance** | **Run away(1)→Roll forward(2)→Roll back(3)** | ★★★★★★ |
| **Phantom Spirits** | P2 only, keep running + final roll | ★★★★★ |
| **Scarlet Aeonia** | P2 leap slam → run out of AoE | ★★★★ |

**Waterfowl Dance detailed dodge:**
1. She jumps → sprint away immediately
2. 1st flurry → roll outward (keep distance)
3. 2nd flurry → **roll FORWARD through her**
4. 3rd flurry → **roll BACKWARD** to escape final petals

**Recommended Builds:**
- **Bleed god:** Dual Scavenger's Curved Swords + Seppuku + White Mask + Lord of Blood's Exultation
- **Freeze pot:** Throw Freezing Pot during Waterfowl startup to proc Frostbite mid-air
- **Mimic Tear:** Most reliable spirit, but your loadout syncs to the mimic
- **Do NOT use Black Knife Tiche** — she constantly feeds Malenia lifesteal

## 📋 Pre-Final-Boss Checklist

Before challenging the final boss gauntlet:

- [ ] Weapon upgraded to **+25** (standard) or **+10** (somber)
- [ ] Flask count: **14 charges (+12)**
- [ ] Flask upgrade level: **+12**
- [ ] Character level at least **110+** (120-130 recommended for final duo)
- [ ] Torrent whistle upgraded (at least 2 seeds activated)
- [ ] Spirit Ash upgraded to **+10**
- [ ] At least **3 Great Runes** collected
- [ ] At least **3 talisman slots** (4 recommended)
- [ ] Holy defense talisman equipped (Haligdrake Talisman+2, found in Haligtree)
- [ ] Malenia attempted (optional but recommended)
