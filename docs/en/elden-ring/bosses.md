# Elden Ring — Boss Strategy Guide

> From the tutorial boss to the final showdown — every fight broken down

## Early Game

### Margit, the Fell Omen
- **Location**: Stormveil Castle gate
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆ (for newcomers)
- **Recommended Level**: 20-25
- **Weakness**: Bleed, parry
- **Strategy**:
  - Summon Rogier + Lone Wolf Ashes — 3v1
  - Light sword triple: roll → roll → roll away
  - 2-second recovery after jumping slam — damage window
  - P2 at 50% HP: adds hammer combos

### Godrick the Grafted
- **Location**: Stormveil Castle summit
- **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆
- **Recommended Level**: 30-35
- **Strategy**: Summon Nepheli. Circle behind, hit the arms. P2 dragon fire: duck to avoid.

## Mid Game

### Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon
- **Location**: Academy of Raya Lucaria
- **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆
- **P1**: Attack glowing students (physical immune — use spells or break shields)
- **P2**: Rennala fights directly. Magic-heavy — keep distance or use spell barrier.

### Starscourge Radahn
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★
- **Location**: Redmane Castle
- **Epic raid boss** — summon up to 6 NPCs
- **Critical**: P2 meteor dive — MOUNT TORRENT AND RUN!

### Mohg, Lord of Blood
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆
- **Location**: Mohgwyn Palace
- **Must-have**: Purifying Crystal Tear (negates three Blood Curses)
- **Strategy**: When cursed, don't panic — just heal through it.

## Late Game

### Maliketh, the Black Blade
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★
- P1: Beast Clergyman. P2: pulls out destined death — insanely aggressive
- **Tip**: Blasphemous Claw can parry specific Maliketh attacks.

### Radagon of the Golden Order + Elden Beast
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★
- **Back-to-back fight!** Radagon weak to fire. Elden Beast weak to physical.
- Stack holy defense as high as possible.

## Hidden Boss

### Malenia, Blade of Miquella
- **Difficulty**: ★★★★★★ (Hardest in the game)
- **Location**: Haligtree Roots
- **Lifesteal**: Every hit on you heals her. Your summons hitting her ALSO heal her.
- **Waterfowl Dance**: Deadliest combo in gaming. Three-phase dodge: run away → roll forward → roll backward.
- **Recommended**: Bleed build + Mimic Tear + Freezing Pot to cancel Waterfowl

> 💀 Malenia has killed players over 300 million times.
