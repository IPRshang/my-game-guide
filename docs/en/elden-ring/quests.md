# Elden Ring: Complete NPC Questlines Guide

Elden Ring's NPC questlines are notoriously easy to miss — killing a boss out of order, exploring the wrong direction, or even skipping a single line of dialogue can permanently lock you out. This guide covers the 7 most important questlines with all fail conditions clearly marked.

Most quest guides list steps in a vacuum. We organize differently: the danger in Elden Ring isn't *remembering* a step, it's *timing* it against boss progression. Half these quests share a bottleneck — Radahn's festival, Maliketh's gate, the burning of the Erdtree — and missing that window is what actually locks people out, not a forgotten dialogue line.

> **Beginner trap we see constantly:** players rush the main path and "accidentally" burn the Erdtree or kill Maliketh before finishing Alexander or Fia. Once Leyndell turns to ash or Farum Azula locks, those NPCs are gone for the run. The fix is boring but real: after every major boss, walk back and exhaust every NPC's dialogue before moving on.

## 1. Ranni's Questline

> The game's most important side quest. Affects the ending and rewards the best INT weapon.

### Full Walkthrough

**Step 1: First Meeting**
- Location: Church of Elleh, Limgrave
- Timing: Nighttime, after obtaining Torrent
- She gives you the Spirit Calling Bell + Lone Wolf Ashes

> ⚠️ **Fail point**: If you reach Roundtable Hold before meeting her at the church, she disappears from Elleh. Doesn't block the quest but you miss the bell (buyable at Roundtable).

**Step 2: Ranni's Rise**
- Location: Northwest Liurnia, behind Caria Manor
- Defeat Royal Knight Loretta, enter Three Sisters area
- Ranni's Rise is the northernmost tower. Speak at the top, choose "serve Ranni"

**Step 3: Talk to her three servants**
- **Iji** (downstairs hall)
- **Blaidd** (standing wolf-man)
- **Seluvis** (kneeling sorcerer)

**Step 4: Blaidd's lead**
- Go to Siofra River (eastern Limgrave, descend via Mistwood lift)
- Find Blaidd investigating Nokron. He sends you to Seluvis

**Step 5: Seluvis's Introduction**
- Get introduction letter from Seluvis
- Take it to **Sellen** (Waypoint Ruins cellar, Limgrave)
- She tells you to defeat Radahn

**Step 6: Starscourge Radahn**
- Redmane Castle, Caelid. Join the festival
- After defeating Radahn, a meteor crashes east of Limgrave
- It opens the entrance to Nokron, Eternal City

**Step 7: Fingerslayer Blade**
- Descend into Nokron, reach Night's Sacred Ground
- Get the **Fingerslayer Blade** from the chest
- Return to Ranni's Rise, give her the blade. She leaves, gives you the **Carian Inverted Statue**

> ⚠️ **Fail point**: Killing Seluvis before giving the blade won't break Ranni's quest. But Seluvis has his own questline with unique rewards.

**Step 8: Renna's Rise**
- Use the Inverted Statue in Carian Study Hall, obtain the **Cursemark of Death**
- Go to **Renna's Rise** (middle tower in Three Sisters)
- Portal at the top takes you to Ainsel River

**Step 9: Miniature Ranni**
- Pick up the **Miniature Ranni Doll** in Ainsel River
- Talk to it repeatedly at a grace until it speaks
- Proceed deeper, defeat the **Baleful Shadow**

> ⚠️ **Fail point**: If you pick up the doll but never talk to it, you can't progress.

**Step 10: Astel**
- Cross the Lake of Rot (bring Preserving Boluses!)
- Defeat **Astel, Naturalborn of the Void**
- Ride the lift to Moonlight Altar

**Step 11: Ending**
- In the Cathedral of Manus Celes underground cave, find Ranni
- Put the **Dark Moon Ring** on her (from Rennala's chest)
- Receive the **Dark Moon Greatsword**
- At the endgame, choose the blue summon sign for the Age of Stars ending

**Our call:** Ranni's line is the one quest worth planning your whole playthrough around — it's the only side quest that changes your ending *and* hands you a top-tier INT weapon. The Spirit Calling Bell she gives at Step 1 is also your first real [Spirit Ashes](/en/elden-ring/spirit-ashes/) unlock, and the Dark Moon Greatsword she awards is a centerpiece of our [Legendary Weapons guide](/en/elden-ring/legendary-weapons/). Do not skip Church of Elleh at night.

## 2. Fia's Questline

> The Deathbed Companion's questline. Affects the ending.

**Step 1: Embrace at Roundtable**
- Find Fia in the bedroom at Roundtable Hold
- Accept her embrace, receive **Baldachin's Blessing**
- Use the blessing, then embrace again

**Step 2: The Weathered Dagger**
- After obtaining two Great Runes, Fia gives you the **Weathered Dagger**
- Give it to **D, Hunter of the Dead** at Roundtable
- Teleport away and back — Fia and D are both gone

**Step 3: Deeproot Depths**
- Reach Deeproot Depths (via the coffin at the end of Siofra Aqueduct)
- Pass through the Nameless Eternal City to the Prince of Death's Throne
- Defeat Fia's Champions, then talk to her

**Step 4: Cursemark of Death**
- Fia asks for the other half of the Cursemark
- Give her the Cursemark from Ranni's questline

> ⚠️ **Fail point**: If you've given D's armor to his brother before this, Fia will be killed.

**Step 5: Lichdragon**
- Enter Fia's deathbed dream
- Defeat **Lichdragon Fortissax**
- Receive the **Mending Rune of the Death-Prince**

**Step 6: Ending**
- After the battle, Fia passes. Loot **Fia's Set** from her
- Use the Mending Rune at endgame for the Duskborn ending

**Our call:** Fia's quest is the easiest to soft-lock because it collides with D's brother and with the Erdtree-burning timer. The golden rule: finish Fia's Steps 1–4 *before* you ever light the Erdtree, and never hand D's armor to his brother until she has the Cursemark.

## 3. Alexander the Iron Fist

> Everyone's favorite jar warrior. Rewards the best talisman.

| # | Location | Action |
|:-:|----------|--------|
| ① | Limgrave, cliff north of Saintsbridge | Talk, heavy attack to free him |
| ② | Caelid, Gael Tunnel | Stuck in a door — go around |
| ③ | Redmane Castle | Summon for Radahn festival |
| ④ | Near Carian Study Hall, Liurnia | Stuck again — need **Oil Pot** |
| ⑤ | Near Magma Wyrm, Mountaintops | Bathing in lava, just talk |
| ⑥ | Farum Azula | **Final duel**. Defeat for **Shard of Alexander** |

> ⚠️ **Fail point**: Only fail risk is missing him in Farum Azula before Maliketh.

**Our call:** Alexander's only real fail point is the Farum Azula window — and it closes the instant you beat Maliketh. His reward, the Shard of Alexander, is one of the highest-value offensive [Talismans](/en/elden-ring/talismans/) in the game for charged-heavy builds, so he's worth a detour every single run.

## 4. Millicent's Questline

> Longest questline. Your final choice determines the reward.

**Step 1: Caelid**
- Talk to Gowry at **Gowry's Shack**, Sellia
- He asks for the **Unalloyed Gold Needle**
- Defeat **Commander O'Neil** in Aeonia Swamp for the needle

**Step 2: Healing Millicent**
- Give the needle to Gowry for repair
- Bring it to Millicent at the **Church of the Plague**, Sellia

**Step 3: Millicent's Journey**

| # | Location | Action |
|:-:|----------|--------|
| ① | Gowry's Shack | Millicent returns, talk to start journey |
| ② | Erdtree-Gazing Hill, Altus | Talk at grace |
| ③ | Shaded Castle | Find **Valkyrie's Prosthesis** inside, give it to her |
| ④ | Windmill Village | After Godskin Apostle, talk north of village |
| ⑤ | Ancient Snow Valley, Mountaintops | Talk |
| ⑥ | Prayer Room, Haligtree | Talk |

**Step 4: The Choice**

> ⚠️ **Critical choice**: At the end of the drainage channel in Haligtree:

| Choice | Outcome | Reward |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 🟡 **Gold sign** (help Millicent) | Fight alongside her vs. sisters | **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** |
| 🔴 **Red sign** (invade Millicent) | Kill her | **Millicent's Prosthesis** (+5 DEX) |

> Most players recommend the gold sign. Rotten Winged Sword Insignia is incredible with multi-hit weapons like Rivers of Blood.

## 5. Blaidd's Questline

> Deeply tied to Ranni's questline. Most steps completed during it.

**Independent steps:**

| # | Location | Action |
|:-:|----------|--------|
| ① | Mistwood Ruins, Limgrave | Hear howling at night → learn Snap gesture from Merchant Kale at Elleh |
| ② | Mistwood Ruins | Use Snap, Blaidd jumps down, talk |
| ③ | Forlorn Hound Evergael | Blaidd asks you to fight the Bloodhound Knight |

After Ranni's questline completes:
- Return to Ranni's Rise, find Blaidd gone mad outside
- Defeat him for **Royal Greatsword** and **Blaidd's Set**

> ⚠️ **Fail point**: Attacking Blaidd before giving Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade locks the quest.

## 6. Varre's Questline

> Only way to reach Mohgwyn Palace early — best rune farm in the game.

**Step 1: The First Step**
- First NPC outside the tutorial. He calls you maidenless.

**Step 2: Rose Church**
- After defeating Godrick, Varre moves to Rose Church, Liurnia
- He gives you **Festering Bloody Fingers**

**Step 3: Three Invasions**
- Invade other players 3 times (winning not required — leave immediately if you want)

**Step 4: Blood Oath**
- Return to Varre, accept his oath
- Receive **Lord of Blood's Favor**

**Step 5: Stain with Blood**
- Go to the Church of Inhibition, Liurnia, find the dead Finger Maiden
- Use the Favor on her blood
- Alternatively: use the maiden corpse at the Four Belfries

**Step 6: Pureblood Knight's Medal**
- Return to Varre, receive the medal
- Use it to teleport directly to Mohgwyn Palace

**Step 7: Final Invasion**
- Deep in Mohgwyn Palace, Varre invades you
- Defeat him for **Varre's Bouquet** (unique weapon)

**Our call:** Varre's line is the most efficient "power move" in the game — three quick invasions unlock Mohgwyn Palace and its rune farm long before you'd reach it naturally. Do this early (Level 1–40 window) and the rest of your build funds itself.

## 7. Sellen's Questline

> The sorceress's questline involving glintstone sorcery and the Academy.

**Step 1: Waypoint Ruins**
- Limgrave, defeat the Mad Pumpkin Head, find Sellen in the cellar
- She teaches sorceries

**Step 2: Sellen's Primal Glintstone**
- Witchbane Ruins, Weeping Peninsula
- Defeat the Mad Pumpkin Head, obtain **Sellen's Primal Glintstone**

**Step 3: New Body**
- Find a puppet body in **Seluvis's Secret Chamber** (near Three Sisters)
- Place the glintstone in the puppet — Sellen gets a new body

**Step 4: Master Lusat**
- Deep in Sellia Hideaway, Caelid, defeat three Crystalians
- Find **Master Lusat** and talk to him

**Step 5: The Choice**

| Choice | Action | Reward |
|--------|--------|--------|
| 🟡 **Help Sellen** | Gold sign in Raya Lucaria Grand Library, help Sellen defeat Witch-Hunter Jerren | Glintstone Kris, Witch's Set |
| 🔴 **Challenge Sellen** | Red sign, help Jerren defeat Sellen | Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone |

## Common Questline Mistakes (and the Fix)

1. **Burning the Erdtree too early.** This single act ashes Leyndell and removes every capital NPC for the run. Fix: finish Fia, Corhyn, and any Roundtable-linked steps before you ever light the tree.
2. **Beating Maliketh before Farum Azula NPCs.** Alexander and Corhyn vanish once the area locks. Fix: clear Alexander's duel *before* Maliketh.
3. **Skipping Church of Elleh at night.** You miss the Spirit Calling Bell permanently (from Elleh). Fix: meet Ranni there before reaching Roundtable Hold.
4. **Giving D's armor to his brother prematurely.** It triggers Fia's death. Fix: hold the armor until after Fia has the Cursemark.
5. **Picking the red sign at Haligtree.** You lose Millicent and the Rotten Winged Sword Insignia for good. Fix: take the gold sign unless you specifically want the DEX prosthesis.

## FAQ

**Which questline changes the ending?** Two: Ranni's (Age of Stars) and Fia's (Duskborn). The other five are reward-focused and don't alter which ending you can pick.

**Can I do every questline in one playthrough?** Yes — but only if you respect the two hard locks: don't burn the Erdtree before Leyndell NPCs are done, and don't kill Maliketh before Farum Azula NPCs are done.

**What's the best early-game power move?** Varre's three invasions to unlock Mohgwyn Palace. The rune farm there funds the rest of your build far ahead of schedule.

**Do I need to fight Radahn for Ranni's quest?** Yes — defeating Starscourge Radahn at the Redmane festival is what drops the meteor and opens Nokron, which Ranni's Step 6 requires. See our [bosses guide](/en/elden-ring/bosses/) for the fight itself.
