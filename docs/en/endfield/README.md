## Common Beginner Pitfalls

1. **Overbuilding the factory before balancing power.** Energy and population are the real constraints, not raw building count. Fix: open the [Base Planner Tool](./planner.md) first and size your grid to your power budget.
2. **Letting redeem codes expire unused.** Codes are time-limited and the easiest free progression in the game. Fix: redeem on the codes page the moment you start, then check back each patch.
3. **Pulling without a tier plan.** Random pulls waste the scarce early resources. Fix: read the [Operator Tier List](./tier-list.md) and the [Beginner Guide](./beginner.md) before your first banner.
4. **Treating base and combat as separate games.** They feed each other — a weak logistics line caps your combat roster. Fix: advance both in parallel, not sequentially.
