# Endfield Operator Tier List

A practical ranking of which operators are worth your early investment. Endfield blends
**combat operators** with **base/logistics operators**, so a good roster supports both the
factory and the battlefield.

## S-Tier — pull & build first

- **Logistics / Reactor operators** — these directly boost base output, which feeds your
  whole account. Prioritize one strong logistics unit early.
- **AoE damage operators** — clear waves fast in the real-time combat sections.

## A-Tier — solid core

- **Single-target burst** — bosses and elite enemies.
- **Shield / tank operators** — keep your backline alive during long auto-battles.

## B-Tier — situational

- **Crowd-control** — strong in specific stages, less universally needed.
- **Support / buff** — good once your core is established.

## How to use this list

1. Spend your first pulls on an **S-tier logistics operator** if available — base economy
   compounds.
2. Fill combat gaps with A-tier damage and tank.
3. Avoid over-investing in B-tier until your core team is stable.

> Rankings shift with new operators and balance patches. Re-check after each version update.
