# Endfield Currency Guide

The first time you load into Arknights: Endfield it doesn't feel like a game — it feels like someone handed you a finance degree. Red currency, gold currency, stock bills, weapon tickets... at some point you stop playing and start doing accounting. This guide cuts through that mess: what each currency is, what it's for, where it comes from, and what it's most often confused with. At the bottom there's a **pull planner** — type in your Oroberyl and it tells you whether to push or save.

## 1. Gacha currencies

### Oroberyl — the main pull currency
- **Use**: recruit Operators on Headhunting banners. 1 pull = 500, ten-pull = 5,000.
- **Get**: open-world exploration (largest early source), chests, daily missions (~200/day), account-level rewards, events, and Origeometry at 1:75.
- ⚠️ **Don't confuse with Origeometry**: Oroberyl is the "pull coin" (red); Origeometry is the "premium coin" (gold).

### Origeometry — the premium currency
- **Rate**: 1 = 75 Oroberyl.
- **Use**: convert to Oroberyl, restore Sanity (40 each — not recommended routinely), unlock Battle Pass, future skins.
- **Get**: main story, Energy Allevium combat challenges, real money.
- **Strategy**: F2P players shouldn't burn it on pulls unless desperate; best value is the Battle Pass unlock.

### Headhunting Permits
- **Basic HH Permit**: standard banner, never expires.
- **Chartered HH Permit**: limited banner only, **expires when the banner ends**.
- **New Horizons Permit**: beginner ten-pull ticket.

### Arsenal Tickets
- **Get**: from character pulls (4★ → 20, 5★ → 200, 6★ → 2,000).
- **Use**: weapon banner (Arsenal Exchange) / Arsenal shop.
- **Nature**: a free byproduct of pulling characters — your weapon source.

## 2. General & upgrade currencies

### T-Creds
- **Use**: the universal currency for leveling Operators, upgrading weapons, building base facilities — constant demand.
- **Get**: dailies, Quota Exchange, Stock Redistribution shop, Protocol Space, chests.

### Credits
- **Daily cap 300**, spent in the Credit Store (Oroberyl, HH Permits, Arsenal Tickets, T-Creds, materials).
- ⚠️ **Anything above 300 is lost at daily reset — spend it the same day!**
- Credit Store unlocks after Chapter 1, Process 2.

## 3. Regional currencies (not shared!)

### Stock Bills
- **Valley IV Stock Bill / Wuling Stock Bill** — valid only in that region.
- **Use**: regional shops for goods / weapons / blueprints, facility upgrades.
- ⚠️ **Each map's currency is independent** — don't hoard in the wrong region.

## 4. Quotas (from duplicate Operators)

| Quota | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| AIC Quota | limited banner pulls | Quota Exchange materials |
| Bond Quota | duplicate Operators | rewards, incl. HH Permits |
| Endpoint Quota | 6★ overflow only | expensive 6★ weapons |

## 6. Three mistakes players make most

1. **"Red vs gold"** → Oroberyl (red, pull) ≠ Origeometry (gold, premium) ≠ T-Creds (general).
2. **"Save Credits for later"** → 300 daily cap, expires — spend daily.
3. **"Universal bills"** → regional Stock Bills don't transfer between maps.
4. **"Track limited-time currencies"** → Some currencies carry expiration dates (e.g. Chartered HH Permits, the daily-reset Credits cap), and the UI scatters them across screens so stock is hard to read at a glance. Spend any time-limited currency promptly before it lapses — don't wait for a version switch to find it void.

> The core loop: **explore for Oroberyl → pull Operators → duplicates become Arsenal Tickets / Quotas → upgrade to farm faster**. Run that loop and the currency anxiety disappears.

## FAQ

**Q: Which Endfield currencies are easiest to confuse?**
A: The red currencies Oroberyl / Origeometry and the regional Stock Bills look similar; Oroberyl and Origeometry are for pulls, while Stock Bills are region-locked and not shared across regions.

**Q: What's the difference between Oroberyl and Origeometry?**
A: Oroberyl is the main pull currency (farmable); Origeometry is the premium currency used for limited / paid banners.

**Q: Why aren't regional Stock Bills shared?**
A: Each region settles independently; Stock Bills only work in their own region and don't carry over, so mixing them up wastes resources.
