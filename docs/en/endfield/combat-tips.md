# Endfield Combat Speed-Up: Skill-Point Loops & DPS Tips

Many Endfield players' first verdict on combat is "slow and grindy." That isn't imagination — the auto-attack loop requires you to **land specific hits** to build skill points, and skill points gate most burst output, so fights are naturally stretched out. This guide explains why it feels slow and gives speed-up tactics you can use immediately.

## 1. Why the skill-point loop slows combat

Endfield combat isn't mindless standing. Normal attacks auto-loop, but **skill-point accumulation depends on landing specific attacks**, not just the passage of time. That means:

- If your output rhythm misses those "effective hits," skill points climb very slowly.
- Without enough skill points, core skills can't fire, so you keep auto-attacking waiting to bank points.
- By the time you bank points and pop the burst, a fight that should end in seconds drags to dozens of seconds or more.

That is the source of the "grindy" feel: the system hands pacing control to "precise hits," and the auto-loop won't be precise for you.

## 2. Turrets are a speed switch for some stages

The good news: some stages let you **deploy turrets**. Placing them on the enemy path or focus point noticeably speeds clears — steady output that doesn't eat your skill-point loop.

But note the flip side: **stages with turrets are faster, turret-less stages are even longer**. Once you lean on turrets for damage, moving to a stage that forbids them drops you back into the slow "bank points → fire skill" loop, and the contrast is sharp. So don't get comfortable only when turrets exist; have a speed plan for turret-less stages too.

## 3. Speed tip 1: concentrate DPS on a few operators

Instead of evenly raising the whole team, **pour resources into 2 to 3 main DPS and max them**. The logic is direct:

- Skill points are limited; feed them to high-multiplier operators for higher damage per unit time.
- The faster your main DPS drops enemy HP, the sooner the fight ends, compressing the skill-point "wait period."
- Supports keep only essential buffs/break — don't take an output slot, or damage gets diluted.

Simply: make bursts hit harder and denser in the same window, instead of smearing damage across the whole fight.

## 4. Speed tip 2: don't drag fights into dead time

"Dragging" is the costliest habit in Endfield. The skill-point loop encourages continuous auto-attacks, but continuous auto-attacks aren't efficient. The keys:

1. **Pick the focus target before engaging**: delete the biggest threat first; don't let trash mobs burn your skill-point rhythm.
2. **Spend skill points the moment they fill**: don't hoard for a "perfect moment" — banking is slow by default, so fire early and enter the next loop sooner.
3. **Cover the vacuum with turrets**: while your main DPS auto-attacks to bank points, turrets add damage so total output never dips hard.

## 5. Speed tip 3: fix the loop gap with team composition

If you keep stalling on "can't bank skill points," check whether your team has someone built to make effective hits — the kind that reliably trigger skill-point accumulation. Putting that role in the loop cures the problem better than raw stat stacking. For who fits, cross-check the high-frequency damage slots in the [Endfield Tier List](/en/endfield/tier-list).

## 6. FAQ

**Q: Why does my combat always take so long?**
A: Probably slow skill-point banking — the auto loop banks points only on "specific hits"; miss the rhythm and you auto-attack waiting for points, stretching the fight.

**Q: Are turrets mandatory?**
A: Some stages are clearly faster with them, but many stages have no turrets, where you must rely on concentrated DPS and early skill use. Don't depend on turrets alone.

**Q: Should I hoard skill points for the ultimate?**
A: Not recommended. Banking itself is slow; firing early drops HP faster and shortens the whole fight, better than waiting for a "perfect moment."

**Q: Who should a beginner power up for speed?**
A: Concentrate on 2 to 3 main DPS, stack their damage, and cover vacuums with turrets — faster than evenly raising the whole team. For team building see the [Endfield Beginner Guide](/en/endfield/beginner).
