# Endfield Redeem Codes — How to Get & Redeem

Redeem codes are one of the fastest ways to grab starter materials in Arknights: Endfield.
This page explains **where codes appear** and **how to use them**, then tracks active codes
as a community resource.

## How to redeem a code

1. Open **Settings** from the main menu.
2. Go to the **Account / Redemption** tab.
3. Paste or type the code and confirm.
4. Rewards are sent to your in-game **mail** — collect them there.

> Codes are **case-sensitive** and usually **time-limited**. Redeem as soon as you find one.

## Where Endfield codes are distributed

- **Official Discord** — the most common drop point for event and milestone codes.
- **Pre-registration milestones** — reached during the launch campaign.
- **In-game mail** — some codes are delivered directly, no manual entry needed.
- **Collaboration / social campaigns** — watch the official X (Twitter) and YouTube.
