# Endfield Gacha & Pity System

Endfield's gacha gets criticized as punishing — a single pull costs 5,000 currency, hard pity is 80, and the featured guarantee needs 120 pulls that **don't carry over**. This page breaks down every pity tier so you know **when to push and when to save**, instead of wasting pulls at the wrong moment.

## 1. Base cost & rates

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Single pull | 500 Oroberyl |
| Ten-pull | 5,000 Oroberyl |
| Base 6★ rate | 0.8% |
| Base 5★ rate | 8% |
| Base 4★ rate | 91.2% |

> Roughly $20 = ten-pull, ~$300 = 120 pulls (reference; varies by top-up tier).

## 2. Character banner pity (the important part)

### 1. 5★ guarantee every 10 pulls
Any character banner guarantees at least one 5★ every 10 pulls. **Carries over** between banners.

### 2. Soft pity at 65 (rate ramps up)
After 65 pulls without a 6★, soft pity activates — 6★ rate rises **+5% per pull**:

| Pull | 6★ rate |
|---|---|
| 1–64 | 0.8% |
| 65 | 0.8% |
| 66 | 5.8% |
| 70 | 25.8% |
| 75 | 50.8% |
| 80 | 100% (hard pity) |

**Soft pity carries over** between banners.

### 3. Hard pity at 80 (guaranteed 6★)
At 80 pulls you're guaranteed a 6★ — but it's a **50/50**: 50% featured, 50% standard / previous limited.

### 4. Featured guarantee at 120 (guaranteed featured)
At 120 pulls you're guaranteed the **current limited 6★**.

> ⚠️ **The trap: the 120 featured guarantee does NOT carry over!** Pull 119 times on banner A without the featured, and when A ends those pulls **reset to zero**. Always save a full 120 (60,000 Oroberyl) before chasing a limited, or you're gambling.

### 5. The 50/50 system
Every 6★ is an independent 50/50 — there's **no "next 6★ is guaranteed featured"** mechanic. You could pull five 6★ before 120 and none be the featured. Only 120 locks it in.

## 3. Weapon banner (Arsenal Exchange)

- **Currency**: Arsenal Tickets (free from character pulls).
- **Rates**: 4% 6★ weapons, 15% 5★.
- **Pity**: 40 pulls guarantees a 6★ weapon, 80 guarantees the featured weapon.
- Essentially free — accumulate from character pulling, no separate grind.

## 4. Beginner & standard banners

| Banner | Pity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New Horizons (beginner) | 6★ at 40 pulls | ten-pull 4,000 (20% off), disappears after 40, also grants a 6★ weapon select |
| Basic (permanent) | 6★ at 80 / select at 300 | uses Basic Permits, pull slowly with tickets |

**Beginner tip**: clear the New Horizons banner for its guaranteed 6★ first — best Oroberyl value.

## 5. Practical rules (golden law)

1. **Save a full 120 before pulling a limited** — don't bet on 50/50.
2. Push during soft pity (65+) for best value.
3. Weapon banner is free — ride Arsenal Tickets.
4. Standard banner: use Basic Permits slowly toward the 300 select.
