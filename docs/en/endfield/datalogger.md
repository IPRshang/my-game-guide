# Endfield Protocol Datalogger Balloon Men

While exploring Talos-II you've surely seen the **yellow, waving balloon figures** standing on pedestals labeled "connecting". Interact with one early and you only get:

> *"Access authorization cannot be verified. Please acquire access authorization before starting again."*

Many players assume this is an environmental puzzle and spend ages hunting for a hidden item nearby. **It isn't.** This guide explains exactly how to unlock them and what they're for.

## 1. Why they don't work early

Protocol Datalogger access is **tied to main-story progression**, not puzzles or pickups. Seeing the error early is normal — don't waste time hunting for a hidden switch.

## 2. How to unlock (3 steps)

1. **Finish all of Chapter I Process I**
   - Includes saving the **Hub Base**
   - Then head to **Valley Pass** to fight the **Landbreakers**
2. **After returning, talk to Andre**
   - The conversation grants access authorization for the balloon men
3. **All Dataloggers become map-marked** — collect them as you explore

> In short: **clear Chapter I Process I, return to base, speak with Andre — the balloons start working.**

## 3. What you get: AIC Index

Interacting with a Datalogger grants **AIC Index**, spent in the **AIC Plan** to research factory tech:

- Build more production units
- Craft new materials
- Raise overall production capacity

For a game built around forced factory + power grid + trade loops, AIC Index is the fuel for mid-to-late game scaling. **Unlock early = accumulate early = factory snowballs faster.**

## 4. FAQ

- **Need a puzzle?** No — pure story unlock.
- **Missed Andre's dialogue?** It's granted automatically as you progress; watch for the character prompt above his head.
- **Where are they?** Scattered across the map; after authorization they're marked, so grab them on the way.
