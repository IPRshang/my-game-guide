# Endfield Beginner Guide

Just started Arknights: Endfield? Here is the efficient path through your first hours.

## First-hour checklist

1. **Push the main story** until the base (factory) system unlocks.
2. **Redeem codes** from the [codes page](./codes.md) — free materials compound early.
3. **Build one logistics operator** into your base to start passive production.
4. **Do the tutorial combat** to learn real-time tactics and operator switching.

## Resource priorities

- **Power** — the backbone of your base. Never overbuild consumers without enough reactors.
- **Rare materials** — bank them; mid-game crafts get expensive fast.
- **Operator XP** — focus on your core 4–6 operators, not everyone.

## Base setup tips

- Place **reactors first**, then production buildings, so you never hit a power deficit.
- Use the [Base Planner](./planner.md) to preview total power before committing.
- Logistics operators reduce craft time — invest early.

## Common mistakes

- Spreading upgrades across too many operators (thin roster).
- Ignoring power caps and overbuilding factories.
- Skipping codes and mail rewards.

**Q: What should I do first in Endfield?**
A: Push the main story to unlock the base, redeem codes for free materials, build one logistics operator, and complete the tutorial combat.
