## ④ Suiming Time-Loop Puzzle Guide

✅ **Confirmed**: Suiming is a new operator debuting in the 9/24 Wave 2, with a companion **puzzle-exploration map** whose core mechanic is a **15-minute time loop**:

- **Tidal Ebb and Flow**: Within each loop the tide rises and falls; at low tide new streets previously submerged are revealed, and explorable areas unlock progressively as loops advance.
- **Parts Retained**: After each loop ends, **collected parts are retained**, so you need not re-collect them, allowing steady cumulative progress on repairs.
- **Waking Bell**: The puzzle goal is to repair the Waking Bell to fend off the periodic **Blight Tide**.

**Loop gameplay tips**:
1. Upon entering a loop, first mark explored streets to avoid repeats;
2. Prioritize collecting rare parts — accumulate first, then repair in bulk;
3. Watch the tide schedule; the low-tide window is the only time to enter new streets;
4. Once Waking Bell repair progress hits the threshold, you can fend off the Blight Tide and clear the map.

🔮 **Speculated**: Suiming's skill mechanics may be tied to the "time/loop" theme; exact numbers are subject to implementation. For more industrial-building ideas, see the [factory guide](/en/endfield/factory-guide).
